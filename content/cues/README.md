# content/cues: moments' looks and weight

A cue is one moment's look and feel as data: the flashes Feel draws, and its shake, camera punch, holds and sound. One `CueBase` file per cue (`core/base/cue_base.gd` documents every key), named `<id>.tres`; the registry finds it by its `id` under `cues`, so a new moment is a new file and no list is edited.

A part may also be a floor mark (`{"part": &"mark", "mark": &"scorch", "reach": 0.9}`; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): stamped once into the room's mark layer, it stays until the room ends. The mark is a kind in `content/marks/`; with `"cards": true` the cue's cards' marks take its place when they have one (a frost blast leaves frost).

Something plays a cue by name: Feel's `play_cue(id, caster, at, dir, size, tint, look, target)`, or the `cue_shown` event from another area. `size` is in pixels (an explosion's radius, a ground strike's reach); a part with `reach` is that many times it. A telegraph's `size` is seconds instead: a part with `lasts_size` lasts that long.

A part can throw motes instead of drawing a flash (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1b; Feel's single-pixel particles, `content/motes/`): `{"part": &"motes", "kind": &"spell", "count": 60, "reach": 1.0, "spread": 360.0}`. Its `kind` is `spell` (the default: the kinds of the cue's cards, its look's layers, in its caster's ramp and colour, so a fire blast throws embers and a frost one flecks), `surface` (the surface kinds, in the colours of what broke: the room's walls, or the art of a body that died) or one kind's id (a kind no card names, such as `ash`, is thrown only this way); `colours` (`spell` or `surface`) says which colours they cool through in place of their kind's. A `spell` part throws the kinds of the cards of the spell the cue is of (its tag; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S2), or of its look's layers when it has none. `count` is how many in all (split between the kinds), `reach` flies them out to the cue's size times it, and `spread`, `up` (times the kind's own), `ring` and `way` shape the throw (`CueBase` documents each key). Every count is times the "Particle amount" setting; with "Particles" off there are none.

A part may light the room instead, while "Spell light" is on (S6, item 4): `{"part": &"light", "radius": 44.0, "strength": 1.0, "seconds": 0.45, "color": ...}` is a soft light round the cue's point, fading over its seconds (`reach` makes it the cue's size times that; `color`, `plain` and `bare` as a flash's, or the cue's colour or its ramp, tint and cards). A cue with a light part lights by it alone: its flashes do not light the room by their kinds as they otherwise do, so the file says how a heal or a rune lights.

A cue that comes by the hundred (a gem picked up in a horde) says `every`: the least seconds between two plays for the same actor; a play sooner is left out whole (S6, item 13).

A cue may have a colour and a strength of its own (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S6): `color` is the exact colour of every flash and motes part that names none (nor `plain`, nor `bare`) of its own, and of what Feel draws of the moment in code (a reaction's name); `power` is how strong it plays, by one rule in `Feel.play_cue`: times every flash's size, every motes part's count and how far they fly, every mark's radius, its holds, shake and punch (never a part's seconds, nor an arc's length). A motes part may fly out a fixed `radius` of pixels when the cue has no size. A motes part with a `share` sheds instead (a shot's end): each kind its own count times the share, flying as it sheds, as one of many a frame, so it leaves the moments' rows alone and is skipped once only those are left. A flash with `body` is in the own colour of the body the cue is of (a dying enemy's), in no one's ramp. The reactions' cues are the same parts in each reaction's colour and power.

Cards name the cues they play: a carrier's `cast_look` `cue` (`FormBase`; played where the carrier shows itself, `form_shown`) and its `impact_look` `cue` (played where its shot ends on no body, as big as its `radius`, and where a carrier with no shot ends, `carrier_ended`: a slam's impact, as big as its ring, a teleport's landing, a rain's fall) and a modifier's `cue` (`ModifierBase`; played when its behaviour goes off, as `blast` does, or where it shows itself, as `zap` does: `form_shown` names the card).

To add one: copy `quake.tres`, give it a new `id` and file name, pick its parts from `CueBase.EFFECTS`, and play it by name. Each part is played by its type (`part`: `effect` when it has none, `motes`, `mark`, `light`; `CueBase.PART_TYPES`); a new kind of part is code, a row there and a player in Feel (`Feel.add_cue_part`, docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S1), and then every cue can use it by data. `tests/content/test_cues.gd` checks every cue file (`CueBase.problems()`). Moments with a life of their own (beams, swing trails, statuses, ghosts) are not cues; a dash's start and landing are (S5), and its path is its trail's motes. A flash part may play only for some cards (`if_cards`, `if_layer`) or sizes (`if_size_over`, `if_size_upto`), and the `arc` flash is drawn along the cue's size.
