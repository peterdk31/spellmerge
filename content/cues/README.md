# content/cues: moments' looks and weight

A cue is one moment's look and feel as data: the flashes Feel draws, and its shake, camera punch, holds and sound. One `CueBase` file per cue (`core/base/cue_base.gd` documents every key), named `<id>.tres`; the registry finds it by its `id` under `cues`, so a new moment is a new file and no list is edited.

A part may also be a floor mark (`{"part": &"mark", "mark": &"scorch", "reach": 0.9}`; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): stamped once into the room's mark layer, it stays until the room ends. The mark is a kind in `content/marks/`; with `"cards": true` the cue's cards' marks take its place when they have one (a frost blast leaves frost).

Something plays a cue by name: Feel's `play_cue(id, caster, at, dir, size, tint, look, target)`, or the `cue_shown` event from another area. `size` is in pixels (an explosion's radius, a ground strike's reach); a part with `reach` is that many times it. A telegraph's `size` is seconds instead: a part with `lasts_size` lasts that long.

A part can throw motes instead of drawing a flash (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1b; Feel's single-pixel particles, `content/motes/`): `{"part": &"motes", "kind": &"spell", "count": 60, "reach": 1.0, "spread": 360.0}`. Its `kind` is `spell` (the default: the kinds of the cue's cards, its look's layers, in its caster's ramp and colour, so a fire blast throws embers and a frost one flecks), `surface` (the surface kinds, in the colours of what broke: the room's walls, or the art of a body that died) or one kind's id (a kind no card names, such as `ash`, is thrown only this way); `colours` (`spell` or `surface`) says which colours they cool through in place of their kind's. A `spell` part throws the kinds of the cards of the spell the cue is of (its tag; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S2), or of its look's layers when it has none. `count` is how many in all (split between the kinds), `reach` flies them out to the cue's size times it (`radius` to that many pixels instead), and `spread`, `up` (times the kind's own), `ring` and `way` shape the throw, `often` marks a moment of many a frame (a hit's sparks, which leave the rarer moments' rows alone) (`CueBase` documents each key). Every count is times the "Particle amount" setting; with "Particles" off there are none.

A cue may have a colour and a strength of its own (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S6): `color` is the exact colour of every flash and motes part that names none (nor `plain`, nor `bare`) of its own, and of what Feel draws of the moment in code (a reaction's name); `power` is how strong it plays, by one rule in `Feel.play_cue`: times every flash's size, every motes part's count and how far they fly, every mark's radius, its holds, shake and punch (never a part's seconds, nor an arc's length). A motes part may fly out a fixed `radius` of pixels when the cue has no size. The reactions' cues are the same parts in each reaction's colour and power.

Cards name the cues they play (S6 2): a carrier's `cast_cue` (`FormBase`; on its caster's staff when a seat casts it, in the seat's colours: the flash, a ring, the camera kicked back; `cast` by default), `shown_cue` (where the carrier shows itself, `form_shown`), `impact_cue` (where each of its shots strikes, on a body or a wall, as big as its `radius`, in the shot's colour and cards; `impact` by default) and `end_cue` (where a carrier with no shot ends, `carrier_ended`: a slam's impact, as big as its ring, a teleport's landing, a rain's fall), and a modifier's `cue` (`ModifierBase`; played when its behaviour goes off, as `blast` does). A kick `back` or along `aim` needs the cue to have a direction (a cast with no aim kicks nowhere).

|---|---|---|
| `cast` | a carrier card's `cast_cue` when it names none (the decoy's, the familiar's) | the cast flash at the staff tip in the seat's colours (`bare`), a 1.5 px kick back against the aim |

| `cast_orbit`, `cast_rune`, `cast_gather` | the orbit's, rune's, and the rain's, swap's and teleport's `cast_cue` | the cast flash and a ring gathering in (`ring_in`), the build-up; a rune's flash twice as big |
| `cast_nova`, `cast_ring` | the nova's and the dash's `cast_cue` | the cast flash and a ring flashing out (`ring_out`); a nova's flash twice as big |
| `impact` | a carrier card's `impact_cue` when it names none, and a shot of no card | a small burst where the shot strikes, in its colour and cards |
| `impact_ring` | the nova's, wall's, swap's, rain's, dash's and teleport's `impact_cue` | the burst and a ring in the shot's colour without its cards (`no_cards`) |
| `hit` | Feel, for a hit on a body with the way its shot flew (`damage_dealt` after `projectile_impact`), `size` its zoom steps (12 px each, bigger for more damage) | a spray along the way in the hit body's colours (ember for an enemy, its own ramp for a wizard) and its `spark` motes (`often`: a hit is one of many a frame) |
| `hit_puff` | Feel, for a hit with no way, and for a kill (the killing blow's spray turned into it, or a new one in ember) | a puff all round and a ring of `spark` motes |
| `blast` | the blast card's `cue`: core/spells, wherever a `blast` goes off | the ragged shell, a scorch mark (frost for a frost card), its cards' motes and the ground's chips out to its radius (its fragments and rubble since S3); shake and a punch away from the caster |
| `fireball` | an exploding barrel (not built yet) | the rising fireball, a scorch mark, its cards' motes and the ground's chips (its fragments); shake and a punch |
| `quake` | a carrier card whose `impact_cue` or `end_cue` names it (none yet) | cracks in the floor (a flash and a crack mark that stays), the ground's chip motes, the caster held, a shake and a punch down the screen |
| `slam` | the slam card's `end_cue`, at the slam's impact (`carrier_ended` `slam`) | a white-hot star, then the quake's cracks and crack mark, chip motes (with a few of its cards' motes), hold, shake and punch |
| `parry` | Feel, for a swing's block (`shot_blocked` while the blocking carrier is a swing) | a big four-point star in exactly Spark gold, the parrying actor held alone |
| `deflect` | Feel, for `shot_deflected`, aimed the way the shot now flies | a big white-hot cross in the deflecting actor's colours |
| `enemy_death` | Feel, for `enemy_died` | a white-hot ring, the killing shot's cards' motes and chips in the colours of the body's own art, ichor in those colours on the floor, the killer and the shot held, a shake (Feel breaks the body into motes of its art and adds the puff) |
| `boss_death` | Feel, for `enemy_died` of an enemy whose data says `boss` | the same as `enemy_death` with many more motes and a bigger pool of ichor, and the game-wide stop |
| `player_downed` | Feel, for `player_downed` (it names no position, so this cue draws nothing) | the game-wide stop and a big shake |
| `self_hit` | Feel, for a hit of an actor by its own spell | a ring in exactly pink, so it is never taken for an enemy's hit |
| `heal` | Feel, for `heal_dealt` | a soft ring in exactly green, no stop and no shake |
| `reaction_<id>` | Feel, for `reaction_triggered` of that reaction (`shatter`, `thaw`, `flare`, `kindle`, `stillness`, `rift`, `overload`, `superconduct`, and lightning's `discharge`) | a puff bigger than a hit's, a ring and a ring of the reacting shot's motes, all in exactly the reaction's own `color`; its caster held, shake and the sound `reaction_<id>`, as strong as its `power` (shatter 1.3 the most, discharge 0.7 the least). | `reaction` | Feel, for a reaction with no cue of its own | the same in white |
| `possess_start` | Feel, for `possess_started`, on the possessed actor's body | a ring closing in the driver's ramp (`bare`), the sound `possess` |
| `possess_end` | Feel, for `possess_ended`, on the body let go (when it is still there) | a ring going out in the driver's ramp |
| `zap` | the zap card's `cue`, wherever a zap shows itself (`form_shown` `zap`), as long as the zap | a jagged arc of lightning in the shot's colours for 0.25 s; a spark into the air (22 px or less, `if_size_upto`) for 0.15 s |
| `discharge_arc` | Feel, for `discharge_arc`, from the struck body to each one zapped, as long as the gap | the arc in exactly the discharge's colour (`color`) |
| `nova` | the nova card's `shown_cue`, at `form_shown` `nova` | the ring leaving the caster, 120 px across, a ring of its cards' motes, and a burn ring on the floor round the caster |
| `rune_burst` | the rune card's `impact_cue`, where a rune goes off (on a body or not: a rune lies on the floor) | the rune's burst (a dotted flash over the diamond, a diamond ring widening) and its scar on the floor, as big as the rune |
| `telegraph` | the rain card's `shown_cue`, at `form_shown` `rain` | a ring closing on where a rain falls, for the seconds until then (`size`) |
| `rainfall` | the rain card's `end_cue`, at `carrier_ended` where it falls | a column of light struck down (`fall`) and a spray |
| `vanish` | the teleport card's `shown_cue`, where it leaves | a column of light pulled up off the spot (`rise`) |
| `appear` | the teleport card's `end_cue`, at `carrier_ended` where it lands | a column struck down (`fall`), a spray, a small shake and punch |
| `trade` | the swap card's `shown_cue`, at each end of its trade | a column struck down and a small one pulled up, their rings crossing |
| `dash_start` | the dash card's `shown_cue`, at `form_shown` `dash` where it leaves (spell-spectacle-2.md, S5) | a few `dust` motes at its feet (`below` 8) in the room's colours, and for lightning (`if_layer` 5) an `arc` along its way as far as it goes |
| `dash_land` | the dash card's `end_cue`, at `carrier_ended` where it lands | a ring in the dasher's ramp without its cards (`bare`), a small flare of its element when it has one (`if_cards`), its cards' motes, and its cards' floor mark if they have one (a mark part of `cards` and no `mark`: frost leaves frost, a plain dash nothing) |
| `conjure` | the decoy's and familiar's `shown_cue`, where one appears | a column struck down and a puff |
| `dismiss` | the decoy's and familiar's `end_cue`, at `carrier_ended` where one goes | a column pulled up and a puff |

To add one: copy `quake.tres`, give it a new `id` and file name, pick its parts from `CueBase.EFFECTS`, and play it by name. Each part is played by its type (`part`: `effect` when it has none, `motes`, `mark`; `CueBase.PART_TYPES`); a new kind of part is code, a row there and a player in Feel (`Feel.add_cue_part`, docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S1), and then every cue can use it by data. `tests/content/test_cues.gd` checks every cue file (`CueBase.problems()`). Moments with a life of their own (beams, swing trails, statuses, ghosts) are not cues; a dash's start and landing are (S5), and its path is its trail's motes. A flash part may play only for some cards (`if_cards`, `if_layer`) or sizes (`if_size_over`, `if_size_upto`), and the `arc` flash is drawn along the cue's size.
