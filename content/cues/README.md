# content/cues: moments' looks and weight

A cue is one moment's look and feel as data: the flashes Feel draws (each a kind of `content/flashes/`, named by a part: `{"flash": &"burst", "reach": 1.0}`), and its shake, camera punch, holds and sound. One `CueBase` file per cue (`core/base/cue_base.gd` documents every key), named `<id>.tres`; the registry finds it by its `id` under `cues`, so a new moment is a new file and no list is edited.

A part may also be a floor mark (`{"part": &"mark", "mark": &"scorch", "reach": 0.9}`; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): stamped once into the room's mark layer, it stays until the room ends. The mark is a kind in `content/marks/`; with `"cards": true` the cue's cards' marks take its place when they have one (a frost blast leaves frost).

Something plays a cue by name: Feel's `play_cue(id, caster, at, dir, size, tint, look, target)`, or the `cue_shown` event from another area. `size` is in pixels (an explosion's radius, a ground strike's reach); a part with `reach` is that many times it. A telegraph's `size` is seconds instead: a part with `lasts_size` lasts that long.

A part can throw motes instead of drawing a flash (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1b; Feel's single-pixel particles, `content/motes/`): `{"part": &"motes", "kind": &"spell", "count": 60, "reach": 1.0, "spread": 360.0}`. Its `kind` is `spell` (the default: the kinds of the cue's cards, its look's layers, in its caster's ramp and colour, so a fire blast throws embers and a frost one flecks), `surface` (the surface kinds, in the colours of what broke: the room's walls, or the art of a body that died) or one kind's id (a kind no card names, such as `ash`, is thrown only this way); `colours` (`spell` or `surface`) says which colours they cool through in place of their kind's. A `spell` part throws the kinds of the cards of the spell the cue is of (its tag; docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S2), or of its look's layers when it has none. `count` is how many in all (split between the kinds), `reach` flies them out to the cue's size times it (`radius` to that many pixels instead), and `spread`, `up` (times the kind's own), `ring` and `way` shape the throw, `often` marks a moment of many a frame (a hit's sparks, which leave the rarer moments' rows alone) (`CueBase` documents each key). Every count is times the "Particle amount" setting; with "Particles" off there are none.

A flash draws its shape and its light only (S6, item 3): its sparks, rubble and card particles are the motes parts of the cue that shows it, so they are data like the rest. A strike (`impact`, `impact_ring`, `expire`, `deflect`, `parry`, `slam`, `shield_block`, `gem_pickup`) throws `spark` motes back against the shot (`way` `back`, `often` where it comes by the hundred); a cast (`cast*`) a few along the aim, in the seat's colours (`bare`: a motes part in the caster's ramp without the cards or the cue's tint, as a bare flash); a rune's burst a ring of sparks all round and its cards' motes; a column pulled up (`vanish`, `dismiss`, `trade`, `revive`) `glimmer` motes rising up it; a column struck down (`appear`, `rainfall`, `conjure`, `trade`) sparks along the floor. A strike's card motes are its shot's own shed (`Spectacle.shed_hit`, `shed_on_wall`), so its cue throws none of its own. A cue that names the `poof` flash throws its own sparks.

A part may light the room instead, while "Spell light" is on (S6, item 4): `{"part": &"light", "radius": 44.0, "strength": 1.0, "seconds": 0.45, "color": ...}` is a soft light round the cue's point, fading over its seconds (`reach` makes it the cue's size times that; `color`, `plain` and `bare` as a flash's, or the cue's colour or its ramp, tint and cards). A cue with a light part lights by it alone: its flashes do not light the room by their kinds as they otherwise do, so the file says how a heal or a rune lights.

A cue has a line (S6, item 9): its way for its `size` pixels from its point (a staff swap plays at the staff's lower end, along it, its size the staff's length). A flash, a motes part or a light says where on the line it plays with `along` (0, the cue's point, by default; 0.5 its middle; 1 its end), and a motes part with `"line": true` is thrown from all along it, each mote from a point of it (a staff unravelling). A motes part's `life` is times its kinds' own lives (0.35: a quick moment).

A cue played on a body follows it (S6, item 9; `Feel.play_cue`'s `follow`): its flashes move with the body while they last, where they were played from it, and end when it is gone; its motes, marks and lights stay where it played. A staff's swap and a possession's rings follow their bodies. The `poof` flash is a staff's bone cloud (living staffs H4).

A part may be a stream (`{"part": &"stream", "kind": &"dash_path", "rate": 120.0, "side": 13.0}`; `gameplay/feel/streams.gd`): motes given off from the cue's caster while it moves, back along the way it goes, from when the cue plays until the carrier it shows ends (`carrier_ended` of its caster and spell), its caster has no anchor, or its `seconds` (2) run out. `kind` is `dash_path` (the dash path kinds, in the caster's ramp) or `spell` (its cards, at a shot's rate for each layer); `side` and `ahead` spread where they leave from, and `feet_layers` leave from `below` pixels under it.

A cue that comes by the hundred (a gem picked up in a horde) says `every`: the least seconds between two plays for the same actor; a play sooner is left out whole (S6, item 13).

A cue may have a colour and a strength of its own (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S6): `color` is the exact colour of every flash and motes part that names none (nor `plain`, nor `bare`) of its own, and of what Feel draws of the moment in code (a reaction's name); `power` is how strong it plays, by one rule in `Feel.play_cue`: times every flash's size, every motes part's count and how far they fly, every mark's radius, its holds, shake and punch (never a part's seconds, nor an arc's length). A motes part may fly out a fixed `radius` of pixels when the cue has no size. A motes part with a `share` sheds instead (a shot's end): each kind its own count times the share, flying as it sheds, as one of many a frame, so it leaves the moments' rows alone and is skipped once only those are left. A flash with `body` is in the own colour of the body the cue is of (a dying enemy's), in no one's ramp. The reactions' cues are the same parts in each reaction's colour and power.

Cards name the cues they play (S6 2): a carrier's `cast_cue` (`FormBase`; on its caster's staff when a seat casts it, in the seat's colours: the flash, a ring, the camera kicked back; `cast` by default), `shown_cue` (where the carrier shows itself, `form_shown`), `impact_cue` (where each of its shots strikes, on a body or a wall, as big as its `radius`, in the shot's colour and cards; `impact` by default) and `end_cue` (where a carrier with no shot ends, `carrier_ended`: a slam's impact, as big as its ring, a teleport's landing, a rain's fall), and a modifier's `cue` (`ModifierBase`; played when its behaviour goes off, as `blast` does). A kick `back` or along `aim` needs the cue to have a direction (a cast with no aim kicks nowhere).

To add one: copy `quake.tres`, give it a new `id` and file name, pick its parts (a flash part names a flash kind of `content/flashes/`: a new look there is a new file too), and play it by name. Each part is played by its type (`part`: `flash` when it has none, `motes`, `mark`, `light`; `CueBase.PART_TYPES`); a new kind of part is code, a row there and a player in Feel (`Feel.add_cue_part`, docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S1), and then every cue can use it by data. `tests/content/test_cues.gd` checks every cue file (`CueBase.problems()`, and `kind_problems` for the flash kinds it names). Moments with a life of their own (beams, swing trails, statuses, ghosts) are not cues; a dash's start, path and landing are (S5): its path is a `stream` part of `dash_start`. A flash part with `"floor": true` is drawn on the floor, under the bodies, instead of over everything (a ground strike's flash: the `slam`'s burst and star, so they never cover the body they hit). A flash part may play only for some cards (`if_cards`, `if_layer`) or sizes (`if_size_over`, `if_size_upto`), and the `arc` flash is drawn along the cue's size.
