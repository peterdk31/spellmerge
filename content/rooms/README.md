# Rooms

## Purpose

Room templates, one folder per biome. A room is a tile-based arena where enemies are fought (the ones the run's map puts in it: run generation RG3; no waves, boss OB2a), a reward is picked or a story event happens.

**Built in M3** (Run structure; see docs/ROADMAP.md). A small M1 base class already exists (`core/base/room_base.gd`, `RoomBase`); M3 extends it. The example to copy is the M1 crypt arena (see "Example to copy"); M3 adds more rooms.

## Contract

- A room is a `.tscn` scene with tiles from the shared tileset (M1 uses repeated `Sprite2D` tiles; a `TileMapLayer` is welcome once a tileset resource exists), plus a `.tres` on the room base class (`RoomBase`: `id`, `scene` and rules over the whole room, `stand_up_after`, `ambient`, `shade`, `air`, `music` (its `MusicTrack`: the crypt's fight rooms share `crypt/crypt_music.tres`, the broom's hall has none, as its boss brings its own, and the hub has `hub/hub_music.tres`; sound plan AU7), `ambience` (its sound under the music: the crypt's rooms share `crypt/crypt_ambience.tres`, the hub has `hub/hub_ambience.tres`; docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, D), `mist` (its low floor mist, a `RoomMist`, drawn by Feel only while spell light is on, thin over the middle and thicker toward the room's edge and pits: the crypt's fight rooms share `crypt/crypt_mist.tres`, the broom's hall has its thicker `crypt/boss_broom/boss_mist.tres`, the hub none; docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, F), the colour and mote overrides; M3 extends it).
- **The scene is the one place a position is written** (props W8a/W8c, docs/milestones/props.md, "W8c: a room foundation"), so a room is edited by hand in the Godot editor and what you move is what the game reads. The game builds a room's layout once a run (`RoomBase.build()`: its scene; a generated room, M3, from pieces by the run's seeded chance) and reads what is where from that instance, the one it shows (`RoomPlaces.read`). Everything is found **at any depth, in the tree's order**, so a piece of scene carries its own:
  - spawns and doors: `Marker2D`s under a node named `PlayerSpawns` (by seat), `EnemySpawns`, `Doors` or `JoinerSpawns` (by seat; where a seat that joins the room under way appears, the hub's beside its altar; none: the player spawn farthest from danger);
  - **what stands in it: a `RoomPlacement`** (core/base), a point holding the data of what stands there. Its kinds are editor scripts in tools/: `PropStandIn` (Add Child Node, "PropStandIn"; pick its `prop`: the editor draws the prop exactly as the game will, in any `state`, its hurt shape red, its ground cyan, where it is stood on yellow) `EnemyStandIn` (pick its `enemy`: the editor shows its scene; the training and shooting dummies) and `ItemStandIn` (items I6: pick its `item`, a premade staff of content/items/ or a card; no body stands for it: Flow hands the room's items to the Items area, `room_items_placed`, which lays one copy each). A new kind of placed thing is a new placement class and data class, nothing in the reader;
  - **its zones: a `RoomZone`** (core/base, a `ReferenceRect` drawn in the editor only, its `zone_name` written in its corner; move and size it by its handles), for a place that is no thing (the hub's `prop_yard`), or the place of a prop the room must not name, a zone named for its role (the hub's `start_lectern`: the game stands the registry's prop of that role at its middle, so deleting that prop's folder leaves the room whole; run generation RG7). A zone means what the game makes of its name (Flow), never anything in `RoomBase`. **A thing that means something is a self-contained prop instead** (owner, 2026-11-11): a prop says what it is (`PropBase.role`) and where it is stood on (`PropBase.area_size`), so moving it moves everything it means.
  - **Places are whole translations:** a rotated, skewed or scaled node on the way to a place is a problem (`RoomPlaces.problems()`; the engine's trigonometry differs between platforms, the game's places may not).
  - **its ground cover's fields: a `CoverField`** (core/base, ground cover GC2, docs/milestones/ground-cover.md): a zone with data, a cover kind (`kind`, a `content/cover` id) and a `density` over its rectangle; it needs no name. Fields lie over the biome's own fill (each cover kind whose `biomes` names the room's `biome` fills its open floor by itself), in the tree's order, each deciding its own cells: its kind at its density, or none for a field of no kind (keep paths clear with one). A room's `.tres` turns the fill off with `cover_fill = false`. Where the cover grows is worked out from the layout alone (`CoverLayout.of`: its walls, doors, spawns, placements and fields), never kept: nothing covers a wall's cell or a door's, spawn's or placement's. Only a placement's own cell (its feet) is kept bare: a field that should leave a big prop's art (the hub's stone, the altar) clear must be drawn round it, or cleared over it by a field of no kind. **To see the cover in the editor** (ground cover GC3): a field draws the tufts of its rectangle as the game will, and a **`CoverPreview`** (tools/, Add Child Node "CoverPreview", anywhere in the scene) draws the whole room's, the biome's fill included, and draws again within half a second of a field, a wall or a spawn being moved (`look_again` after a cover kind or the room's `.tres` was made). Its rolls are the game's once the room's `.tres` names the scene. It is taken out before play, as the fields are, and the no-scripts rule allows it. **The game's cover** (GC8, content/cover/README.md): the crypt's moss and cobwebs fill every crypt room by themselves, and the hub's grass the hub (`biome = &"hub"`); each covered room holds its fields under a `Cover` node and one `CoverPreview` (`tests/content/rooms/test_room_cover.gd` checks it): each arena two corners of cobwebs and a patch of moss, the boss's arena cobwebs in its four corners, the hub a meadow round the dummies and fields of no kind keeping clear the stone, the altar, the lectern and the ways from the spawns and the joiners to the stone. A crypt room's cobwebs stay a small share of its floor (at most 6%): the AI walks round them (GC7).
  The game takes every placement and zone out of the room it shows (`RoomPlaces.strip`) and stands the real bodies there (`RoomContents`, gameplay/flow). `tests/content/rooms/test_room_places.gd` checks every room: its places are sound, and a prop's stand-in draws what the game's body draws, state by state. The placements and zones are the only scripts a room's scene holds (editor scripts; never game code).
- **Decoration** (W8c conventions, built when wanted): static art is the scene's sprites and tiles, with `mote_source` and `light_spot` nodes (as today). Decor that only reacts to bodies (grass swaying as a wizard walks through) is Feel's, never the simulation's: a decor field node in a group, scattered by Feel into one batch and swayed by a shader fed the bodies near it. Decor that changes the game (grass that burns, slows, is trampled) is one simulation grid of cells, never an actor a tuft, placed as a `RoomZone` with data of its own.
- Rooms are discovered by folder through `core/registry`: put it in its biome's folder, no list to edit.
- **As a run's template** (run generation RG1, docs/milestones/run-generation.md): its `biome` (`&"crypt"`) and the room kinds it can host (`kinds`: `fight`, `boss`; content/room_kinds), so a run's map of that biome may pick it for a room of that kind. A room with no kinds (the hub) is offered for no run; one with kinds must name its biome (`RoomBase.template_problems`, checked by `tests/content/runs/test_runs.gd`). The crypt arena hosts `fight`.
- **Its exits** (run generation RG4): a room of a run's map leads on by its exits, props of the role `room_exit` (`content/props/room_exit`, a `PropStandIn` each) placed where the party leaves, on the floor clear of walls; a template stands only for a room with no more links than it has exits (`RoomBase.exit_count`), and a kind that leads on needs a template with exits for the most ways a room may lead (`MapRules.exit_problems`: 2 for the crypt run). Dark until the room is cleared, then one lit a way, left to right; the last room's (the boss's) need none. The crypt arena has two, either side of its top door.
- **Its sockets and walkways** (connected biomes CB2, docs/milestones/connected-biomes.md, decision 2): a template a biome may join marks a `RoomSocket` (core/base; the crypt's rooms keep them under `Sockets`) at the middle of each mouth on its slab's lip, on the 32 px grid, facing out of the slab (`facing`); a mouth is 64 px across (`RoomSocket.MOUTH`). A template's size sits on the 32 px grid too. The rules (`PieceShape.problems`): open floor on the socket's landing (16 px inside it, a mouth wide), the slab ending within 16 px past it, every socket walkable to every other, in all four mirrorings. A **walkway** (`RoomBase.walkway`: a bridge) spans the dark between two rooms' sockets, its two sockets on its own edge at its ends; it hosts no room kind. The crypt's is `crypt/bridge/crypt_bridge` (a 64 px band of flagstones, 128 px long, drawn from its shape: a stand-in until the bridges' art, CB12). A `SocketPreview` (tools/) in the scene draws each socket's mouth, landing and cut, red where a rule breaks. The game takes the sockets out with the placements. `tests/content/rooms/test_room_sockets.gd` checks every template with sockets and every walkway.
- Space for four: the room must be playable and readable with 4 players, with room to dodge and no spot where a player can get stuck.
- Valid doors and spawns: the required doors, spawn points for 4 players, and enemy spawn points that are reachable and not inside walls. Validators for this arrive when M3 extends the room base class.
- Rooms hold layout only. Enemies come from `content/enemies/` through the run's map (gameplay/run: by the room's `biome` and the kind it hosts) and the spawn points (a room that hosts a kind has enemy spawns and a player spawn a seat: `tests/content/rooms/test_room_templates.gd`); do not reference other areas directly. A room that stands dummies or props holds their placements (above), whose data the game reads.
- Tiles come from the shared tilesets in `art/tilesets/`, a ground drawn from a shape from `art/grounds/<room>/`; do not add new art here.
- What its walls shed when struck (Feel's chips) takes the colours of its own art: `RoomBase.surface_colors()` samples the sprites on its wall bodies, leaving out the floor's colour. Set `debris_colors` (light, base, shadow) on its `.tres` only when the sample looks wrong. Which motes they are is `surface_kinds` (mote kind ids, content/motes): empty, every `surface` kind (the crypt's chips); a room of another material names its own.
- **Its walls stop spell light** (docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, C): every wall that stands inside the room (a sarcophagus, a low wall of bones: a wall not joined to the room's outer edge through other walls) blocks the light of a candle, a flash or a carried light behind it, worked out from the layout as it is built (Feel's `light_blockers.gd`); a floating room's void and a tiled room's outer walls do not (light is never drawn past them). Nothing to set: a new low wall blocks by being a wall. A room's walls and the props that block light (its pillars) fit `LightBlockers.MAX` (32) boxes; `tests/gameplay/feel/test_shadows.gd` counts every crypt room's.
- Its `Floor` sprite is at `z_index` `RoomBase.FLOOR_Z` (-2), so Feel's floor marks (one above it) lie on the floor and under every body (0); `tests/gameplay/feel/test_marks.gd` checks every room. The marks drawn in the floor's own shades take its colours from its art (`RoomBase.floor_colors()`: the `Floor` sprite's most common colours times its modulate); set `floor_mark_colors` (light, base, shadow) only when the sample looks wrong.

## A room's ground from a shape

(Hub rework, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Built for every room".) Any room may draw its floor from a shape file instead of tiles: `<room>.shape.json` beside its scene says its `size` (whole 8 px cells), its `outlines` (land inside, the void outside: holes with `"void": "hole"`, an island in the void as the hub is, or walls, the default, a floor's edge inside walls: a wavy, rough oval each, `centre`, `radii`, `waves`, `rough`, `seed`), the `cracks` that run in from an outline's edge (`angle` in turns round its middle, `width` at the mouth, `length`, `seed`: they wander, taper and fork, and stop short of every `keep_clear` circle and of everything the room's scene places, `keep_clear_round` pixels round each), `areas` of another ground material inside it, which edge style its edges are (`edge`, content/edges) and which ground material its floor is (`ground`, content/grounds). `python3 tools/ground_shape.py <shape file>` writes `<room>_ground.tscn` beside it and its art in `art/grounds/<room>/`: a `Sprite2D` root that the room instances under the name `Floor` (so everything that reads a room's `Floor` reads it), opaque wherever a body may stand; its walls and holes (a cell is open where the art is clear at its middle, so the two never disagree; an open cell in a crack is a hole, `Holes` on the hole layer, and one in the void is as the shape's `void` says); the rock face, on the walls' body (on the root when the void is holes); the glow; the rims' and drips' `MoteSource` lines. A crack narrower than a cell is drawn into the floor and is never wall or hole; every part of an edge beside a crack fades smoothly with its width. The scene carries the hash of what it was drawn from, and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools` fails while a shape, the room's placements, its edge style, its ground materials or the generator changed since, or a file differs from what is drawn again. Never edit a ground scene by hand. A room drawn from a shape names its `floor_mark_colors` and `debris_colors` in its `.tres` when its art is large (sampling it would stall the first marks). The hub is the first. **Drawn again as it is laid out** (connected biomes CB4, docs/milestones/connected-biomes.md, decision 6): a room whose `.tres` names its shape (`ground_shape = ExtResource(...)`, the `<room>.shape.json`; every crypt room) has its ground drawn again by the game as Flow lays it out, from the shape as the room is placed, mirrored or not (gameplay/feel/README.md, "The ground, drawn as a room is laid out"): unmirrored it is the baked art to the pixel (`tests/gameplay/feel/test_ground_drawing.gd`), mirrored it is drawn again, never flipped, the light still from the upper left. The baked scene stays: it is what the editor shows, it holds the room's walls and holes, and its art is what the drawing is held to. A new room drawn from a shape names it in its `.tres` (the test fails for one that does not); the hub names none and keeps its baked art until it moves over too.

**A hall inside walls** (crypt rework CR2, docs/milestones/crypt-rework.md): a shape's `frame` is a floor inside walls, a rounded rectangle `inset` pixels in from the room's border (`corner` its corners' radius), its edge wandering by noise (`rough`, the noise's scales, and `seed`) up to `wander[0]` pixels in and `wander[1]` out; held to the plain line along each of its `pins` (`[x0, y0, x1, y1]`: under a door, a torch, a narrow way that must keep its width), easing back over 12 pixels; opened into `bays` (`[x, y, r]`, rough round alcoves); and pushed back round everything its `clear` names (`{"round": r, "of": ["PlayerSpawns", "Props", ...]}`: every node of the room's scene under one of those, `r` pixels round), so a wandering wall never covers a spawn, an exit or a prop. The room's outermost cell is always wall. Where the void is wall and the edge style draws a `top` or a `face` (a style that wants its walls drawn whole names both), the walls get their own art (`WallArt`, a sprite on the `Walls` body, so a hit sheds its colours; tools/ground_walls.py): the face above the floor's top edges, the rough top falling into the dark. A shape's `pits` are outlines cut out of the floor (as an outline is drawn, a wavy, rough oval), always holes whatever the room's `void` says; a crack may start from a pit's edge (`"pit": <index>`) and runs outward. A shape may name a second edge style for its pits and the cracks from them (`pit_edge`): each edge is drawn in the style of the open ground nearest it; a pit's shaft is that style's rock face, on the ground scene's root, and its rim (if the style has motes) runs with the pit on its left, so its motes fall in. **A floating room** (the owner's second round, crypt rework CR-G): a frame drawn in the edge style `crypt_edge` keeps its `void` a wall but draws no wall: the slab of floor hangs in the dark (a room places its own `Void` sprite behind it, as the hub does), its edge a broken lip, its rock underside under its south edges (on the `Walls` body, so a hit on the edge sheds it, unless the room has pits), dust falling off it; the edge stops walking, dashes and shots as a wall does. Its pillars are `crypt_pillar` props and its exits `crypt_gate` props (content/props), each a `PropStandIn`.

**Holes** (hub rework HR-H1, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Holes"): any room may have holes, cells a walking body stops at the lip of and that a dash, a teleport, a swap, a portal and every push cross; shots, beams, sight and gates pass over them and they are never cover. A room marks them as it marks its walls: a `StaticBody2D` whose `collision_layer` is exactly `RoomBase.HOLE_LAYER` (2; a body left on layer 1 is a wall), with `RectangleShape2D`s over the holes' cells (`RoomPlaces.hole_rects`). Where a wall and a hole cover one cell, the wall wins. The room's size counts its holes. **Outside the room is a hole too** in a room with holes (question 9: Flow lays the bounds' margin as holes, `set_holes_outside`, and `is_hole` says yes past the grid), so a long dash off the edge falls. **Falling** (HR-H2): a body that walks and ends a move over a hole with nothing crossing falls (`Actor`, gameplay/actor/README.md; `HoleRules`, core/base); a wizard comes back on its last ground, paying a fifth of its most health unless the room's `falls_free` (the hub); anything else dies of it, so a room can be cleared by sweeping strays into a crack; a boss never falls. Everything else sees holes as HR-H2 built them: the AI walks round them and shoots across them (gameplay/ai), no cover grows over one (`CoverLayout.of`, which blocks a hole as a wall, so a kind's `wall_gap` keeps it off a crack's lip), no floor mark is laid over one, an item let go over one lands at the nearest ground, a phase's add's spot over one takes the nearest ground, and a spawn, a door or a placement over one is a problem (`RoomPlaces.problems()`, its point; `RoomContents.problems`, a prop's footprint), told by Flow as a room is laid.

## Allowed files

`.tscn` (text), `.tres`, a room's `<room>.shape.json`, plus `.uid` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Rooms hold layout only and carry no game scripts (their placements and zones, editor scripts, aside); a ground's art is in `art/grounds/<room>/`.

## Steps

1. Copy the example room into the right biome folder and rename it.
2. Paint the layout with the shared tileset; place doors and spawn points.
3. Edit the `.tres` values. Commit the `.uid` files Godot creates.
4. Run the checks below, then open a PR with a screenshot of the room.

## Example to copy

`content/rooms/crypt/arena_01.tscn` and `arena_01.tres` ("Crypt antechamber", crypt rework CR3, docs/milestones/crypt-rework.md): a **floating room**, 640×384 px, a slab of the crypt's flagstones hanging in the dark. Its ground is drawn from `arena_01.shape.json` ("A room's ground from a shape", above: a `frame` in the edge style `crypt_edge` on the ground `flagstone`, its `void` a wall, so its edge is an invisible wall and nothing is drawn over the dark; two bays where the slab runs out; its floor pushed out round every spawn, gate, pillar and decal it names in `clear`): edit the shape and draw it again (`python3 tools/ground_shape.py content/rooms/crypt/arena_01.shape.json`), never `arena_01_ground.tscn`. Its own `Void` (`art/tilesets/crypt/void.svg`, repeated, z -3) is drawn behind the slab, `Floor` is the instanced ground. Its five pillars are `crypt_pillar` props (`PropStandIn`s under `Props`, each at its foot): they stop walking and the shots that hit them but not sight, rays or piercing shots, and they break (content/props/README.md); its two exits are `crypt_gate` props under `Exits`, standing against the slab's north edge with their arches over the dark. Its landmark is the middle pair of pillars, one fallen: `Decor/FallenColumn` (its stump and drums, walked over as rubble is) under `Decor/Beam`, the shared light shaft (`content/rooms/decor/`). It is lit by six candle clusters on the floor (`content/rooms/crypt/decals/candles.tscn`) and the shaft, its `ambient` 0.8, and it names its floor-mark and debris colours. Four player spawns in a row at the bottom, six enemy spawns, the `Doors` markers an entry (bottom) and an exit (top), two cobweb fields in its upper corners.

**No crypt room is tiled any more** (the crypt rework drew the last, the broom's hall, from a shape in CR7), but a room may still be: the template tests check what a room holds, not how it was drawn, and the old tiles stay. Torches are an instance of `content/rooms/crypt/decals/torch.tscn`: a bracket, a looping flame (an `AnimatedSprite2D`) and a flickering additive glow, each from frames shared by every torch; the room sets only each instance's `speed_scale` and start `frame`, so the flames are out of step. No script runs any of it. Each torch is also a light for spell light: a `Marker2D` named `Light` at the flame, in the group `light_spot`, with `metadata/light_art` naming the flame, so it lights the room round it in its flame's colour (core/base/light_spot.gd has the other metadata). Its painted glow (the additive `Glow` and `Core` sprites) is in the group `painted_light`, so spell light hides it while it draws the spot's light and shows it again when the setting is off: a torch is lit once (S6, item 12). A room's `ambient` in its `.tres` darkens it where nothing lights it (1.0, the default, is the room as drawn; below it darker), and `shade` gives that dark a colour. **Its air** (`air`, a `MoteSource`; none by default: docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, B) is what drifts over the whole room, given off by Feel over the part in view at its `rate` for every 100 x 100 px (`RoomBase.AIR_AREA`), its `from` and places not read; the crypt's rooms share `content/rooms/crypt/crypt_air.tres` (lit-only `air_dust`, seen only where light falls) and the hub has `content/rooms/hub/hub_air.tres` (lit-only `air_glimmer`). A file with no `id` beside the rooms is not a room (the registry skips it). The scene is layout only: a tiled room's floor and walls are `Sprite2D` nodes with the shared tiles from `art/tilesets/crypt/` repeated, the walls are one `StaticBody2D` on physics layer 1 (the wall layer, see the player README), and spawns and doors are `Marker2D` nodes under `PlayerSpawns`, `EnemySpawns` and `Doors`, the only place their positions are written (each room's own test, `tests/content/rooms/test_<id>_data.gd`, checks its data and that its scene has no scripts).
