# Spells

## Purpose

The **cards** players put in their staffs, in any order, and that a staff reads left to right (docs/milestones/M2-staffs.md, "Reading a staff"): **carriers** (`forms/`: bolt, beam, rune, dash, pulse), what leaves the caster and ends a group; **modifiers** (`modifiers/`: split, pierce, homing, chain, bigger, faster, nova, skyfall, orbit) and **infusions** (`elements/`: fire, frost, storm, void, lightning), which change the next carrier (wasted when none follows: end the group with **Pulse**, `forms/pulse/`, to put them on the caster, or on what fired a payload, a body, a shot or its point; docs/CARRIERS.md); **triggers** (`triggers/`: on hit, on expire, timer), which make the group read after the next carrier its payload; and **multicast** (`multicasts/`), which pulls more carriers into one group. Every arrangement is legal; there is no budget. Few cards, many combinations: this is the game's first pillar (docs/DESIGN.md). The class names (`FormBase`, `ElementBase`) and kinds (`form`, `element`) stay from the first design.

**Built in M2**, reworked by the staffs pivot (docs/milestones/M2-staffs.md). The new cards come one PR each (staffs phase C). The card icons are placeholders until the art pass.

## Contract

- **A card is one folder**, named like its `id`, and everything that is the card's lives in it: a `.tres` on one of the five classes in `core/base/` (`FormBase`, `ElementBase`, `ModifierBase`, `TriggerBase`, `MulticastBase`, all on `SpellComponent`) with its builder `description` in it, a 16×16 icon, its `assets.json`, its combos in `combos/<combo id>.json`, its own test `test_<id>.gd` (more `test_*.gd` if it has more to say, like homing's `test_homing_forms.gd`; run with the unit tests, left out of the shipped game by `export_presets.cfg`; most extend `tests/support/card_rig.gd`, which builds, casts and steps a card with the real runtime and checks what every card is with `_check_card`), and optionally a `.gd` or an SFX. **Adding a card is adding the folder; taking one out is deleting the folder.** The rules every card is held to are tests over the registry in `tests/content/spells/` (the validator, the combos, the looks, the elements, the carrier cues), and `tests/core/test_cards_together.gd` uses the first cards as examples of cards together (payloads, several triggers on one carrier). Nothing else lists cards: the registry, the combo catalogue, the atlas and the tests that play every card find them by folder. A combo in another card's folder that uses a deleted card fails the combo test, which names it.
- **Most components need no script:** the fields below can express them. Write a `.gd` only for behaviour no field can say. A modifier's behaviour is named (`behaviour_id = &"return"`); a new kind of behaviour is engine work (`core/`: the `Behaviour` value, its row in `ModifierBase.RULES` and the code that runs it), not part of a card.
- Content is discovered by folder through `core/registry`. Do not edit any central list.
- Components depend only on `core/`. They never call other areas; they talk through `core/` events. Every effect takes a player index 0–3; nothing assumes a single player.
- Players, bots and the HUD never read a component's fields. They call `Spells` (`compile_staff`, `install_staff`, `cast_staff`, `staff_view`) and answer to its events; `tests/core/test_spell_field_reads.gd` fails if a script outside `content/spells/`, `core/base/` and `core/spells/` reads one.
- Spawn shots only through the projectile manager (`core/projectiles`); no per-frame allocations.
- Names are original. No names from other works (write Fireball, not Hadouken).

### Fields every component has

`id` (equals the folder name, unique across all four kinds), `display_name`, `description` (the one line the builder shows: at most 60 characters, truthful about what the card does, in the tone of docs/STORY.md; without one a debug build warns and the builder shows nothing), `icon` (16×16, listed in its `assets.json`) and `complexity`.

### Name words

Cards have no `name_words` any more: the generated cast names (staffs E1) are gone (owner, 2026-10-31); a cast is described in plain words by the compiler.

### Fields and ranges per kind

The script of each class documents every field, and its `ranges()` holds the allowed range (both ends included). `range_problems()` lists what is outside; the validator reports the same.

| Multicast (`MulticastBase`) | `extra_draws` 0–4, `mode` (TOGETHER, SCATTER, BURST), `repeats` 1–8, `repeat_interval` 0–1 s, `mana_cost` 0–100, `wind_down` 0–2 s |

Multipliers multiply the carrier's numbers and adds are added, in slot order. A cast's wind-down is every carrier's of its own group (payloads add none) and its multicast cards'; after the cycle's last cast the staff's `cooldown` follows (docs/milestones/cast-timing.md).

### The look of a shot

A shot is drawn by the shot shaders in `core/projectiles/` (core/README.md, "The shot shaders"; a shot in flight's head `shot_head.gdshader`; a shot its card's own shader, `look_shader`: a rune's `forms/rune/rune.gdshader`, a bolt's `forms/bolt/bolt.gdshader`) and Feel's motes from its cards (staffs B6a; docs/milestones/M2-staffs.md, "The look of a shot"). Its body stays plain: a small white-hot core shaped by its carrier (a bolt's 3 px cross, flying or circling, a rune's diamond) in the palette ramp of its first infusion's `tint` (a plain shot's in its player's colours), and a soft glow round it; its tail is motes left in the world along its path (one of three tail kinds a shot, a quarter of an infused shot's in its player's colours), so it bends with any path. The cards show in what it gives off: every infusion and modifier with a `look_id` adds a particle style, and the shot keeps a few particles alive (4 + 2 per style, at most 12), each born along its path as a mote with a style picked at random, so one card is a trickle and many cards a mix. The styles, by the name a card gives in `look_id` (`core/base/look_names.gd`; the engine's `LOOK_*` numbers in `core/projectiles/projectile_manager.gd` stay inside it): `embers` (fire), `flecks` (frost), `gusts` (storm), `pull`: pulled-in pixels and a dark heart (void), `sparks` and a flickering light (lightning), `motes`: rising motes, `glints` (bigger), `streaks` and a longer core (pierce), `burst`, `orbiters`: orbiting sparks (shield), `weave` of the body (homing), `glitter`, `hops` (chain), `wake` (faster), `weight` (heavy, sticky), `ripple` (growth, linger), `curl` (spiral, return, relay, recoil, orbit), `zig` (bounce, zap), `double` (echo, mirror, ghost), `converge` (magnet, siphon, possess), `drift` (slow, hold, faster casting). `plain` is a shot with no look (a mote kind may belong to it). Which cards share a kind is in docs/DECISIONS.md, and `tests/content/spells/test_original_looks.gd` checks it. A new card names one, or leaves `look_id` empty for none; an infusion should pick one no other infusion uses, so elements read without colour. Sheets: `tests/screenshots/shot_looks.gd` (still) and `shot_moving_looks.gd` (in flight).

A carrier card's own look (docs/milestones/card-looks.md): whichever area draws its carrier (`Carrier.look_drawer`: the projectile manager's flying shots or still shots, Feel's beams, swing trails or portal gates, Entities' familiar orbs; a dash or a decoy has none of its own), the card may name a shader in its folder, `look_shader` (start from the plain drawing of that area: a shot that flies, a beam, a swing and an orb are drawn by includes, `core/projectiles/shot_head.gdshaderinc`, `shot_beam.gdshaderinc`, `shot_swing.gdshaderinc`, `shot_orbit.gdshaderinc`, which a card's shader includes to keep every fix to them (the head lets it reshape its core with `HEAD_ARM` and `HEAD_BETWEEN`, as the bolt's does) or copies to draw a look of its own; a still one copies `forms/pool/pool.gdshader` or `shot_still.gdshader`; a portal's includes `core/projectiles/shot_portal.gdshaderinc` (its gate's quad and the plain lens) as `forms/portal/portal.gdshader` does; `tests/content/spells/test_card_looks.gd` checks it keeps that area's quad kinds, particle rule and warm-up), and say how bright and how far it lights the room with spell light on, `look_light_intensity` (0 to 2) and `look_light_reach` (0.25 to 2), both 1 as any shot of its size. A still card shapes its look once, in its hit size as drawn: `look_shape` (a pool's oval's half sizes, a wall's piece's half span and seam depth) and `look_open`/`look_fade` (seconds), which its shader reads as the uniforms `look_shape` and `look_clock` and its motes leave and come and go by: `look_motes`, a list of `CardMoteStream` (`core/base/card_mote_stream.gd`: a mote kind, a rate a shot, from its `area`, its `rim`, its middle `point` or along its piece of a `row`, how high, from when and until when on its still clock, how far apart), as many streams as its look needs (the wall's seam, the pool's mire and splash, the rune's drips and ring once armed). Sharing the plain shader is the default: a shader of its own costs a compile and up to four draw calls with spell light on.

### No budget, engine safeguards only

There is no complexity budget and no build limit: a staff may hold any cards in any order, as many modifiers and as deep payloads as its slots allow, and the compiled numbers are not clamped. `complexity` (1–4) stays on every card as a rough measure of its power (the validator keeps it in range). What stays are the engine safeguards in `core/spells/limits.gd` and the projectile pool, which never change a build. Price a card by its mana and cooldown: a card stronger than its neighbours costs more; one that makes another obsolete needs a rebalance, not an addition.

### Reactions

Element interactions go in `reactions.tres` (a `ReactionTable` of `ReactionRow`s), so a new element needs no change in other components. One row says: a hit of `element_id` on a target with `status_id` does `effect` (BURST, CONSUME, EXTEND or SPREAD) instead of the element's own status. A pair reacts both ways, so it has two rows with the same `reaction_id` (see the eight that ship in pairs: shatter, thaw, flare, kindle, stillness, rift, overload and superconduct; the two rows of a pair may use different effects). `status_id` is the status the target has (`burn`, `chill`, `gust`, `hex`, `static`) and `element_id` the element that hits it. Feel shows a reaction by its id with its cue, `content/cues/reaction_<id>.tres` (copy `reaction_thaw.tres`: its own `color`, its `power` if it should hit harder or softer than 1.0, and its sound `reaction_<id>`, an `.ogg` under `audio/`; until it exists the reaction is silent); a reaction with no cue plays the plain white `reaction`. `tests/gameplay/feel/test_feel_reactions.gd` fails when a reaction of these rows has no cue with a colour, and when two reaction colours are too close to tell apart. `discharge` (lightning's static) is not a row: combat sends it, with a `discharge_arc` for each enemy zapped, and it has a cue like the rows.

### The combo row rule

**Every new component comes with at least two combos that work**, each a file of its own in the card's own `combos/` folder, named like the combo's `id` (`modifiers/slow/combos/slow_fire_fan.json`, one combo per file, so card PRs never edit the same list and a card's combos go with it). A combo lives in the folder of the card it was written for; it may use any cards. A combo has an `id`, `name`, `does` (plain words), `casts` (`staff`, `cards`: one card id per slot, read as a staff reads them, optional `at` seconds and `player`), `imps` (`[x, y]` or `[x, y, health]` pixels from the caster, who stands at (0, 0) and aims along +x), optional `walls` (`[x, y, width, height]` rects, for cards that meet walls), `seconds` and `checks`. Example: `{"staff": 0, "cards": ["frost", "timer", "rune", "frost", "nova", "bolt"]}` is a frost rune that drops a frost nova of bolts on a timer (docs/milestones/M2-staffs.md, "Reading a staff"). Check kinds: `damaged`, `status`, `status_count`, `lost_at_least`, `keeps_burning`, `reaction`, `max_shots`, `caster_moved`, `blocked`, `deflected` (min: enemy shots a deflect turned), `killed` (imps). Optional `guard` (true: the caster's own body stands at (0, 0)) and `foes` (`[x, y]`: an enemy bolt cast at the caster from there) let a combo show a shield block or a deflect. Optional `needs` (a card id in it): the combo's point is that card, and `test_combos.gd` checks its checks fail without it. Copy a file from a card's `combos/`. `tests/content/spells/test_combos.gd` casts every combo in the test arena with the real runtime and checks that every component the registry finds is in two of them, so a new combo needs no new test code and a new component no list to edit.

### What a new element still needs outside its folder

These steps are not new files only (the report of #243, the fifth element):

- **A status of its own** (`status_id` that combat does not know): code in `gameplay/combat/combat.gd` (its row in the status tables and what it does to a hit; gameplay/combat/README.md, "Every status is a row of one table"), a word in `core/spells/spells.gd` and a name in `core/spells/validator.gd`. An element that reuses `burn`, `chill`, `gust`, `hex` or `static` needs none.
- **A look of its own** when no free look fits: a new `LOOK_*` number in `core/projectiles/projectile_manager.gd` with its rate in `SPARK_RATES` there, its name at that number in `core/base/look_names.gd`, and its branch in `particle` (`core/projectiles/shot_particle.gdshaderinc`, for runes, beams and effects).
- **A colour ramp** if the palette has no free hue: `docs/STYLE.md` and `art/kit/`.
- **A look for its status** on the enemy: `status_looks` in the Feel preset.

## Allowed files

`.tres`, `.gd` (a script, and the card's own `test_<id>.gd`), the icon (`.png` or `.svg`), SFX (`.ogg`), a carrier's own look shader (`.gdshader`, named by `look_shader`: copy `forms/pool/pool.gdshader` for a still carrier, `forms/bolt/bolt.gdshader` for one that flies; core/README.md, "The shot shaders", has the rules a shader keeps, small and branch-light), `.json` (the combos in `combos/`, and `assets.json`), plus Godot's `.uid` and `.import` files and `.license` files. Limits: 1 MB per image, 5 MB per `.ogg`, 1 MB for anything else. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs (see AGENTS.md, hard rules).

## Steps

1. Copy the example folder of the same kind into `forms/`, `elements/`, `modifiers/` or `triggers/` and rename the folder.
2. Edit the `.tres`: `id` (equal to the folder), `display_name`, `complexity` (a rough measure, 1–4) and the numbers; a carrier also its `staff_pose` (copy one from a carrier that moves alike). Write a `.gd` only if the behaviour is new.
3. Replace the icon (16×16, palette from docs/STYLE.md) and the optional SFX. List each in an `assets.json` in the component's folder, in the same shape as `art/manifest.json` (`{"assets": [{"path": "content/spells/...", "origin": ..., "author": ..., "license": ...}]}`; for AI also tool and prompt). The validator and the art test read `art/manifest.json`, `audio/manifest.json` and every `assets.json`. Commit the `.uid` and `.import` files Godot creates.
4. If it interacts with other elements, add the rows to `reactions.tres`, then a cue for each new reaction id, `content/cues/reaction_<id>.tres` (see Reactions above).
5. Add its two combo files to its own `combos/` folder (the combo test fails until the component is in two).
6. Write its builder `description` in the `.tres`: one honest line, at most 60 characters, saying only what the component really does. Without it the builder shows no description and a debug build warns, but nothing fails.
7. Run the checks below, then open a PR using the template.

## Examples to copy

Each is a `.tres` plus an `icon.svg`, no script.

- **Form: `forms/bolt/`.** `FormBase` with damage 5, mana 5, wind-down 0.35 s, speed 220, one shot (`carrier` `atom`: a bolt is a bigger, slower atom), complexity 1. A beam sets `carrier` to `beam` and `ray_length`: it lives its `lifetime` (0.42 s: a held one covers its wind-down and the starter staff's cooldown with no gap), follows its caster and hits every `SpellLimits.BEAM_TICK` (0.15 s), so held fire keeps it on; a nova casts the carrier eight ways round its caster (`behaviour_strength` ways, more cards add). The dash moves a fixed `ray_length` (80 px) at its `speed`, 25 mana (its limit since cast timing T4); `reach · dash` goes 128.
- **Element: `elements/frost/`.** `ElementBase` with `status_id = &"chill"`, 3 s, strength 40, `damage_scale` 1.0 and a palette `tint`, complexity 1. The reactions with other elements are rows in `reactions.tres`, not fields here.
- **Modifier: `modifiers/split/`.** `ModifierBase` with `shot_mult = 3`, `damage_mult = 0.5`, `mana_mult = 1.5`, complexity 2. A modifier names only the fields it changes and leaves the rest at 1; its cost in damage or mana pays for its power. Homing and chain use `behaviour_id` and `behaviour_strength`; drag (`modifiers/drag/`) is numbers only, its speed over life.
- **Trigger: `triggers/on_hit/`.** `TriggerBase` with `event = 0` (HIT), complexity 1. The timer sets `event = 2` and `wind_up = 0.5`.

## Validate

```sh
tools/check.sh                      # hard rules, on your changes
ci/build/build.sh --quick --tests   # import, smoke test and every unit test, including the validator and the combo test
```

The registry's check in debug builds and `tests/content/spells/test_validators.gd` report problems as rule, file, field and fix (ids, icons, ranges, complexity, reactions). Fix every one. For a gameplay-visible component, add a short GIF or video to the PR.

## Review questions

Besides the general review (bugs, edge cases, 1–4 players, performance, clarity, fun):

- Does it create creative, surprising interactions with existing components?
- Is its power in line with its mana and wind-down?
- Does it avoid making another component obsolete?
- Does it come with a combo row that works?
