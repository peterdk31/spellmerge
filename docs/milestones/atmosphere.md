# Atmosphere: more layers in Feel

**Status: the owner's direction of 2026-11-18.** "The atmosphere in the game is just not there yet. I think it needs some more layers in Feel to really make it look good." The owner picked every layer proposed (darker tinted rooms, lit dust, shadows, and shafts, mist, sound and reactions), with **soft mist allowed** as an exception to docs/STYLE.md's no-fades rule (like the shots' glow), and asked for this plan, then phase A built.

Like the spell spectacle, this changes no damage, hit, rule or card behaviour. Everything here is drawn or heard, and all of it is Feel's (view only, outside the simulation).

## What is wrong today

1. **The dark is barely there.** Every crypt room's `ambient` is 0.8 and the hub's 1.0 (crypt rework, rule 6), so spell and torch light have little dark to stand out against: a torch's pool is a small step up from a floor that is nearly as bright.
2. **The dark has no colour.** `ambient` darkens evenly. Warm torches against a cool dark ("Warm against cold", crypt-rework.md) is drawn in the art only, so it is lost where the light is the game's.
3. **Darker would hurt reading.** The apply rect sits above the bodies (spell_light.gd), so a darker room darkens the wizards with it, against rule 6 ("readable first").
4. **The air is empty.** Motes come from spells, props and pit edges. Nothing drifts in the room's air, so the light has nothing to fall on between the floor and the camera.
5. **Light passes through everything.** A torch lights the far side of a pillar, and no body casts a shadow, so the room reads flat.
6. **The room is silent but for the fight and the music,** and its things (torches, cover) take no notice of the spells going past.

## Rules

1. **View only.** No simulation state, no sim clock, nothing a snapshot holds. Feel's clock and real time are fine (it is drawing).
2. **Readable first, still.** The crypt rework's rule 6 stands: wizards, enemies and shots are the brightest, most saturated things on screen. A layer that makes a fight harder to read is turned down until it does not. Each phase checks a fight's screenshot (`shot_rooms.gd`'s `room_<id>_fight`) with spell light on and off.
3. **Data, not code, for what a room looks like.** A room's dark, its dust and its mist are fields on `RoomBase` (or mote kinds and sources), with defaults that leave a room as it is today. Only a new kind of layer is engine work.
4. **Each layer has its setting's off.** A layer that costs GPU time follows the spell light's or the motes' setting (off: not made), so the web build on a slow machine can turn it away.
5. **No per-frame allocations,** and lights through the spell light's batches (one row each), never a node per light.
6. **Smooth** (docs/STYLE.md): nothing pops. A light that comes or goes eases in over frames; a layer turned on by a room comes with the room.
7. **Soft mist is an exception** (the owner, 2026-11-18), written into docs/STYLE.md as the shots' glow is: the mist alone may be soft and see-through. Everything else keeps the pixel rules.

Each is one PR with its tests, `tools/check.sh` and the suite passing, and a review.

## Phase A: as built

- **`RoomBase.shade`** (core/base/room_base.gd): the colour of the room's dark, white by default. Where nothing lights it a pixel is its art times `ambient` times `shade`; light adds as before. Feel's spectacle hands it to the spell light with the ambient (`SpellLight.set_ambient(ambient, shade)`); the apply shader draws nothing where nothing is lit and the dark is white at 1.0, as before.
- **Carried lights** (`gameplay/feel/carried_lights.gd`): each frame, every seat's wizard in the tree gives one light row at its staff tip (`Actor.muzzle_position`), in its ramp's light shade (`ProjectileManager.ramp_light`), reaching CARRIED_REACH px (its radius), CARRIED_STRENGTH bright, drawn in the spell light's batch for them (`SpellLight.light_carried`). While a seat possesses another body the light goes with the body it drives (`Actors.shown_of_seat`), still in the seat's hat colour. **It never pops:** its strength eases over EASE_SECONDS (9 frames) to half while downed, out as the body falls into a hole (`fall_share`), in as a body comes, and out where it last stood when its body is gone. Only while spell light is on, as every light.
- **The crypt:** each crypt room `ambient` 0.65 and `shade` a cool blue `Color(0.82, 0.88, 1)`, checked by `room_scan.gd` (`CRYPT_AMBIENT`, `CRYPT_SHADE`; rule 6, updated in crypt-rework.md). The candles light in Fire's light shade (`light_tint` `#ffa23c` on `content/rooms/crypt/decals/candles.tscn`): their flame art's lightest colour is Bone light, nearly white, so they lit white against the cool dark. The torch decal keeps its art's colour (no room places it).
- **Checked:** `shot_rooms.gd`'s `room_<id>_fight` shots before and after, every crypt room: darker and cooler, the candles' pools warm, each wizard in a pool of its hat's colour, the enemies still clear of the floor.

## Phase B: as built

- **`RoomBase.air`** (core/base/room_base.gd): what drifts in a room's air, a `MoteSource` (the props' data, reused: its kind, rate, colours, way, spread, reach and height; where a source leaves from is neither read nor checked, `RoomBase.problems_of_air` and `MoteSource.stream_problems`). Null by default, so a room is as it was. Its `rate` is the motes a second for every `RoomBase.AIR_AREA` (100 x 100 px) of the room in view, so a big room and a small one look as thick. Checked with the room (`air_problems`, part of `template_problems`, which the registry's validation runs).
- **Given off** by `gameplay/feel/air_motes.gd` (the spectacle's `air`): over the room's bounds within `VIEW_MARGIN` of the view (nothing where nobody looks), from points at random, as motes of many a frame (`often`), and never more than `RING_SHARE` (an eighth) of the motes' ring alive at once, so on the "Particles" setting's Minimal (2,048 rows) the dust holds at most 256 and never crowds out a fight's motes. Its colours are ramp table rows, asked for again whenever the table writes a row (`RampTable.writes`), since the table's 64 rows are a ring a long fight's colours can wrap. It knows the room only by what the spectacle hands it (`use_room`: the air; `step`: the room's bounds and the view). **Only while spell light and the particles are on.**
- **Lit only** (`MoteKind.lit_only`): the motes' shader reads the spell light's buffer (`SpellLight.buffer`, handed over by the spectacle with `Motes.set_light` whenever the light is made or freed or the ring remade; the light knows nothing of what reads it) under each lit-only pixel (`SCREEN_UV`, as the apply rect reads it), and the kind's row says so in a fourth texel of the kind table (`KIND_COLUMNS` 4). **Its steps are the floor's:** `SpellLight.lit_steps()` from `LIT_FROM` 0.16 and `BRIGHT_FROM` 0.45, the numbers the apply rect's `lit_from` and `bright_from` are set from: not drawn below 0.12 (`FAINT_SHARE` of LIT_FROM: a faint shadow-shade stage just inside a pool's edge), base from 0.16 (where the floor reads lit), light from 0.305, white-hot from 0.45, tinted toward the light's hue (`lit_hue` 0.6). **Smooth:** a lit-only mote does not cool over its life; it comes in and goes out through its shades (`RISE_SPEED`: its brightest from a quarter of its life to three quarters), the dimmer of its life's shade and its light's winning, so it changes a shade at a time and is only ever gone from its shadow shade; its highlights are drawn as its body. `Motes.lit_shade` and `Motes.lit_life_shade` are the shader's pick in GDScript, and a test holds them to that. A lit-only kind never glows and never settles. (A first cut let a kind skip its dark shades, `lit_darkest`, for the hub; it popped in its light shade, so it went, and the hub's glimmers take colours whose shadow reads on grass instead.)
- **Spell light off: nothing** (the pick asked for): no air is given off and a lit-only mote with no buffer folds away. With the light off the rooms are not dark, so dust drawn everywhere would only be noise, and the setting is the slow machine's way out (rule 4).
- **The crypt:** its five rooms share `content/rooms/crypt/crypt_air.tres`: `air_dust` (content/motes: a pixel drifting 1 to 4 px a second, swaying, 3 to 4 s, no gravity) in Bone at 8 a second for every AIR_AREA. Over the 640 x 360 view and its margin (704 x 424 px, about 30 AIR_AREA) that is about 240 a second and, at 3.5 s each, **about 835 alive** at the "Particles" setting's Low and above (Minimal's ring share holds it to 256). Nearly all are in the dark and unseen; a candle's pool (about 3,000 px² lit) holds about 8, warm specks; the wizards' hat colours show round their staffs, and the dust lights up as a fireball passes. Picked by eye from `dust_candle`: denser read as noise over the fight.
- **The hub:** `content/rooms/hub/hub_air.tres`: `air_glimmer`, lit only, rising softly, in Bone and Spark (Ice's dark shadow read as dirt on the grass), 8 a second for every AIR_AREA. The hub is not dark (`ambient` 1.0), so its glimmers show only round the wizards' staffs and the spells, a few each: a little magic in the air where the players are.
- **Checked:** `tests/gameplay/feel/test_air_motes.gd` (the data and its problems, the kind table's light texel, the buffer and steps handed to the shader and taken back with the setting, the steps from the floor's numbers, never popping by the mirrored pick, the rate over the area in view, the ring's share, the ramp rows asked for again, a frame allocating nothing, a room naming Feel its air and none given off with spell light or the particles off); `tests/screenshots/shot_atmosphere_dust.gd` (a crypt fight with and without its air, with spell light off, a candle's pool and a wizard close, the hub's glimmers): rule 2 holds, the dust is a few specks against the fight.

## Phase C: as built

- **What blocks light** (`gameplay/feel/light_blockers.gd`, the spectacle's `blockers`): up to `MAX` (32) boxes. First the room's **standing walls**, read from the layout Flow builds (`room_built`, `RoomPlaces.wall_rects`): a wall joined to the room's outer edge through other walls is left out (`standing`), so a floating room's void (its slab's wavy edge) never cuts the pools along it, and only the walls inside a room block (the crypt's sarcophagi and bone walls; a test holds every crypt room's standing walls to its own `Walls` blocks and its boxes to MAX). Then each **prop that blocks light** (`PropBase.blocks_light`, new, view only: `crypt_pillar` and `cracked_pillar`, false for every other prop), found in the actor table each frame (a body made again from a snapshot is found as any other): it blocks by its ground's box (`Footprint.rect_at`) while that blocks (`Footprint.solid`, its state's `blocks`), and its shaft is the column drawn over it (`PropBase.shaft_size`, `shaft_offset`, new, view only: the box round its standing art, the crypt pillar's 32 x 61 up to its capital; zero: its hurt box). Each prop's state is kept by its actor's key (`Actors.key_of`, id and birth) and node, so a new body under an old id, or one made again from a snapshot, takes its box as it stands; a room built forgets the last room's props (`use_walls`).
- **Smooth:** a prop's box comes with the room at full strength (and as it stands whenever the spell light is made anew, `refresh`: a pillar that crumbled while the light was off is rubble already). When it stops blocking (a pillar crumbles into rubble) its strength eases to nothing over `FADE_SECONDS` (0.6 s, about the rubble strip's 9 frames at 12 a second) and its column's top comes down to its base with it, so the light comes back over the rubble a band at a time as the shaft falls (36 frames, never more than a frame's share at once; tested).
- **Drawn** by `light.gdshader` (the boxes handed over by `SpellLight.set_blockers` whenever they change; the spell light knows nothing of rooms or props): a box blocks the way from a pixel to the light's middle unless it holds either end (a wall's own face is lit; a light standing in a prop lights round it). A pixel in a column is lit as the floor just in front of its base, and its own box blocks only a light behind it (past its back edge): a pillar's shaft is lit from before and beside, dark from behind. **Where a light is lit from:** a spot row from where it stands on the floor (`SpellLight.write_spot_row`'s `ground`, its floats 14 and 15: zero for the room's spots; a carried light's staff tip stands on its body's feet), so a wizard behind a pillar lights round itself and its staff tip over a low wall never opens the wall; a flash's or a cue's from its middle, moved in front of the base only when it is on a shaft (a blast on the pillar's face; they never move). A fading box blocks by its strength, cut into the floor's bands by the apply rect as any light, so the light returns a band at a time. Analytic boxes rather than a mask texture: exact, crisp at the light buffer's resolution, nothing to upload, and a GDScript mirror (`LightBlockers.lit_as`, `column_of`, `shade_between`) that the bodies' shadows and the tests use. Headless runs compile no shader, so a test checks the shader's constants and each line of its pick by text against the mirror; that they agree in the picture is checked by eye. The vertex stage marks which boxes come near each light (a bit each), so a light near none does no more work than before.
- **Shots are not blocked** (`SpellLight.SHOTS_BLOCKED` false): their lights pass the pillars as their glow does. Measured with `tests/benchmark/shot_gpu.gd -- --light` on llvmpipe under the Compatibility renderer (the web build's; GLSL ES 3.0 could not be started here, no EGL) (light buffer GPU median; 60 / 400 / 3,000 shots): before phase C 0.39 / 1.24 / 8.10 ms; with phase C and no boxes 0.42 / 1.35 / 7.83 ms; with a crypt room's 12 boxes (`--blockers`) and shots blocked 0.90 / 3.94 / 31.26 ms (3.5x at 3,000), so they are not: 0.40 / 1.32 / 9.39 ms (within llvmpipe's run-to-run noise of no boxes). Flashes, cue lights, the room's spots and carried lights are blocked (dozens, not thousands). A beam's light is not (a band along a path).
- **Bodies' shadows** (`gameplay/feel/body_shadows.gd`, `body_shadow.gdshader`; the spectacle's `shadows`, made and freed with the spell light): one batch (`Batches.multimesh()`) at `MARK_Z`, under every body and over the floor marks, one row a body that walks (wizards, enemies, bosses: an actor with a Mover). Props have none: they stand on their own art, and the tall ones block light instead. The shadow is the flat disc the body stands on (the staff's drawn shadow's shape: `RADIUS_PAD` 1 past the footprint, `FLAT` 0.4 deep, `DROP` 2 below the feet), in the Outline's colour at 0.45 (a little denser than the staff's black at 0.35: the lit room is darker round it), swept up to `LENGTH` 3 px away from the light. **Which way:** each of the room's spots and the carried lights (handed over each frame in the spot rows' layout, `begin_lights`, `add_lights`) pulls it away from where the light stands on the floor as much as it lights the feet (strength times `light.gdshader`'s falloff), less what blocks it (a body behind a pillar from a candle casts nothing from that candle), and the pulls add up (full length from `FULL_AT` 0.2): rather than the one brightest light, so two lights either side share the shadow and it never flips from one to the other. A body's own carried light (over its own head) casts none (`CarriedLights.owners`). Shots, flashes and cues cast no body shadow: thousands of rows, and they come and go faster than a shadow eases.
- **Smooth and hard:** the cast eases at LENGTH px per `EASE_SECONDS` (0.25 s), and is drawn rounded to whole pixels at whole-pixel feet, each pixel in or out by its middle: it moves a pixel at a time (tested), never soft. A body that comes (or a new body under an old id: `Actors.key_of`) takes its cast at once; an arriving body's shadow is none while it is under the floor (the arrival's mark is drawn there) and grows as it rises (`rise_share`, `ArriveLook.MARK_END`); as it falls into a hole it shrinks with it (`fall_share`); a body gone (dead, hidden, out of view) leaves its shadow shrinking away where it was over `GONE_SECONDS` (6 frames).
- **One shadow a body:** while the cast shadows are drawn, the shadows a body's own look draws (the staff's soft one, now in the group `LightSpot.DRAWN_SHADOW`) are hidden, and shown again with spell light off, as the painted light is for the spots. Enemies had none before; with spell light on they stand on one too.
- **Cost on the CPU** (headless debug build, Feel's step): the blockers 0.09 ms a frame (8 pillars among 100 bodies); the shadows of 100 bodies under 12 lights 0.30 ms with nothing blocking and 0.51 ms with every light near a pillar (the boxes near each light worked out once a frame). No object is made a frame (tested by the object count; while a box eases its packed arrays are copied into the materials, which is memory, not an object).
- **Readable first:** the pools are small (a candle's lit band is about 25 px round, a carried light's about 20), so in today's rooms blocking shows round blasts and cue lights next to a pillar or wall, and on a shaft's face (lit only from the side a light is on). No room puts a candle by a pillar yet; the screenshot stands one there with its light raised to show the wedge.
- **Checked:** `tests/gameplay/feel/test_shadows.gd` (standing walls only; every crypt room's walls and pillars fit; the pillars' data; a pillar's box and shaft; a crumble easing out over frames and its shaft coming down; the pick (behind, beside, past, either end inside, the shaft lit from before and beside, not behind, a fading box); the shader's numbers and pick by text; a new pillar under an old id, a light made anew and a room built each taking boxes as they stand; a wizard behind a pillar lighting round itself and a staff tip over a wall not opening it; the spell light handing its lights the boxes but not its shots; a shadow cast away from the light, sharing two, none out of reach, none from its own light or from a light a pillar hides; the ease a pixel at a time; an arriving body's shadow rising with it, a falling one's shrinking, a gone one's shrinking away where it was over frames, props having none; the drawn shadows hidden and shown again; a frame making no object; Feel blocking by the room it builds, a light made again handed the boxes, no shadows with spell light off); `tests/gameplay/feel/test_light.gd` (the shadows lie under the light, with the floor); `tests/screenshots/shot_atmosphere_shadows.gd` (`shadows_fight` and `_plain`, a crypt fight with spell light on and off; `shadows_pillar`, `_unblocked` and `_plain`, a candle with its light raised at a pillar's foot, blocking, the same frame with nothing blocking, and spell light off; `shadows_wizard`, a wizard's shadow cast away from it; `shadows_crumble` and `_pillar`, the pillars down and the light back over the rubble).
- **Open for the owner:** the pools may be too small for the blocking to read much in play: bigger candle pools, or a candle at some pillars' feet, would show it (a room's data). Whether shots should be blocked too on desktop (a setting's worth of cost). Whether enemies' new shadows, or the shadows' 0.45, read right in every room.

## Phase D: as built

- **`RoomBase.ambience`** (core/base/room_base.gd): the room's sound under its music, an `Ambience` (`core/base/ambience.gd`): looping **layers** (`AmbienceLayer`: a sound id, imported to loop, and its volume), **accents** heard now and then (`AmbienceAccent`: sound ids picked at random, a volume and its spread, a pitch spread, `gap_min` to `gap_max` s between plays, from up to `wide` px left or right of the middle of the picture), a `volume` over the whole (quiet under the music) and a `fade`. At most `LAYERS_MOST` (4) layers and `ACCENTS_MOST` (4) accents; checked with the room (`Ambience.problems`, part of `template_problems`, which the registry's validation runs). Null by default: the room is silent but for the fight and the music, as before.
- **Played** by `gameplay/feel/room_ambience.gd` (Feel's `ambience()`; Feel hands it the room's ambience on `room_entered` and steps it once a frame by real time with the middle of the picture; Feel's sounds and music are untouched). **Smooth for sound:** every layer starts together and loops; a room's ambience comes in from silence over its `fade` (2 s), and a new room's comes in while the last goes out from where it was over its own on a second set of players (as the music's tracks do), so a room change never cuts it; the same ambience in the next room (crypt to crypt) plays on unbroken; a room naming none fades the last out to silence. Each accent is heard as loud as the bed's fade has come, by the player's own clock and dice (`rng`, view only); one ringing when the room changes rings out. **Where it is heard:** every player on `RoomAmbience.BUS`, `Master`: the bus the music and Feel's sounds play on. The game has no other bus and no volume setting yet (the pause menu's settings are visual), so a volume added later on that bus, or one bus for music and ambience, reaches it with one line. Its players are Feel's children, so they stand still with the tree as the music does (the pause menu, a boss's intro) and go on after. Nothing is made a frame (tested by the object count). Leaving the tree (Feel freed with its world) it stops every player and lets go of its streams. **No device, no playback:** on Godot's audio driver with no device (`Dummy`: every headless run) it works everything out but starts no player (`RoomAmbience.audible`): a loop still playing as a headless run quits is left in the audio server's list when its mixing thread is starved (four parallel test processes), a leak of the stream and its playback the test runner fails on (the bot balance, stress and four-bot smoke tests, which play the real crypt). It is the engine's shutdown race, and Feel's sounds and music will meet it once their files exist.
- **The sounds** (`audio/ambience/`, a new folder of the audio contract, each in `audio/manifest.json` as `ai`, by Claude, CC-BY-SA-4.0, tier `placeholder` until listened to in play, its synthesis described in its `prompt`): made by procedural synthesis (numpy, a generator kept out of the repository), 44.1 kHz, Vorbis q3. **The loops are periodic by construction:** every noise is shaped in the frequency domain over exactly the loop's length and every tone and slow swell has whole cycles in it, so the last sample runs into the first (the seam's crossfade is built in); checked on the decoded `.ogg`: the step across the seam is within the loops' own ordinary sample-to-sample steps, and the level either side of it is the loop's.
