# The boss intro: a screen-wide overlay before the fight

**Status (2026-11-16): approved by the owner** ("Merge the plan"). **BI1 done (this document).** Written with the owner in an owner session on 2026-11-16 and reviewed by a fresh-context agent against the code the same day; its findings (the order of the room's setup and `boss_shown`, Feel while the tree is paused, the broom's cues, A in the other menus, the rules' header, the frame's version, the warm-up, a restore's open, the last seat leaving, the run's end, the docs' other no-waiting lines, names, the font's file) are written into the rules below. It replaces the boss plan's entrance (docs/milestones/boss-old-broom.md, "The entrance", and OB16's banner) for a boss that has an intro; a boss with none keeps the banner.

**The owner (2026-11-16):** "We need a cool screen wide overlay when facing a boss in its arena. It should animate in. The boss should be completely dark at first like a silhouette. And then when its name appears (with a cool font and effects), the boss should show looking mean. If it has dialog, now would be the place to show it. After the last dialog (if any) a player should press A to close the overlay and start the fight."

**The owner's answers (2026-11-16):** every player can move it on (any seat's A, on any machine); a drawn portrait for each boss; the boss is the only speaker; only at the fight's start (no intro at a phase change).

The steps are **BI1 to BI6** (BI for boss intro). Each is one PR with its tests, in order.

## What the players see

The view is 640×360. The overlay's timing is the HUD's frame (real time, as the banner's); only its pages and its end are the game's (rule 2). The world stands still under it, its sounds paused with the tree, as under the pause menu.

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0.0 s | The room has just appeared, the boss standing in it; the game has stopped on that tick. |
| 0.4–0.7 s | Black bars slide in from the top and the bottom (36 px each); the world under them dims to 40%. |
| 0.7–1.2 s | A slash in the boss's accent colour streaks across the middle from the right. The **portrait** slides in on it from the right, **completely black**, with a 1 px rim in the accent colour. |
| 1.2–1.9 s | The silhouette holds. Its eyes light up: a few pixels in the dark. The intro's first sound (the broom: a creak). |
| 1.9 s | **The reveal:** a white flash for two frames, the portrait in full colour in its **mean pose**, the overlay shaking 4 px and settling within 0.3 s. The **name slams in letter by letter** (each letter from 4× to its 2× in whole-number steps, 40 ms apart) in the title type, then a glint sweeps across it. The lore line types out under it in the small type. The intro's sting plays. |
| 2.9 s → | **Dialog**, if the boss has lines: a box at the bottom left, the boss's line typing out (30 characters a second), a blinking A glyph (`action_glyph`) once the line is out. With no lines, the A glyph and "Fight" show under the name. |
| A on the last page | The bars slide out, the portrait leaves the way it came, the name's letters fall away, the boss bar fades in; the game goes on from the next tick, and the boss's music with it. |

**The layout:** the portrait at the right, its feet on the bottom bar (a 96×96 frame drawn at 2×, 192 px); the name at the left, 48 px in and about a third of the way down; the lore line under it; the dialog box under that, 352×56 px, over the bottom bar's top edge. With a narrow view (a split screen later) everything keeps its place on the view's own width.

**Pressing A:**
- Before the reveal is over, a press does nothing (nobody skips the silhouette by accident).
- On a line still typing, a press writes the line out at once, on that machine only.
- On a line written out, a press is the next page for everyone: the next line, or after the last line (or with none) the fight.
- Any seat's A, on any machine; nobody waits for one player in particular (decision 1).
- While this machine's pause menu is open, its A is the menu's, never the overlay's.

## The rules

1. **An intro is a boss's data.** `BossIntro` (core/base, new; a sub-resource of the boss's `.tres`) on `EnemyBase.boss_intro` (null: no intro, the banner as now):
   - `portrait: Texture2D`: a sheet of three 96×96 frames in the boss's folder: **rest** (the silhouette's shape), **mean** (the reveal), **eyes** (only the pixels that light up in the dark, drawn over the silhouette).
   - `lines: Array[String]`: what the boss says, the boss alone (decision 3), at most `MAX_LINES` (3) of at most `StoryValidator.MAX_SPEECH_CHARS` (60) each. STORY.md's speech rule applies from now on; the story check holds every boss's lines to it.
   - `accent: StringName`: a palette ramp of docs/STYLE.md (the slash, the rim, the glint, the name's shadow).
   - `dark_cue`, `reveal_cue: StringName`: the cues played at the silhouette and at the reveal, a boss's own (the broom's `broom_intro_dark` and `broom_intro_reveal` in content/cues, as its other `broom_*` cues, OB15); empty for the reveal plays the shared `boss_intro`, so the shared cue is never tuned to one boss.
   - `problems()`: the portrait's size (three frames of `FRAME`), the lines, the ramp, the cues known. `tests/content/bosses/test_boss_intros.gd` (new) checks it for every boss with an intro.
   - The timeline's constants (`LEAD`, `DARK_AT`, `REVEAL_AT`, `LETTER_GAP`, ...) are on `BossIntro` too, so the HUD and Feel time the same moments without naming each other.
   The name is `EnemyBase.shown_name()` and the line under it `lore`, as the banner's.
2. **The game stops on a tick, and only commands move it on.** The pause and the page are the simulation's, so on every machine and in a replay they happen on the same tick (online-ready, "Commands at the next tick's start"):
   - `BossIntroHold` (gameplay/flow, new; Flow's, as `RoomAdds` is, its members state, its boss a REF) opens **at the end of a room's setup, once the world is in the tree and the room is announced**: one call, `_open_boss_intro()`, after `_show_world` and `_announce_room` in both `start_run` and `_enter_next_room` (in `start_run` the enemies are stood before the world joins the tree, so their ids are not open yet). It opens for the first boss with an intro among the room's own enemies (`_stand_room_enemies`; never the sandbox's spawns or a phase's adds), when the run's rules have intros on (rule 4), and never twice in a room. It stops the game (`Sim.stop(&"boss_intro")`), keeps the boss and the page (0), and sends `boss_intro_opened(actor, boss_name, lore, intro, page, fresh)` (core/events, new; `fresh` true here, rule 3).
   - `boss_intro_next_requested(player, page)` (core/events and `InputFrame.COMMAND_NAMES`, new, with its `COMMAND_SHAPES` row `"i"`; added at the end of the list, so the frame's version stays 4, as RG7's `start_depth_picked` did) is a seat's A on a page written out, carrying the page that seat's HUD showed. The hold, hearing it through `Events.listen`, turns the page only when that is still the page: two players pressing on the same tick turn one page, not two (the menus' body key, items I8, is the same idea). It sends `boss_intro_paged(page)`; past the last page it lets the game go (`Sim.go`) and sends `boss_intro_closed(actor)`.
   - **It closes by itself** when its boss leaves the tree or no seat is joined any more (the last seat's `LEAVE`, heard as Flow hears a seat leave): Flow does not step while the game is stopped, so its restart timer would never run and nobody would be left to press A.
   - **It is cleared with the run** in `_clear_run` / `_forget_enemies` (Flow is not in `Tick.RUN_ORDER`; it renews its own state), and a run's start lets every stop go (`Tick.begin_run`).
   - A stop for another reason (the pause menu, an unplugged pad: `Coop.MENU_OF`, `PAD_OF`) stacks with it: closing the intro while a menu is open lets the game go only when the menu shuts too (`Sim.stops`).
   - **Presses held through the intro do nothing on the fight's first tick:** a staff button pressed during it must not cast as the game goes on (`Frames.stood` carries a frame's presses to the first tick that steps). BI2 finds how the pause menu's close is handled and does the same, or drops presses for the ticks the intro stopped; a test holds it.
   - The boss's first cast still waits its `first_wait` in game time, from the fight's start.
3. **Snapshots and joins.** The hold's state (its boss, the page, whether it is open, the room's intro done) is saved and restored with the rest. Its `rebuild_derived()` sends `boss_intro_opened` again while it is open, with `fresh` false: a restore happens on the same machine too (`test_snapshot_game`, a joiner's own `start_run` first), so the HUD never guesses. The HUD's open is idempotent for one boss and page: fresh plays the timeline from the start; not fresh shows that page already revealed.
4. **A run's rule, on by default.** `SessionRules.boss_intros`, a `FIELDS` row `[&"boss_intros", "boss intros", 0, 1]` (`BYTES` 28 to 32: a 32-bit number a field), `Replay.FORMAT` 9 to 10 (as items I4's new rules did). `defaults()` has it on. **Off for automation:** Flow turns it off in the run's rules where it turns `intro_enabled` off (a test script is the main loop: tests, bots, the smoke test, screenshots), and the test suite's `reset_machine` turns it off; a test of the intro turns it on. It is a rule and not a machine's setting because it changes when the game stops: a replay recorded with it on would not play back the same with it off.
5. **The HUD shows it and sends A.** `hud/panels/boss/boss_intro.gd` (node `Root/BossIntro`, over the world and the corner panels, under the menus and the pause shade) hears the three events, plays the timeline, writes the lines out, gates A (the reveal first; a line typing is finished locally; never while this machine's pause menu is open, asked of the panels, since the tree is paused for the whole intro and `get_tree().paused` says nothing) and sends `boss_intro_next_requested` for this machine's seats (`Frames.seats_here`). **While it is open the builder and the inventory do not open** (`panels.gd`), so A is never two menus' at once; the pause menu still opens. It knows nothing of Flow or the hold.
   - **`boss_shown` says whether the boss has an intro** (a new last argument, `has_intro`, from its data; `BossPresence`), so the bar, the banner and Feel never depend on which event comes first: with an intro the banner does not show and the bar waits for `boss_intro_closed`.
6. **The silhouette is a shader on the same art.** `hud/panels/boss/silhouette.gdshader`: `reveal` (0 black, 1 its colours), `rim` (the accent colour round the edge, 1 px), `flash` (white); the eyes frame drawn over it, glowing. Warmed as the panels make the overlay (`ShaderWarmup.want(shader, RECT)` in its `_ready`), so it is compiled behind the first loading screen, not at the first boss. The portrait is drawn at whole-number scale only (2×).
7. **A title type.** One bitmap face, original and drawn for the game, all printable ASCII on a 12×16 grid, heavy and a little worn, for boss names now and biome titles later. Drawn on a grid as the game's other art is (`tools/pixel_grid.py`), as `art/ui/fonts/title.svg`, imported with Godot's image font importer (`font_data_image`, a text `.import`), so no `.ttf` (AGENTS.md hard rule 2); BI3 checks the importer takes the SVG first, and falls back to a `.png` of the same grid if not. Listed in art/manifest.json. A theme variation `TitleLabel` in `hud/theme`, drawn at 2× (32 px) for a boss's name. It is the game's first font file: docs/STYLE.md allows it for names only (decision 5). A test checks every boss's shown name has a glyph for each character.
8. **Sound from Feel, while the tree is paused.** The world's sounds and the music stand still with the tree for the whole intro, as under the pause menu. The intro's own two cues are played by a small node of Feel's own, `gameplay/feel/boss_intro_sound.gd` (its own script, never more of `feel.gd`), with `process_mode` ALWAYS and its own players, on Feel's bus: it hears `boss_intro_opened` (fresh only) and plays `dark_cue` at `DARK_AT` and `reveal_cue` at `REVEAL_AT` by the HUD's frame, and stops them on `boss_intro_closed`. Feel's `boss_shown` no longer plays `boss_intro` for a boss with an intro (`has_intro`). The boss's music is unchanged: `body_sounds.gd` picks it up from the first living body with `music` as before, so it starts as the game goes on.
9. **Nothing names the broom.** The hold, the HUD and Feel read only `BossIntro` and the boss's data; a second boss with an intro needs a portrait, its lines, a ramp and its cues.

**The broom's lines (the owner, 2026-11-16), two pages:**
1. "What an awful smell of The Wizard."
2. "I must clean it up."

The players are the wizard's dropped staffs (docs/STORY.md), so the broom smells the wizard on them; its grudge is the demotion to sweeping, and it sweeps them up.

**Names:** "intro" alone is the game's opening (`gameplay/flow/intro.gd`, `Flow.intro`, `intro_enabled`), so everything here is `boss_intro`: `BossIntro`, `EnemyBase.boss_intro`, `BossIntroHold`, `boss_intro_opened`, `boss_intro_paged`, `boss_intro_closed`, `boss_intro_next_requested`, the stop `&"boss_intro"`, the rule `boss_intros`. The shared `boss_intro` cue (OB15) keeps its name: it is the reveal's sound for a boss with no cue of its own.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-16)

1. **Any player moves it on, on any machine.** An exception to pillar 2 beside the pause menu and an unplugged pad: the boss's entrance is a moment everyone sees together, and since any one A moves it on, nobody waits for a particular player. Online it stops every machine, unlike the pause menu (DESIGN.md, no-waiting rule 7).
2. **A drawn portrait for each boss,** not the body scaled up: the overlay is the boss's close-up, and the mean pose is drawn for it.
3. **The boss is the only speaker.** No wizard lines, so nothing in the story speaks for a player (STORY.md, "Together").
4. **Only at the fight's start.** A phase change keeps its look, its shudder and its transition (OB8, OB9), with no overlay.
5. **A title type beside the body font** (the session's recommendation, for the owner to overrule in review): a bitmap face for names, drawn on the pixel grid and imported as an image font. The owner allowed `.ttf` files on 2026-11-16 ("if that makes it easier"); a face of our own drawn on a grid is no easier as a `.ttf` (it would need a font-building tool in tools/), so the image font stays, needing no change to the hard rules' checker (`ci/smbot/rules.py`, the owner's, still lists no `.ttf`).

## The chain (owner, 2026-11-16)

The owner: "Chain build the plan in new sessions. The sessions should merge their task and then start the next task in a new session." As the boss plan's and the ground cover plan's chains: each step is one session with the same prompt but for its step number. It builds the step on its own branch (`claude/boss-intro-<step>`), runs the relevant tests, then `ci/build/build.sh --quick` and the full `tools/ptest.sh`, has a fresh-context agent review the diff against this plan and fixes what it finds, writes the choices the plan left open under "Built (BIn)", updates this plan's status line, docs/LOG.md and the READMEs it touches, opens a PR and merges it itself once `rules` and `ci-tests` pass on the head it tested, then starts the next step's session. A link stops without merging, and starts nothing, on a test it cannot fix, a review finding it cannot resolve, a check it cannot fix, a decision only the owner can make or a permission refused, and says in the PR what it needs. The chain runs **BI2 to BI5**; BI6 is the owner's playtest, never started by the chain. The sessions are tagged `boss-intro-chain`.

## Not in this plan

Cutscenes of other kinds (they wait for M3's story schema, as `gameplay/flow/intro.gd` says); a wizard speaking; an intro at a phase change; a setting to turn intros off (it would be a run's rule, rule 4, and an M4 option); the music ducking under the overlay (the world's sounds stand still with the tree).
