# The floor's look: shaped water, ice and steam at 64 px

**Status (2026-11-25): built (this PR).** The owner's request of 2026-11-25 on the floor's states ([floor.md](floor.md): "It does not look that good as it is now"), drawn at ART0's 64-px detail (docs/STYLE.md, "The picture and the art's detail"). View only: no simulation change and no new state; the floor stays its own layer (one sprite a room, `gameplay/feel/cover_flat.gd`, under the tufts).

## What the players see

- **Shapes, not squares.** The cells stay the game's 16 px grid; only the look is shaped. A puddle, an ice sheet or a steaming patch is one shape across its cells: a side toward a dry cell is its edge, wobbling in and out by whole pixels along its length (a hash of where it lies along the world's line, so two cells' edges meet), its outer corners rounded (a stepped quarter circle, 3 world px), and where three wet cells meet round a dry one the dry one draws the inner bend (a fillet) in its neighbour's look. **A puddle grows and shrinks as one puddle** (the owner's answer of 2026-11-26): drying and steam shrink it from its outer edge to its middle, the deepest water last; freezing creeps ice in from the shore while the water shrinks before it (`FlatMotion.GROW_IN`); thawing shrinks the ice from its edge off the water; wetting spreads water out from its middle. Each by whole-pixel steps of its state's frames (4), through each cell's distance from its puddle's edge (`cover_flat.gd` works it out as what is drawn changes: a chamfer distance over the grid and each puddle's deepest; the shader's `depths`, weighed between the four nearest cells' middles), so a lake of any size plays out in its state's time. A motion is the cells joined by a side that began the same state at the same moment (one touch, or a lake drying at once), so new water at a puddle's edge, a patch of steam in a lake or a ring that dries first is a shape of its own; each cell's distance is written as its motion begins and held until it ends, so a change nearby never moves a front already playing. The last frame is exact (a grow whole, the next state's shape with its inner bends; a shrink gone), so no handover pops.
- **Water** lies below the floor: a band of its shadow under its upper left bank, a rim of its light inside the lower right one, and ripples (short dashes of its light) that grow and shrink by a pixel a frame, each from its own phase (6 frames at 6 fps).
- **Live water** keeps its sparks and adds arcs: a zig-zag of Arc's light across a few blocks, new each frame of its 4-frame loop (12 fps), and it lights the room round it in Arc.
- **Ice** is a raised sheet: lit along its upper left edge, cracks of its shadow, diagonal bands of shine and twinkles (a + of light over 3 frames of 8). It **catches the spell light**: where the light grid is lit (the apply rect's lit step) its shine widens and glints more, and where it is bright the shine's middle turns white-hot (Bone's light).
- **Steam** rises off the water as it boils away: wisps of Bone's light rising a pixel a frame (12 fps), thickest in its middle frames and thinning to none; a faint warm light while it lasts; the smoke motes as before.
- **The marks left.** Steam leaves a faint scorch (Stone's shadow) and a puddle that dried its damp (Water's shadow): a sparse speckle on the dry cell thinning away in 4 steps (3 s and 2 s).
- **Motes, a few:** live water's sparks (1.5 a cell a second) and steam's smoke (2), through the cover's motes (`cover_motes.gd`: at most 48 a frame for all the cover, none far from the view).

**Real 64-px detail** (the owner's standard of 2026-11-26: 64-px art is new hand-drawn detail, not a 2x upscale): every detail is worked out in single art pixels, never in 2x2 blocks: the edges' wobble and corners, ripples an art pixel tall with a second crest at their widest, arcs that step a pixel at a time, cracks and shine lines, glints, twinkles' arms, wisps an art pixel wide that curl, and the marks' speckle.

## How it is built (gameplay/feel, core/base)

- **The look is data** (`CoverState`, view only): `flat_surface` (PLAIN, WATER, ICE, STEAM: the shader's `surface`), `flat_glow` and `flat_glow_strength` (the light it gives), `flat_trace` and `flat_trace_seconds` (the mark it leaves when it ends in a state not drawn). `CoverKind.problems` names a glow or mark of no palette material. **Part 2 slots in by data:** oiled, burning and scorched are new states with a surface each (a new `FlatSurface` row and a branch of the shader's `surface`), a glow (burning: Fire) and a mark (scorched: Stone).
- **The shader** (`cover_flat.gdshader`) works at `CoverFlat.DETAIL` = `ArtDetail.DETAIL` (64 px a 32-unit cell). Its grid texture is RGBA float: red the state, green when it began, blue the state before (for the mark), alpha which of its 8 neighbours are drawn (kept by `cover_flat.gd` as cells change), so a pixel reads one texel, and three at an inner bend only. Compiles on both renderers (`renderer_shaders.gd`): no `return` in a loop, `texelFetch` only inside the grid, one varying (the light view's).
- **The floor's lights** (`cover_lights.gd`): each room's flat look gathers its glowing cells (`write_lights`; a list kept as cells change, so still water costs nothing) into patches of 2 x 2 cells, one spot row a patch at the middle of its glowing cells, its glow eased in over 0.15 s as a cell begins to show it and out as it ends (Smooth), at most 48 rows a frame for every room; `CoverRooms.lights`, handed by Feel through the spectacle to the spell light (`SpellLight.light_floor`), lit with the spots on the CPU, natively or on the GPU (`LightGrid.add_rows`).
- **Ice reads the light:** every flat look's material is a light reader (`Spectacle.read_floor`, keys `floor_<n>`; handed again whenever a view lays its flats, `CoverRooms.flats_version`, and to every light made). In a split of three or four its copies follow the sprite's texture (`ViewReaders`).
- **The effects' rows** (`effects_quality.gd`): `floor_motes` (Low 0, Medium 0.5, High 1: the share of a flat kind's motes, `CoverMotes.flat_share`) and `floor_shimmer` (off at Low: ripples, twinkles and wisps held still; a live lake's crackle plays at every row, it is the game's danger). `CoverRooms.set_floor_quality`.

- **The owner's answers (2026-11-26):** puddles shrink as one puddle from their outer edge (built); the +14% on 4p is fine, since Low turns the extras off; the light strengths (live 0.3 Arc, steam 0.15 Bone) and the shading (water sunken, ice raised) stay as built.
