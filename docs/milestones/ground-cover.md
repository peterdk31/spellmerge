## What the players see

- The hub's island is grassy; the crypt's floors have their own cover (moss and cobwebs: decision 3) in clumps, never under a wall.
- Grass sways in a slow wind and bends away as a wizard, an enemy or a rolling barrel goes through it, springing back behind them.
- **Fire lights it.** A fire blast, pulse or pool, or a fire shot ending on the floor, sets the grass there burning; it spreads to the grass beside it, burns whatever stands in it (the burn status, as any burn: resisted by the body's stats), lights dust heaps and barrels standing in it (their own triggers: a burn on them), and leaves ash. Frost puts burning cover out. Cobwebs slow whoever walks through them (the chill status) until burnt away.
- Bosses and enemies read burning ground as danger and walk round it; so do the test bots.

## The rules

1. **One layout, worked out from the room, never state.** A room's cover is a grid of cells (16 px, half a tile), each holding a cover kind or none: `CoverLayout.of(layout_root, room, kinds)` (core/base, new), a pure function of the built room's node, its `RoomBase` and the cover kinds there are. It reads the walls from the layout itself (the room's wall bodies, as `RoomPlaces` and `RoomBase.surface_colors` find them) and the doors, spawns and placements from `RoomPlaces.read`, so the game and the editor can never disagree. Two sources, in order: the biome's **fill** (each `CoverKind` whose `biomes` names the room's `biome` fills open floor by its own rule: density, clump size, how far it keeps from walls, doors, spawns and placements), then the room's **fields** (`CoverField`, new: a `RoomZone` with data, a kind and a density over its rectangle; a field of no kind clears). A room's `.tres` may turn the fill off (`RoomBase.cover_fill = false`). The clumps come from one seed-free helper, `CoverLayout.hash4(a, b, c, d)` (the engine's hash of a `Vector4i`, integer code; a `StringName` enters it as the hash of its string), keyed by the room's id, the kind's id and the cell; never `sim.chance`, so the editor, the game and every machine work out the same layout and no roll of the run moves. Feel's tuft scatter and the editor preview use the same helper.
2. **One event for a built room, sent whenever a room is laid out.** Flow sends `room_built(room_id, layout_root)` (core/events, new; W8c's named event) from `Flow._lay_out`, so a room entered and a snapshot restored into another room (`Flow.rebuild_derived` lays it out again) both send it. The cover area, Feel and the editor-free consumers hear it; nothing calls them. A restore into the room already shown sends nothing, and needs nothing: every consumer's derived layout is still the room's.
3. **What it looks like and what it does are one kind's data.** A cover kind is one content folder (`content/cover/<id>/`: its `.tres` on `CoverKind` (core/base, new), the tuft frames, `assets.json`, its test), discovered by folder (a new registry folder `cover`). Its **states** are rows, shaped like a prop's (`PropStates`) on purpose: a state's look (which tuft frames, sway, bend), what it does to a body standing in it (a status id, strength, duration and how often; none for most), whether it spreads (an element to its four neighbours, a chance a second), and its triggers to the next state: `element` (fire, frost, ... touched it) and `after` (seconds). The grass: `rest` −fire→ `burning` (1.2 s, burn on standers, spreads fire) −after→ `ash` (−after 30 s→ `rest` for the hub's grass only: decision 2); `burning` −frost→ `rest`. A new kind of cover is new files; a new kind of trigger is engine work (a trample that plays, decision 4, would add `walked` then).
4. **The game's part is one simulation area, not an actor a tuft** (W8c). `Cover` (gameplay/cover, new area and autoload, after `Items` in project.godot's autoload order; simulation, so its members are state unless listed) joins the Tick under the key `&"Cover"`, written in `Tick.STEPS` between `BODIES` and `&"Items"` (it reads where bodies stood after their move), and is renewed at a run's start (an entry in `Tick.RUN_ORDER`, `SimArea.renewed_by`). **State:** each cell's state and its seconds in it (packed arrays), the active cells (not at rest, a packed list in cell order, stepped alone), the queue of touches heard since its last step, each standing body's next effect time keyed by actor id (an id is dropped the tick its body goes: ids are handed out again), the counter of its rolls. **Derived:** the layout and the kinds, set by `room_built`, which never touches the state; it only checks that the state's arrays are the layout's size, and sizes them to it at rest when they are empty. **The state is reset to rest only where a room starts in play** (the run's start and each room entered; GC5 names the exact path and tests that a restore never takes it), so a snapshot's fire survives the `room_built` its restore sends. A room where nothing has happened hashes as a run of zeros. Spread rolls are `sim.chance(&"cover_spread", cell * 4 + direction, counter)`, the counter the area's one, cells in order, so cell order alone fixes the rolls. Standing bodies are found through the actor table, in actor id order, props included; an effect is asked as any status is (`Events.status_requested`), so Combat applies it by its own rules (resist and all) and a prop's own `status` trigger (`PropTrigger`) lights a dust heap or a barrel. The cover area never calls Combat.
5. **Elements reach the floor by one event.** Spells (core) tells where an element touched the floor: `floor_touched(caster, element, at, radius)` (core/events, new), one event per element of the spell, in its infusions' order. Sent from `Spells._blast` (a blast, and a pulse's ring where it goes off), from `CarrierPool.step` on each of the pool's own ticks (`_pl_clock`, every `SpellLimits.POOL_TICK`, 0.5 s, so its repeats follow the pool's state with no timer of their own; the first as it lies), and where an elemental shot ends (it hit a body or a wall, or ran out; the projectile manager's end, as `projectile_expired` and `shot_blocked` are sent). A shot in flight touches nothing (decision 1). The cover area queues each touch and fires the `element` triggers of the cells inside its circle, in cell order, at its next step. Spells knows nothing of cover.
6. **Feel draws it, from the layout, the changes and a fill-in.** `gameplay/feel/cover.gd` (its own script, never more of `feel.gd`) hears `room_built`, scatters each covered cell's tufts (`CoverLayout.hash4` of the cell; the same tufts the editor shows) into one batch per kind (`Batches.multimesh()`), at z `RoomBase.FLOOR_Z + 1` (the floor marks' z) and before the marks' layer in the tree, so marks lie over the cover and every body (0) over both. Its shader sways every tuft with a wind (view time) and bends it away from the nearest bodies (a fixed array of at most 32 positions, those nearest the camera, set each frame without allocating); a tuft's state picks its frames through the instance's custom data. The cover area tells what changed (`cover_changed(cells, states)`, one event a tick with that tick's changes) and, after each `room_built`, every cell not at rest once, so a machine that joins mid-fire draws the fire. Feel updates those tufts only, and gives a burning cell embers and smoke through the mote sources it already has. **Trampled is Feel's** (decision 4, look only): a view grid of when each cell was last walked through, from the same bodies it bends by, flattening the tufts for a while; no simulation state. The shader is listed in the shader warmup. The look follows the state, never the other way round.
7. **The editor shows what the game shows.** `CoverField` (core/base, an editor script) draws the tufts of its rectangle; `CoverPreview` (tools/, an editor script, its own child node of the room's scene, never a script on `Floor`) draws the whole room's cover from `CoverLayout.of`. Both are taken out before play by `RoomPlaces.strip` as placements and zones are, and the rooms' no-scripts test allows them. The editor finds the cover kinds without the `Registry` autoload (which does not run in the editor) through a static scan in core/registry (`Registry.scan_folder(&"cover")` or the like, the one place that loads by a computed path), so moving a field or a wall in the editor moves the grass.
8. **The AI steers off it through the bus.** The cover area answers where the floor would harm a body (`cover_danger_looked(actor, at, reach, sight)`, filling a `CoverSight` (core/, new) as `ground_looked` fills a `GroundSight`). The AI has no pathfinder: the brain probes the cells along its planned move (beside `AISight.detour`) and turns its move off cells that harm it, and `evade` steps out of one it stands in. The AI never calls the cover area.

**The reader work for fields (GC2):** `RoomZone` gains `data()` (null for a plain zone, as `RoomPlacement.placed()`), `RoomPlaces` keeps a zone's data beside its name and rect (`zone_data`), and a zone with data may go without a name (`problems()`).

**Built (GC2):** the layout, data only. `CoverKind`, `CoverState` and `CoverTrigger` (core/base; the registry's folder `cover`, none in content yet, a test grass at tests/core/fixtures/cover_content), `CoverField` with its `CoverPatch` (`RoomZone.data()`, `RoomPlaces.zone_data` and `zones_of`; a zone with data needs no name), `RoomPlaces.wall_rects` (the walls the room's size and its cover are read from), `CoverLayout.of`, `hash4`, `name_hash` and `rolls`, `RoomBase.cover_fill`, `Registry.scan_folder` (static, called through the script, `preload("res://core/registry/registry.gd")`, since the autoload does not run in the editor) and `room_built`, sent by `Flow._lay_out`. Tests: `tests/core/test_cover_layout.gd`, `tests/content/rooms/test_room_places.gd` (fields; every room's cover sound), `tests/gameplay/flow/test_flow_exits.gd` (`room_built` on every room laid out, before `room_entered`; once on a restore into another room, never on one into the room shown), `tests/core/test_events.gd`. **Choices GC2 made** (the plan left them open):

- **`room_built` is sent before the placements and zones are taken out, and before the layout joins the tree.** The fields are zones, and `RoomPlaces.strip` frees them, so a listener sent the stripped layout could not see them. A listener works out what it needs then (`CoverLayout.of`) and keeps neither the node nor anything in it; Feel's own nodes (GC3) go in Feel's layer, not the layout.
- **A field decides its own cells:** each cell whose middle lies in its rectangle gets its kind with the chance of its `density` and none otherwise (a field of no kind is density 0); fields in the tree's order, the later over the earlier. So a thin field over a biome's fill thins it, as drawn.
- **The fill's shape:** in id order, each kind's clump centres are the open cells it keeps to that roll under `fill_density`; a clump is a disc (dx² + dy² ≤ r² + r) of reach r between half its `clump_size` (rounded up) and all of it, rolled per centre; a cell keeps the first kind that filled it. `wall_gap` and `keep_gap` are in cells, Chebyshev, the room's edge counting as a wall.
- **Nothing ever covers a wall's cell** (one a wall rectangle overlaps, as the box round its corners) **or a door's, a spawn's or a placement's cell**, fields included; the gaps are the fill's alone.
- **The cover's data classes are editor scripts** (`CoverKind`, `CoverState`, `CoverTrigger`, `CoverPatch`, `@tool`, as the props' are): in the editor a resource of a non-tool script is a placeholder whose methods cannot be called, and `CoverLayout.of` calls the kinds'. It only reads the `RoomBase`'s properties. A test holds them to it; `hash4`, a name's hash and one fixed room's cover are pinned, so an engine that hashes otherwise shows.
- **The rolls' fourth key is what a roll is for** (`ROLL_CLUMP`, `ROLL_REACH`, `ROLL_FIELD`; Feel's tufts from `ROLL_TUFT` on), the third the cell's index: `hash4(room, kind, cell, roll)`, a chance out of 65,536.

**Built (GC3):** Feel's look and the editor's preview. `gameplay/feel/cover.gd` (Feel's `Cover`, `Feel.cover()`) hears `room_built`, works out `CoverLayout.of` and lays one batch a kind (`Batches.multimesh()`), a row a tuft, drawn by `gameplay/feel/cover.gdshader` (warmed through `ShaderWarmup.want`) at `MARK_Z` before the spectacle in Feel's tree; `show_state(cell, state)` rewrites a cell's tufts' frames, sway and bend for GC5. The scatter is core's, `CoverTufts` (core/base), shared with the editor's drawing, `CoverSketch` (core/base: `tufts`, `draw`, the kinds by `Registry.scan_folder`, the room by the rooms' `.tres`); `CoverField` draws its rectangle's tufts in the editor, `CoverPreview` (tools/) the whole room's. `CoverLayout.room_key` keeps the room's key. Tests: `tests/gameplay/feel/test_cover_look.gd` (a batch a kind by `Batches.multimesh()` at the marks' z, the shader warmed, the tufts the layout's and the editor's in the same order, a room built heard and laid and a new room laying its own, a cell's state picking its frames and folding away, the wind on Feel's clock, the editor's field and preview drawing the room's tufts, the preview never in the room shown in every room), `tests/gameplay/feel/test_marks.gd` (the marks over the cover, also after the setting makes the layer again); the screenshots `tests/screenshots/shot_cover.gd` (every room template under a stand-in grass drawn in code). **Choices GC3 made** (the plan left them open):

- **The preview is a `RoomPreview`** (core/base, new): core cannot name a tools/ class, so `RoomPlaces.strip` takes out any `RoomPreview` (and `read` passes over one) and `CoverPreview` extends it; the rooms' no-scripts test (`RoomScan.scripts_below`) allows it. No room's scene holds one yet: GC8 adds one to each room it places cover in.
- **A tuft's every pick is one roll** (`hash4(room, kind, cell, ROLL_TUFT + tuft)`): its foot on a whole pixel, at least a pixel from the cell's sides, in bands from the cell's top down, its frame from its state's `frames`, mirrored or not, and where its sway starts (that roll hashed again). **A kind's tufts are drawn back to front:** its rows are sorted by their feet (`CoverTufts.draw_key`: row, then column), across cells, in Feel and the editor alike. Across kinds a batch is drawn whole (one batch a kind, rule 6): where two kinds meet, the later kind's tufts are over the earlier's; GC8 sees whether that shows.
- **A frame is exactly `frame_size` of the sheet's top row** (the shader is told the sheet's size), as the editor draws it, whatever the sheet's size.
- **A kind with problems is in no room's cover** (`CoverSketch.usable`: `CoverKind.problems()` on its own data), the game's and the editor's alike, with a warning in the game; GC5's area takes the kinds the same way.
- **The wind is on Feel's clock** (`view_time`, set at Feel's step), not the shader's `TIME`: a look's time, and the same in a screenshot at fixed frames. Two slow sines rolling across the room; a tuft's tip moves by whole pixels, its foot never. A tuft sways at most `Cover.SWAY_CODE` (8) pixels.
- **The rows are kept in Feel** (`rows_of`) and a changed kind's handed to its batch once at the frame's `step`, however many of its cells changed in it; a frame with no change hands over nothing and allocates nothing. A kind with no sheet has no batch; a state that draws no frame folds its tufts away (colour alpha 0).
- **A field draws its rectangle's cells from the whole room's cover** (the fill, the walls and the fields after it), so it shows what the game lays there; with a `CoverPreview` in the scene (a `RoomPreview` whose `draws_cover()`), the preview draws them and the field only its outline. Both look at the room again twice a second (editor time; the field also at its own place) and draw again when it changed; a change to a kind's look alone is seen after the preview's `look_again` (the kinds are read once, as the rooms). Both draw at the game's z (`CoverSketch.Z`, set on the rendering server, so no scene saves it), under the props, stand-ins and walls. A scene no room's `.tres` names yet is drawn as a room of no id and no biome (its fields only, rolled by key 0); its rolls become the game's once a room names it.
- **No asset in this step:** the tests' and the screenshot's tuft sheets are drawn in code; the game's art is GC8's, and until then no room has cover in play. The screenshot covers the room templates; the hub has no biome until GC8.

**Built (GC4):** bodies bend the cover and trample it, Feel's look only (decision 4). `gameplay/feel/cover.gd`'s `step(now, table, focus)` (Feel passes its actor table and the party camera's middle) takes every body of the actor table, keeps the `BODIES` (32) nearest the camera's middle and hands them to each batch's shader (`bodies`, `body_count`), which leans a tuft within `BEND_REACH` of a body away from it, the nearer the more, by whole pixels at its tip. The same bodies walk Feel's view grid (`walked_at`: an `Image`, a float texel a cell, the shader's `trampled` texture, each tuft reading its own cell: `foot_cell`, the shader's lookup mirrored and tested), which flattens a walked cell's tufts while bodies are in it and `TRAMPLE_HOLD` seconds after, then springs them back over `TRAMPLE_SPRING` (`flatness`, as the shader works it out; a kind's own `trample_hold` and `trample_spring` since GC9, below). Tests: `tests/gameplay/feel/test_cover_bend.gd` (the nearest bodies handed to every batch, none without a table; a body walks only the covered cell it stands in, every tuft reads its own cell's walk, flat, held, springing back; a room laid again starts never walked; a state's trampled frames in its rows; 200 frames of 48 bodies through the grass make no object; Feel's step bends it), `tests/core/test_cover_layout.gd` (a trampled frame the sheet lacks is named); the screenshot strip `tests/screenshots/shot_cover_trample.gd` (a wizard walking through, then the grass springing back; the stand-in grass of `tests/support/stand_in_grass.gd` until GC9, the hub's own grass since). **Choices GC4 made** (the plan left them open):

- **A state says what its tufts draw trampled:** `CoverState.trampled_frames` (the art's trampled frames, GC8), each tuft picking one by the roll it picks its `frames` by; it rides in the row's colour (red: the frame + 1, / 255; 0 none), the one channel left, since the fragment draws the sheet's own colours. A tuft swaps to it while more than half flat; a state with none is pressed down instead (`FLATTEN`, half its height, by whole pixels). A state's `bend` scales the bend and the trample alike, so a state that does not bend (ash) is never trampled. `CoverKind.problems()` names a trampled frame its sheet lacks.
- **Which bodies:** every open actor with a body in the tree, props included (a rolling barrel bends it, as the plan says), the 32 nearest the middle of the party camera's picture, a tie going to the lower actor id (the order handed over is the order found); with no picture, the first 32 by id. A body walks the one cell its position is in, and only a covered one (since GC9 every covered cell under its `FOOTPRINT`, a box 12 px wide from 4 px behind its foot to 10 px in front, so the row in front, whose tufts stood over a one-cell trail, lies flat too); it bends the tufts round it within `BEND_REACH`.
- **The numbers are Feel's** (constants in `cover.gd`, handed to the shader once a batch, tuned at GC9): `BEND_REACH` 14 px, `BEND_PIXELS` 4, `FLATTEN` 0.5. How long a trampled cell lies flat and springs back is its kind's since GC9 (the owner's playtest, 2026-11-16: the grass sprang back far too soon): `CoverKind.trample_hold` and `trample_spring`, 0.6 s and 1.5 s by default (the moss's), the hub's grass 5 s and 4 s (its trail lies flat about 7 s and is gone after 9).
- **Nothing allocated a frame:** the bodies go to the shader only when they changed, into one of two arrays sized once, in turn, so the array the materials hold is never written (written in place, it would be copied); the grid's image is written only where walked and handed to its texture only on a frame that walked a cell. A room laid starts a new grid, never walked; a restore into the room shown keeps it (it is the view's).
- **The editor draws no bend or trample:** there are no bodies there; its tufts are the resting ones.

**Built (GC5):** the cover area. `gameplay/cover/cover.gd` (autoload `Cover`, after `Items`; `Tick.STEPS` "Cover" between the bodies and the items, `Tick.RUN_ORDER` after Combat) keeps each cell's state and seconds, the cells not at rest, the queued touches, the harmed bodies' timers and its roll counter; its layout comes from `room_built`, and `room_entered` alone resets it. `floor_touched` and `cover_changed` (core/events) are new; Spells sends `floor_touched` (`_touch_floor`, `_touch_end`; `CarrierKit.touch_floor` for the pool and the pulse), and Feel's cover hears `cover_changed` (`show_state`). Tests: `tests/gameplay/cover/test_cover.gd` (fire lights the cells of its circle, frost puts it out, burning to ash and the grass back after its 30 s, ash that never regrows, the same seed the same fire, a spread only onto cover its element moves on, a body burnt by Combat every half second and dropped when it leaves, a dust heap blown, `room_built` keeping the state and `room_entered` resetting it, a snapshot mid-fire restored and telling the view, a room with no cover keeping no state, a run's start), `tests/gameplay/cover/test_cover_rooms.gd` (on the real autoloads: a room entered starts at rest and Feel draws its fire; a snapshot mid-fire in the run's second room restored into a game in its first keeps the fire, and Feel draws it, and again into the room shown), `tests/core/test_floor_touched.gd` (a shot's end on a body and as it runs out, never in flight, none for no element, an event an element in order, a blast once with its radius, a pool as it lies and on its tick, a pulse's ring), `tests/core/test_snapshot_game.gd` (every room of a biome under a stand-in grass over every open cell; fire burns in at least one room's run), `tests/core/test_content_determinism.gd` (every fight on the test grass over the whole floor, `content_fight.gd`), `tests/core/test_events.gd`. **Choices GC5 made** (the plan left them open):

- **The exact reset path is `room_entered`** (Flow's `_announce_room`: a run's start and each room the party goes on into), never `room_built`: a restore sends only the latter. A room with no cover keeps its state empty (no packed arrays at all) and queues no touch.
- **A touch's circle:** the cell it lands in, and every cell whose middle is within its radius, in cell order; walls do not stop it (a pulse beside a thin wall may light the cover behind it: GC9 sees whether that shows).
- **Which carriers touch the floor:** a blast (with the blast's radius), a pulse's ring where it goes off (at its widest: where a returning ring turns home, else its end, with the ring's radius then), a pool as it lies and on each of its ticks (with its radius, where it lies then), and a shot's or a ray's end: a hit that spends it, its life running out, a wall, with its radius. **A shot a shield stops touches nothing, though rule 5 names `shot_blocked`:** that event names the blocker (its caster, place and tag, for its `on block`), not the shot it stops, so the stopped shot's elements are not known there; the stopped shot ends in the air at the shield. Giving it a touch would mean a new event from the projectile manager naming the stopped shot; left for the owner to ask for. A still shot's end is a shot's end too: a pool, a rune or a wall touches once more where it lies as its life runs out. A shot or a ring with a blast touches as its blast does, not twice. A beam, a swing, a slam and the other carriers touch only through a blast card.
- **A spread's chance:** `spread_chance` is a second's, so each tick rolls under `spread_chance × delta`; a cell rolls only for a neighbour its element would move on (so cover that ignores fire costs no roll), and the spreads rolled apply once every cell has stepped (a cell lit by a spread this tick spreads from the next; one lit by a touch steps and spreads in the tick it is lit, the touches coming first). A cell whose `after` trigger fires this tick does not spread in it.
- **A body's effect:** one timer a body (by actor id and key, in id order), whatever cover it moves between; the first at once, then every `status_every` while it stays in cover that gives a status; out of such cover, it is dropped (since GC8: once its time to the next is out, so stepping in and out is asked no oftener than standing in it). Asked by NO_PLAYER, so a burn from grass credits no one. `status_requested` now has two senders (Spells' portal, and this area), as rule 4 asks.
- **The view is told by `_said`** (derived): Feel, an earlier autoload, hears `room_built` first and lays at rest, then the area tells every cell not at rest; a restore into the room shown tells every cell the view was told otherwise (`rebuild_derived`); a room entered tells the cells that went back to rest. The arrays `cover_changed` hands over are reused, so a listener reads them while it is sent.
- **What allocates:** the harmed bodies are worked out in scratch sized for every actor once and written over in place, so a body standing in harmful cover makes no array; a tick that changes cells, rolls a spread or hears a touch still grows its packed lists (`cover_changed`'s arrays, the queue). GC6's benchmark measures it.
- **Going on into a room of the same size** tells the view the last room's cells against the new layout for a moment, inside Flow's one call, and `room_entered` tells them back to rest before anything is drawn; harmless, so left as it is.
- **Embers and smoke over burning cover are GC8's** (the plan's step table puts them with the art, content/motes): Feel draws only the tufts' states for now.

**Built (GC7):** the AI steers off harmful cover (rule 8). `cover_danger_looked(actor, at, reach, sight)` (core/events, new) and `CoverSight` (core/base/cover_sight.gd, new: a window of at most 9 cells a side round the place asked about, each marked where a body would be harmed; `harms`, `harms_cell`, `harms_along`), filled at once by the cover area (`Cover.look`). Tests: `tests/gameplay/ai/test_ai_cover.gd` (new: the look marks only the cells that harm, clipped to the room, through the bus; ash and a room with no cover mark nothing; a way harms through every cell it crosses, a corner too; an enemy walks round a burning strip to its target, never stepping in; through grass at rest it walks straight; an enemy steps out of burning grass with no target and before it fights; a bot steps out; ringed by fire it walks on), `tests/core/test_events.gd`. **Choices GC7 made** (the plan left them open):

- **What harms is what the area would ask a status for:** a cell whose state gives a status (burning grass, and cobwebs at rest: GC8's crypt), whatever the status and whoever the body; whether the body would resist it is Combat's and never asked (the cover area calls no area). So the AI walks round cobwebs too; GC8 and GC9 see whether that crowds the crypt's fights.
- **The look is a window, not a list:** the cells within the reach asked, at most 9 a side (64 px round), clipped to the room; outside it every cell reads safe. The area keeps a count of the cells in a state that harms (`_harming`, derived: counted from the state as a room is laid, entered or restored, and kept with each change; each kind's states' harm a table in `CoverMoves`) and answers at once, marking nothing, when it is 0, so a room with grass at rest, or only ash after a fire, costs a brain one ask a step (about half a microsecond); while something burns, a step's look is about 12 µs a walking brain (the review's measure), which GC9's playtest sees in a crowded fire.
- **Every step it walks, not only at a decision:** the brain moves between decisions (a reaction is a few tenths of a second), so it probes each step it has a move, every cell its move crosses to 24 px ahead (`HARM_PROBE`), past the cell it stands in (a corner clipped counts: a probe at points let a diagonal cut a burning cell's corner). The sight is scratch filled and read in the step (DERIVED), so a restore steers as the game it came from.
- **The turn:** to the nearest (by dot) of `AISight.DIRECTIONS` whose cells are clear of harm and whose line is clear of walls, at the same speed; once it turned, that side's ways come first (`HARM_SIDE_KEEP`) until its way ahead is clear again, so it walks round a fire one way and does not swing between two (it did, along a long strip). With no way free (ringed by fire) it walks on as it would: steering never stops a body.
- **Stepping out is `evade`'s:** standing in harmful cover, target or none, it wants out at 2.5 + 0.4 × caution (above the most a fight or a heal wants, a teammate's call and commitment included; the review found 1.2 lost to them), and the brain makes no random swap to its second choice then; a POINT plan to the middle of the nearest cell that does not harm with no wall between, within 64 px (`HARM_EXIT_LOOK`). Not urgent: a dash would carry it past an exit a cell or two off, farther than its steering looks. With no such cell in sight it does not want it, and the turn alone works on its move.
- **A goal standing in harmful cover is walked round, never reached:** a target in a burning patch is circled at its edge (a caster still casts at it from its band), and `pick up` walks round an item lying in fire. Left as it is for GC9's playtest to judge; a behaviour that gives up such a goal is a later change.
- **Every brain, no profile setting:** enemies, bosses, minions, possessing AIs and the test bots alike. A profile number for how much an AI minds the floor can come at GC9 if the playtest asks.

**The hub's biome (GC8):** the hub and the sandbox have no biome today, so no fill reaches them. GC8 sets the hub's `biome = &"hub"` (safe: a room with no `kinds` is offered for no run) and the hub's grass names `biomes: [hub]`; the sandbox stays bare.

**Built (GC8):** the art and placing. Three kinds in `content/cover/` (README new), each its folder (`.tres`, `tufts.grid.json` drawn to `tufts.svg` by `tools/pixel_grid.py`, `assets.json`, its own test through the real area: `tests/gameplay/cover/cover_kit.gd`): **`hub_grass`** (eleven frames: six at rest, two burning, ash, two trampled; `rest` −fire→ `burning` 1.2 s, burn 2 for 1.5 s every 0.5 s, spreads fire 0.6 a second → `ash` −30 s→ `rest`; `burning` −frost→ `rest`; fills the hub), **`crypt_moss`** (look only, decision 3: three cushions and a trampled one, one state; fills the crypt) and **`cobweb`** (a web a cell, two at rest and two burning; `rest` chills 30% for 0.45 s every 0.5 s −fire→ `burning` 0.5 s, spreads fire 1.0 a second → `gone`, drawing nothing, for good; `burning` −frost→ `rest`; fills the crypt sparsely). The hub's `biome = &"hub"`. Fields (`CoverField`, under a `Cover` node) and a `CoverPreview` in the hub (a meadow round the dummies; cleared: the stone, the altar and the lectern, the way from the spawns to the stone and from the joiners by the altar to it), the four arenas (two corners of cobwebs, clear of the spawns, and a patch of moss each) and the boss's arena (cobwebs in its four corners). Burning grass: embers (`ember`, Fire and Spark, 4 a second a cell) and smoke (`smoke`, new, Stone, 1.5); a burning web: embers, 6.

## Cost

A crypt arena is about 1,700 cells; the boss's arena 1,664 at 16 px. Only active cells are stepped; a body's cell is one lookup. Bodies standing in harmful cover are a handful. The hash of a resting room is a few packed arrays of zeros. The look is one batch a kind and one shader; the bend's cost goes with the bodies (at most 32), not the tufts. GC6 measures it: a benchmark (`tests/benchmark/cover_cost.gd`) of the largest room fully covered, all of it burning at once, with 100 bodies in it, a tick's cost and the frame's, against main. No native kernel unless that benchmark asks for one (then it mirrors the GDScript to the bit, as every kernel does).

**Built (GC6):** the benchmark, `tests/benchmark/cover_cost.gd` (with `cover_cost_runner.gd`; not run in the build, under a minute): the largest room (the boss's arena, 1,243 cells under a full stand-in grass), 100 bodies in it moving a little every tick, and four scenes, none, at rest, all of it burning and a fire's front, each printing a tick's cost (the Tick's `run`, and its steps one by one), the frame's (Feel's `step`, and its cover's alone), the Cover area's hash and, for the front, its rolls and its state's hash at its end (the seed fixed, so two checkouts that burn alike print the same). The numbers, main against GC6, are in docs/DECISIONS.md (2026-11-16): a tick in a covered room at rest 0.54 → 0.38 ms (the area's step 0.22 ms → 0.01), all of it burning 9.6 → 2.8 ms (the area's 9.2 → 1.8), a front 0.77 → 0.66 ms; the frame 0.34-0.61 ms throughout. Tests: `tests/gameplay/cover/test_cover_moves.gd` (new), `tests/gameplay/cover/test_cover.gd` (a self-naming row rolls nothing; cover harmful at rest harms with nothing burning; only a kind with cells in the room counts as harmful at rest). **Choices GC6 made** (the plan left them open):

- **The kinds' moves are tables** (`gameplay/cover/cover_moves.gd`, derived, worked out with the layout at `room_built`): each state's move on each element named anywhere, its `after` rows that would move it (to a state its kind has, other than its own) in order, its spread's element and chance. The two loops that may cover the whole room (the touches, the cells' step) read them directly; the rules are unchanged and the front burns the same as on main, roll for roll.
- **A spread rolls only for a neighbour its element moves to another state:** a row naming the state the cell is in, or a state its kind lacks (a kind problem, kept out of the registry), moves nothing, and is no longer rolled for (the README's rule; no kind so far has one, so no roll moved).
- **The bodies are looked at only while something can harm one:** a cell out of rest, a kind with cells in the room that harms at rest (cobwebs; the layout holds every kind given, so one with no cell there does not count) or a body still kept as harmed. The harmed bodies and their timers are as before.
- **No native kernel:** the worst tick (a whole room burning) is 1.8 ms of the area's after the tables, a real front a fraction of that. The tick a whole room catches in one touch stays a one-off spike (10-24 ms, Feel's tufts a third of it); a blast lights tens of cells.
- **Feel keeps each tuft's roll** from laying the room (`_slot_rolls`), so a cell's change rolls nothing again.
- **Not measured: the GPU** (the benchmark is headless); GC8's art and GC9's playtest on `/preview/` see it.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-11-15)

The owner took every recommendation ("I'll follow your recommendations for all of them").

1. **Fire lights cover only where it lands:** an elemental blast or pulse, a pool lying, a shot's end (rule 5). A shot in flight touches nothing: a flying shot lighting a line behind it every tick would set most of a room alight in any fight with fire, and cost a check a shot a tick.
2. **Regrowth is a kind's data** (its `after` row from `ash`): the hub's grass grows back in 30 s (the lobby is played for long); the crypt's cover never does within a room (a fight changes its room).
3. **The crypt's first cover is moss and cobwebs:** moss is look only (it sways, bends and is trampled; it does not burn); cobwebs play (whoever walks through them is chilled, the slow; fire burns them away fast). Bone dust and fungus may come later as data.
4. **Trampled is look only:** tufts flattened for a while where bodies walk, springing back, changing nothing in the game; so it is Feel's (rule 6), not a state of the cover area (first review, 2026-11-15). A trample that plays (a firebreak) is a row of data later.
5. **When:** the owner's exception to one milestone at a time (docs/ROADMAP.md), built beside the rest of the boss plan (run generation is done). Neither waits for the other; each link merges main into its branch when main moves.

The owner: "set it up as a chain. Each task should merge itself when done, and then start a new session for the next task."
