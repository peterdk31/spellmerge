# Actor

## Purpose

The shared body kit: one kind of body for players and enemies (actors G4, [docs/milestones/M2-actors.md](../../docs/milestones/M2-actors.md)).

An actor is a `Node2D` with `actor.gd` on its root (no physics body: online-ready N6) and components as children: Health, Hurtbox, Contact, Mover, Caster, Inventory, its looks and the controller that drives it. A player and an enemy differ only in which components they have and which controller drives them. Built in phase G of M2: the kit lands in G4, enemies move onto it in G6 and players in G7 (`content/wizard/base/wizard.tscn`, with the player's own components from `gameplay/player`).

## Contract

- **Public scripts:** every script in this folder (the layering exception below). Each has a `class_name`, so scenes and other areas use them by type.
- **`possession.gd` (`Possession`, actors G9):** the possess rule. An actor hears `possess_requested` for its own id and calls `Possession.begin(by, target, seconds)`: every hit possesses (owner, 2026-10-29), for the seconds times (1 - the target's possess resist, its `Stats`, boss plan OB7; a share of 1 is never possessed). The driver is the caster body's controller (its home controller while it stands empty; a puppet's leader); a body it drives, already possesses or calls home is skipped. Another driver's older possession of the target ends first. The driver keeps its possessions oldest first (`ActorController.possessions`) and drives the oldest (its own body stands with no controller, can be hit, keeps its home team); each other one is driven by a `PuppetController` (`puppet_controller.gd`) that copies the driver's intent each step. The target's own controller is put aside (`ActorController.set_aside(actor, seconds)`); team and seat follow the driver in the table (a puppet is no seat). It ends (`end`) when the time is up or the target dies or is down: the target gets its own controller back (`started`: an AI starts a fresh chase); a driver that was in it moves into its next oldest possession (`Possession.move_on`), else home, else waits behind a possession of its home body. The caster's body down or dead ends nothing: a home controller whose body dies while it possesses leaves the body and is freed when its last possession ends. Sends `possess_started`/`possess_ended(caster, actor)`, `caster` being the driver's home body. `PossessedLook` (an `ActorLook` the possession adds, owner 2026-10-28) draws, just above the character, the timer: a small ring like the player's revive ring with an arc for the time left in the driver's colour (`ramp_light` of its seat, else its home body's ramp); and beside it, over an actor a seat drives that is not its own body (`shows_bars`), its health (Fire) and mana (Spark) as two 16 px bars, outlined, on a Stone trough. A player an enemy's AI drives shows only the timer (its numbers are on its player's panel). `tests/screenshots/shot_possess.gd` shows both. No builder edits the target: a seat's builder edits its own body. Sound id for Feel: `possess` (not made yet; silent; docs/BACKLOG.md).
- **`actor.gd` (`Actor`):** identity (`id` in the actor table `core/actors`, opened when ready and on every re-entry, closed when it leaves the tree, and its home controller's seat as that seat's home body, `Actors.set_home`; `ramp`; `team()` and `seat()` from the controller driving it, else `own_team()`: its home controller's team, else `idle_team`, a prop's `Actors.TEAM_WORLD`). Components are found once in `_ready` (the first child of each kind; none is required) and reached as `stats`, `health`, `hurtbox`, `contact`, `mover`, `caster`, `inventory`, `footprint`, `state_triggers` (a prop's); a body whose scene has no `Stats`, `Footprint`, `Inventory` or `DamageNumbers` is given one. `home_controller` (the first `ActorController` child) and `controller` (the one driving now); `drive(controller)` hands the body to a guest (or to nobody with null) and takes that controller off the actor it drove before (`ActorController.driving`), so one controller never drives two bodies; `release()` gives it back, and each change updates the table's team and seat at once. `belongs_to` (the actor whose body this one belongs to, NONE for none; a decoy belongs to its caster: that actor's shots pass it, since its hurt circle is a projectile target owned by that actor, `Caster.own_hurtbox` and Combat), `possessed_by`, `add_look`/`remove_look` (a look made at run time), `is_leaving()`. Body points: `body_offset` (hurt circle and orbit anchor), `aim_pivot()` (`hand_offset`), `muzzle_position()` (`reach()` along the aim: the tip of the staff on show, `Caster.tip()`, or `staff_reach` when it names none; living staffs H3), `butt_position()` (the drawn staff's lower end, `Caster.butt()` behind the hand against the aim; the whole staff runs from there to the tip), `aim` (unit length). `is_still()` (props W2, docs/milestones/props.md: no controller drives it, it has no Mover and it keeps no time (a prop with a `timer` row, `PropStateTriggers.keeps_time`, is stepped, so its clock runs and its pools refill: the fountain); the Tick never steps it, and the actor table's `still` says so, set when its id opens and in `_tell_driver`), `rebuild_derived()` (after a snapshot gave the body its state back: each look's `sync()`, so a look that follows moments, a prop's, shows the state given back with no moment played; `ActorLook.sync` is a no-op in the base), `rest_leaf()` (the body as a few values for the state hash while it is still, whole and every component `at_rest()`; null otherwise). `step(delta)` in a fixed order: the intent's presses are spent, Health's protection counts down, `controller.think`, the aim follows the intent, `caster.step`, `mover.step`, `hurtbox.step`, the looks, then `home_controller.watch_home` while its home controller is away (it drives another actor, or another drives this one); The tick (core/sim/tick.gd, online-ready N3) steps every body by actor id through `tick_step`: `step`, then `move(delta)`, but never a still one (core/sim/README.md, "The tick"). `move(delta)`: the body, a circle of its Mover's `body_radius`, slides along the walls by `velocity` (a body with no Mover never moves, driven or not: a prop stands in its own footprint, props W4) (`RoomGrid.slide_body`, the room's and the solid shots' wall grid, the same to the bit on every machine), and `velocity` is left as what it moved by (along a wall, its slide; the AI's lead reads it). **In a room with holes** (hub rework HR-H1, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Holes"; `RoomGrid.has_holes`) the move is slid in two parts (`_move_over_holes`): first what crosses holes (the Mover's `crossing`: its dashes and what is done to it, a pull, a swap's own move, a recoil, a carry, Combat's push), against the walls and the props' ground alone, then its own walk, which also stops at a hole's lip; while something crosses and it does not walk, nothing more is slid, and while it walks with its circle already over a lip its walk is slid against the ground alone and dropped if it would set its centre over the hole (so a push slower than its radius a tick still carries it in, and a carry is never pinned at a lip); a walk from over a hole crosses too (not pushed back out of it, so a jump onto a hole, a teleport, a swap or a portal's exit, or a push into one keeps it there until HR-H2's fall). A room with no holes slides the whole velocity once, as before. Actors walk through each other; Combat's push parts them. A body has no physics callback: `ticking` says whether the tick steps it, so a test or another area that steps a body itself turns it off (`body.ticking = false`; Entities does for its decoys). `data` (an `EnemyBase`) sets health (times `stat_scale`: the room's scale, set by difficulty before the body is added, run generation RG5; 1 otherwise, state; and by the wizards in play, `players`, set by Flow before the body is added, `EnemyBase.health_for`, boss plan OB13: `count_players(count)` changes its most and its health by the same share as one joins or leaves), speed, contact damage, size (`set_size(factor)`: the node's scale, the hurt circle, body and hand; `size`), a boss's `phases` (`Phases`, above) and its 1–4 staffs (`EnemyBase.staffs`, one per button, each with its own pool) before the components are set up (no staff with a card: no caster). `is_down()`/`set_down(on)` (the Downed component, `gameplay/player`), `is_downed()` (the old name the AI and the bots still ask), `die(killer)` (the local `died`, then freed), `downable` (something else takes its 0 health: the Downed component, Entities' decoys, a prop's PropStateTriggers; Combat then neither frees it nor tells its death). Local signals `touched(by)` (boss plan OB5: a body that walks, `by`, overlaps its touch area, `Actors.set_touch_area`; told by that body at the end of its `move`, its circle against the walls at its feet asked of the table, `Actors.touching`, lowest id first, every tick it overlaps; a body down, dying or leaving touches nothing, and one spawning is touched by nothing), `status_gained(by, status)` and `reacted(by, reaction)` (props W6: Combat sends them with `status_applied` and `reaction_triggered` for the actor, a prop's triggers hear them), `damaged(by, amount, element)` and `healed(by, amount)`: Combat sends `damaged` with every `damage_dealt` that names the actor (`element` the hit's first, `&""` for a plain one; an invincible actor's too, though it takes nothing), and `healed` with what a heal gave it, for the actor's own components, looks and controller (the AI's grudge, the looks' hurt pose, the Face's worried brows, the damage numbers), so no actor listens to the bus for its hits. `caster_number()` and `open_caster_number()`/`close_caster_number()`: the actor's `id` while its caster is open in Spells (`Spells.open_caster`/`close_caster`; actors G3b). **Falls** (hub rework HR-H2, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Holes" 4 and 5): right after its move in a room with holes, a body that walks whose centre is over a hole and that crosses nothing (no share of the move crossing, no move of its Mover left, no local stop holding it) falls (`body_fell`): its moves end answered where it is, and for `HoleRules.FALL_SECONDS` (`fall_left`) it is down to everything that asks (`is_down`: its hurt shape is off, it casts nothing, holds no orbit or beam, touches nothing, is no target, revives nobody), though not downed (`is_downed`: the Downed component's own state, what a revive, the down looks and the exits ask). Its hurt shape off, its Combat row goes, so a fall clears its statuses and barrier as a down does. Then a wizard (`returns_from_falls`: a seat's own body) comes back on its last ground (`ground`, `has_ground`: where it last stood with no hole within `HoleRules.GROUND_MARGIN` of its circle; forgotten when it is `placed`, as Flow brings it into a room; with none, `nearest_ground`), pays its fall (`Combat.take_fall`), is protected for `HoleRules.RETURN_PROTECTION` and tells `body_returned`; anything else dies of it through Combat (`Combat.die_of_fall`, its killer `fell_by`, whoever last hit or pulled it as it fell; a `downable` body, a decoy, has its health emptied, `Health.empty`, so what owns it ends it). A boss never falls (`never_falls`): a move that leaves it over a hole puts it on the nearest ground. A body downed over a hole falls too and comes back still down. All of it is state; Feel draws it from `fall_share()`. **Spawning** (enemy spawning EA1, docs/milestones/enemy-spawning.md, "The simulation"): `spawn(seconds, style)`, set before the body is added to the tree (Flow's `_stand_enemy` hands it the `seconds` and id of the spawn style it picked, `SpawnStyle.pick`: content/spawn_styles, `rise`'s 0.8 today), makes it not there yet for `spawn_left` seconds (`spawn_seconds` the whole of it, `spawn_style` the style's id, state so every machine and a snapshot draw the same style, read by nothing in the simulation; `is_spawning()`, `spawn_share()` 0 to 1, and 1 while it stands): it is down to everything that asks (`is_down`, as a fall: its hurt shape is never registered, not even on its first tick, so it can't be hurt, targeted or possessed and its contact damage starts only once it has landed; not downed), it neither touches nor is touched (`_touch` skips a body that spawns in on both sides), and its `step` holds it: the timer counts down and the hurt shape follows `is_down`, then it returns before its phases, possession, controller, caster and mover (intent cleared, `velocity` zero; its looks still step). `tick_step` does not `move` it (no slide, no ground check: a spawn never turns into a fall). The tick its timer runs out it steps as a standing body (its hurt shape registers in that step). All of it is state; Feel draws it from `spawn_share()` (`SpawnLook`, gameplay/feel). Tests: `tests/gameplay/actor/test_spawn.gd`.
- **`phases.gd` (`Phases`, enemy AI AI7, docs/milestones/M2-enemy-ai.md):** a boss's phases. The actor adds it itself when its `data` has `phases` (`Actor.phases`; no scene of its own) and steps it after Health. Each step the next `EnemyPhase` whose condition holds (`health_below` a fraction of the most, or `after_seconds` since the body appeared) starts, once, in order (`begin(index)` starts one at once), but never while the body is possessed (boss plan OB7: a phase met during a possession starts when it ends, so the possessor never loses its staffs mid-cast): its `stats`, when it names some, become the body's (`Stats.apply`; null keeps the last; a new spell scale builds the staffs it keeps again, `Caster.rebuild`), the body is protected for its `transition` (`Health.protect`), its staffs replaced (`Caster.replace_staffs`, each pool full; a button it no longer has is emptied), its `PhaseLook` applied (the Face's `restyle(style, glow, row)`, `Actor.set_size`; the sprite by its look, which hears `started(index, phase)`), and the controller told (`ActorController.phase_started(actor, phase)`: the AI stands through the transition and takes the phase's profile). It follows the body whoever drives it. **Its adds** (boss plan OB13, `EnemyPhase.adds`, `EnemyAdds`): as a phase starts it asks the bus for each entry's burst (`adds_requested(actor, adds, key, false)`; `key` is `adds_key(phase, entry)`, for Flow's count under the entry's cap), and an entry with waves again every `every` seconds of the body's own steps while the phase lasts (`wave` true; `wave_left`), never while the body is possessed; the next phase's entries replace the last's waves. Flow stands them; nothing here knows the room. `list`, `current`, `elapsed`. Test: `tests/gameplay/ai/test_ai_phases.gd`.
- **`elite_look.gd` (`EliteLook`, connected biomes CB11, decision 24):** an elite's outline. An elite is an enemy whose `Actor.elite_card` is set (by Flow before it is added; `is_elite()`): its first staff holds the card in a slot more, in front of its own cards (`Caster.hold_staffs`, `Caster.extra_slots`, read by `slot_count(0)` and the compiler; new staffs from `replace_staffs` have no slot more), Difficulty gives it more health, and the actor adds this look itself in `_apply_data`, as it adds a boss's presence: a ring of whole pixels in the void's light round its "Sprite", on each clear pixel beside an opaque one (worked out once a texture frame and shared), coming in by three stages at 12 fps once it has spawned in. View only. (An elite's Inventory, made from its start cards, keeps its staff's own slots: an elite a wizard possesses and rebuilds loses the card; no enemy carries an Inventory today.)
- **`boss_presence.gd` (`BossPresence`, boss plan OB16, docs/milestones/boss-old-broom.md "HUD: the boss bar and the entrance"):** a boss in play, told to the HUD. A look the actor adds itself when its `data` is a boss's (`EnemyBase.boss`; in `_apply_data`, as it adds `Phases`); it draws nothing and is never stepped. Once the body is ready (its id open; a body a snapshot makes again has it already) and each time it comes back into the tree it sends `boss_shown` (its id, `EnemyBase.shown_name()`, `lore`, each phase's `health_below` largest first, and `has_intro`: whether its data has an intro, `EnemyBase.boss_intro`, docs/milestones/boss-intro.md), then `boss_health_changed`, and again on each `Health.changed`; `boss_hidden` once as it dies (`died`) or leaves the tree, under the id it was shown under. After a snapshot (`sync`) only its health is told again. View only.
- **`stats.gd` (`Stats`, boss plan OB7, docs/milestones/boss-old-broom.md, "Actor stats"):** the body's stats, its own copy of an `ActorStats` (core/base, read-only shared data): taken as it is set up (first of the components, so the Caster builds at its spell scale) from its enemy's `EnemyBase.stats`, else its prop's `PropBase.stats`, else its own `base` (the wizard's scene names `content/wizard/base/wizard_stats.tres`; null: the defaults, which change nothing), and again from a phase (`apply(from)`, which says whether the spell scale changed). Every actor has one: its scene's, or one the actor adds itself as it adds a `Footprint` (`actor.stats`). Never stepped; state (hashed, in snapshots; `taken` keeps a body made again from a snapshot from taking its data's again). `power` (Combat reads it at a spell hit from the caster's body by its actor id, only while the hit's build is the open caster's, `Spells.cast_by_open`: the hit, its slam and its burn; never contact damage, a plain hit or a heal), `mass` (Combat divides a shove by it: a hit's push, a pull), `spell_scale` (built into its staffs: `Caster` compiles at it, `Carrier.scale_size`), `resist` (a fixed array in Combat's status order, `Combat.STATUSES`, a freeze as a chill, then `POSSESS`; `resist_of(st)`: a status and a possession last (1 - share) as long). Relics and difficulty later change the copy, never the resource. `at_rest()` while its numbers are its data's. Test: `tests/gameplay/actor/test_actor_stats.gd`.
- **`footprint.gd` (`Footprint`, props W4, docs/milestones/props.md):** the ground a body stands on: a circle of `radius` (6 by default) or a box `size` across, `offset` from the feet. Every actor has one: its scene's (the wizard 6, a mite 5, a husk 7, a decoy its own), or one the actor adds itself in `_find_components`, as it adds `Phases` (`actor.footprint`). One rule, by whether the body can move (owner, 2026-11-11): a body that walks (a Mover) slides along the walls as its circle (`Actor.wall_radius`, `move`) and lands clear as it (a teleport); a body that cannot move (no Mover: a prop, a training dummy) is solid ground to the bodies that walk, laying its box or circle into the room grid's ground (`RoomGrid.set_footprint`, through `Projectiles.grid()`) while in the tree and `solid` (`set_solid(on)`: rubble, W6). Bodies that walk part softly (Combat's push). Shots, rays and sight never see it; the body is hit by its hurt shape, so a tree's canopy is hit and its trunk blocks. The footprint the actor adds under a box hurt shape is that box's base (`fit_base`: as wide, `BASE_SHARE` (half) as deep, its bottom edge on the box's). `scale_by(factor)` (`Actor.set_size`), `at_rest()` always (what is laid is the manager's state).
- **`prop_state_triggers.gd` (`PropStateTriggers`, props W6, docs/milestones/props.md, "Triggers"):** a prop's states and their triggers (`PropBase`, `PropState`, `PropTrigger` in core/base; content/props/README.md). It holds `states` (a `PropStates`, the prop's `PropBase.states`: all it reads of the prop, so any actor could hold one) and `state` (an index into it, the first to start; `current()`, `state_id()`), both state (hashed, in snapshots), with `clock` (seconds in its state, counted only while that state has a `timer` row); it is stepped only when it keeps time (`keeps_time()`: a `timer` row in any state; `step(delta)`, called by `Actor.step` before the caster's, tells `timer` as its clock passes each whole number of a row's `seconds`, and entering a state sets the clock to 0), and otherwise hears its own body's moments, a hit (`damaged`: `element_hit`), a status (`status_gained`), a reaction (`reacted`), its health falling below a share (`Health.changed`: `health_below`, the hit that crosses it) or running out (`Health.depleted`: `death`), one of its casts leaving (`cast_left` on the bus, only when its data has a `cast` trigger) a state entered (`entered`) and a body that walks overlapping its `area` (`Actor.touched`: `touched`, boss plan OB5; its area round its feet is laid in the actor table, `Actors.set_touch_area`, only while its state has a `touched` row, and taken away as it leaves that state or the tree). One hit is `health_below`, `death`, then `element_hit`, each read against the state it finds. On a moment every trigger of the current state that matches fires in order (`cast`: `Caster.cast(at, false, true)`, the prop's own cast, never a press, and never of a staff whose cast is leaving now), and the first `then` among them is entered after they all fired; a moment inside one (a trigger's cast whose blast sets this prop off again) is read against the state then, and once it moved the state on, the outer moment stops and its `then` is dropped. Entering a state ends the wind-ups the moment did not cast (`Caster.cancel_wind`), sets the hurt shape and footprint from `hittable` and `blocks` (`Hurtbox.set_enabled`, `Footprint.set_solid`), sends `prop_state_changed` and its local `entered(state_id)`, fires its `entered` triggers, and frees the body for a `gone` state still in. It takes the body's 0 health (`downable`): its `death` triggers run and a possession of it ends; without a `gone` state it stands. Moments more than `MAX_DEPTH` deep are dropped with a warning (a loop `PropStates.problems` names). `enter(state_id)` enters a state from outside its triggers, as a `then` would (the hub's stone, W8; tests). At rest in its first state. Test: `tests/gameplay/actor/test_prop_state_triggers.gd` (the exploding barrel in all its paths, a chain, several triggers on one hit, `gone`, invincible, each trigger, the hash and a snapshot).
- **`prop_look.gd` (`PropLook`, props W7, docs/milestones/props.md, "W7: the look"):** a prop's look, an `ActorLook` (VIEW_ONLY) on the shared prop body: the look of the state the body is in (`Actor.prop.look_of`), again as its `PropStateTriggers` enters a state (`entered`, its cue played on the bus, `cue_shown`; a state of the same look plays the cue and keeps what is shown) or a snapshot gives one back (`sync`, no cue). Its first layer is the actor's own `Sprite` (Feel flashes a hit body's `Sprite`), the rest sprites of its own (`Layer<i>`, among the bodies, over them, or hidden), each frame's bottom middle on the feet; a node in the group `mote_source` for each of a layer's `MoteSource`s (`MoteSource.put`, `mote_art` its layer's sprite by the path the tree gives), and a marker in the group `light_spot` for its light (`LightSpot.mark`), so Feel gives off and lights them with nothing more. A still prop is never stepped, so a layer of frames plays on the look's own `_process` (only while one does; the rule in `ActorLook`). Test: `tests/gameplay/actor/test_prop_look.gd`.
- **`prop_body.gd` (`PropBody`, props W6):** `make(data)`: a prop's body, its data's `scene` (the shared content/props/base/prop.tscn) filled in from its `PropBase` before it is added (`fill(body, data)`: the body's `prop` (its data, state: content kept as itself, so a body made again from a snapshot has it and its look), Health's most and `invincible`, the hurt shape and the body's points, the footprint, else its hurt box's base, the staffs, `Caster.hold_staffs`, and the PropStateTriggers' `states` and `area`, `PropBase.area_rect`). Nothing runs for a body made again from a snapshot: the scene and its saved state are the body.
- **`actor_intent.gd` (`ActorIntent`):** what a controller wants this step: `move` (length ≤ 1), `aim` (zero keeps the aim), per staff `press(staff)`/`is_pressed` (this step only) and `hold(staff, on)`/`is_held`, kept as two bit masks (`pressed_bits`, `held_bits`, read only); `copy_from(other)` (a puppet). Made once per actor.
- **`actor_controller.gd` (`ActorController`):** `team`, `seat` (-1 for none), `think(actor, delta)` (writes `actor.intent`, nothing else), `started(actor)`, `stopped(actor)`, `set_aside(actor, seconds)` (a possession took its actor), `watch_home(home, delta)` (each step of its home actor while it does not drive it; read only: the seat's panel), `is_telegraphing()` (winding up a committed cast: the AI's windup; the base never does), `telegraph_staff()` (the staff it winds up; 0 in the base), `phase_started(actor, phase)` (a boss's phase began; the base does nothing); for possess, `home_actor`, `possessions` (oldest first), `possesses(body)`, `leader()` (itself; a puppet's leader) and `end_possessions()`.
- **`puppet_controller.gd` (`PuppetController`):** drives a body its `leader_controller` possesses but does not drive: each step it copies the intent of the actor the leader drives (nothing while the leader drives nothing). Its team is the leader's, it is no seat, and `leader()` is the leader, so a puppet's possess hits are the leader's.
- **`actor_component.gd` (`ActorComponent`):** the components' base: `actor`, `setup(actor)` once, `attach()` on entering the tree, `detach()` on leaving, `at_rest()` (as its scene and data made it, but for what the actor's `rest_leaf` holds; false in the base, so a component that does not say is always hashed whole: Health full and unprotected, Hurtbox registered, Contact always, Caster its start cards unedited and every pool full).
- **`health.gd` (`Health`):** `max_health`, `health`, `take(amount, killer)`, `heal(amount)`, `reset(amount)`, `rescale(most)` (a new most, the health keeping its share: boss plan OB13), `protect(seconds)`, `is_protected()`, `is_depleted()`, `protecting` (true while a protection counts down: the actor steps Health only then); local signals `changed(health, max_health)` and `depleted(killer)`. Combat calls it (G5).
- **`hurtbox.gd` (`Hurtbox`):** `radius`, `offset`, and `size` for a box in place of the circle (props: a bench, a big boss; `HitShape`, core/projectiles/hit_shape.gd: every hit test takes it, touch too); `half()`, `is_box()`, `outer_radius()` (to its corner: what asks how big the body is, the AI's reach); registers the actor with `Combat.register_actor` (health, shape, Contact's damage) on attach, `unregister_actor` on detach and while the actor is down or the hurtbox is not `enabled` (`set_enabled(on)`: a prop's rubble, props W6, registered or forgotten at once); `is_on()`, `registered` (the actor steps the hurtbox only when this is not what its down state and `enabled` want).
- **`contact.gd` (`Contact`):** `damage`, handed to Combat by the Hurtbox.
- **`mover.gd` (`Mover`):** `speed` (the body's circle is its `Footprint`'s); walking (`speed` × the intent's move × Combat's pace) plus Combat's push, asked by the actor's own id (`Combat.own_pace`, `Combat.own_knockback`), and the caster kit, one implementation for every actor: dash, teleport, pull, swap, recoil from `caster_move_requested` (each with its own way and distance) whose tag's caster is the actor's caster number (unless the event names a body that belongs to the actor: its decoy moves instead), or, for a body that belongs to an actor (a decoy), its owner's card when the event names it (a payload the decoy's body fired; owner, 2026-11-06), all adding up in one list of moves (rules R4 F1: a teleport at once, a dash instead of the walk and slowed by statuses, the others on top); `caster_move_finished` for every move; `target_move_requested` naming the actor moves it at once. A dash goes at its cards' speed curve (`CarrierDash.move_curve`, `MoveCurve`: its whole way in its time) and ends where the body is held up head on (`held_up`, told by `Actor.move` when the slide changed the move: a step that keeps under `HEAD_ON_SHARE` of the way the dash asked along its own way, under about 45 degrees from straight in; walls, prop ground and the Wall card's stones alike; a glancing one slides on; boss plan OB10). The kit may use `core/`, Combat's status order (`Combat.STATUSES`, `STATUS_COUNT`: `Stats`' resist array) and the Combat autoload's actor API (`register_actor`, `unregister_actor`, `pace`, `consume_knockback`, `own_pace`, `own_knockback`). A step runs for every actor every frame, and a GDScript call costs more than a few field reads, so each component is called only when it has something to do and sends the systems only what changed (performance pass, 2026-10-28, docs/DECISIONS.md).

## Allowed files

`.gd`, plus `.uid` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. The text shared by all sub-areas is in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Steps

Follow the steps and test layout in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md). A new kind of component extends `ActorComponent` (or `ActorLook` for a look) and is found by the actor as a child; the actor steps only the kinds it names, so a component that needs a step is added to `Actor.step` in its place in the order.

## Review questions

Besides the general review:

- Does it hold for every actor, player and enemy alike, by one rule? Does nothing assume an actor id is a seat?
- Does a step stay free of allocations, with 200 actors in a room?
- Do components reach each other only through the actor, never by node path?
