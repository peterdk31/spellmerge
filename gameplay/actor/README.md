# Actor

## Purpose

The shared body kit: one kind of body for players and enemies (actors G4, [docs/milestones/M2-actors.md](../../docs/milestones/M2-actors.md)).

An actor is a `CharacterBody2D` with `actor.gd` on its root and components as children: Health, Hurtbox, Contact, Mover, Caster, its looks and the controller that drives it. A player and an enemy differ only in which components they have and which controller drives them. Built in phase G of M2: the kit lands in G4, enemies move onto it in G6 and players in G7 (`content/wizard/base/wizard.tscn`, with the player's own components from `gameplay/player`).

## Contract

- **Public scripts:** every script in this folder (the layering exception below). Each has a `class_name`, so scenes and other areas use them by type.
- **`possession.gd` (`Possession`, actors G9):** the possess rule. An actor hears `possess_requested` for its own id and calls `Possession.begin(by, target, seconds)`: every hit possesses (owner, 2026-10-29). The driver is the caster body's controller (its home controller while it stands empty; a puppet's leader); a body it drives, already possesses or calls home is skipped. Another driver's older possession of the target ends first. The driver keeps its possessions oldest first (`ActorController.possessions`) and drives the oldest (its own body stands with no controller, can be hit, keeps its home team); each other one is driven by a `PuppetController` (`puppet_controller.gd`) that copies the driver's intent each step. The target's own controller is put aside (`ActorController.set_aside(actor, seconds)`); team and seat follow the driver in the table (a puppet is no seat). It ends (`end`) when the time is up or the target dies or is down: the target gets its own controller back (`started`: an AI starts a fresh chase); a driver that was in it moves into its next oldest possession (`Possession.move_on`), else home, else waits behind a possession of its home body. The caster's body down or dead ends nothing: a home controller whose body dies while it possesses leaves the body and is freed when its last possession ends. Sends `possess_started`/`possess_ended(caster, actor)`, `caster` being the driver's home body. `PossessedLook` (an `ActorLook` the possession adds, owner 2026-10-28) draws, just above the character, the timer: a small ring like the player's revive ring with an arc for the time left in the driver's colour (`ramp_light` of its seat, else its home body's ramp); and beside it, over an actor a seat drives that is not its own body (`shows_bars`), its health (Fire) and mana (Spark) as two 16 px bars, outlined, on a Stone trough. A player an enemy's AI drives shows only the timer (its numbers are on its player's panel). `tests/screenshots/shot_possess.gd` shows both. No builder edits the target: a seat's builder edits its own body. Sound id for Feel: `possess` (not made yet; silent; docs/BACKLOG.md).
- **`actor.gd` (`Actor`):** identity (`id` in the actor table `core/actors`, opened when ready and on every re-entry, closed when it leaves the tree, and its home controller's seat as that seat's home body, `Actors.set_home`; `ramp`; `team()` and `seat()` from the controller driving it, `idle_team` with none). Components are found once in `_ready` (the first child of each kind; none is required) and reached as `health`, `hurtbox`, `contact`, `mover`, `caster`. `home_controller` (the first `ActorController` child) and `controller` (the one driving now); `drive(controller)` hands the body to a guest (or to nobody with null) and takes that controller off the actor it drove before (`ActorController.driving`), so one controller never drives two bodies; `release()` gives it back, and each change updates the table's team and seat at once. `possessed_by`, `add_look`/`remove_look` (a look made at run time), `is_leaving()`. Body points: `body_offset` (hurt circle and orbit anchor), `aim_pivot()` (`hand_offset`), `muzzle_position()` (`reach()` along the aim: the tip of the staff on show, `Caster.tip()`, or `staff_reach` when it names none; living staffs H3), `aim` (unit length). `step(delta)` in a fixed order: the intent's presses are spent, Health's protection counts down, `controller.think`, the aim follows the intent, `caster.step`, `mover.step`, `hurtbox.step`, the looks, then `home_controller.watch_home` while its home controller is away (it drives another actor, or another drives this one); `_physics_process` then calls `move_and_slide()`. `data` (an `EnemyBase`) sets health, speed, contact damage, size (`set_size(factor)`: the node's scale, the hurt circle, body and hand; `size`), a boss's `phases` (`Phases`, above) and its 1–4 staffs (`EnemyBase.staffs`, one per button, each with its own pool) before the components are set up (no staff with a card: no caster). `is_down()`/`set_down(on)` (the Downed component, `gameplay/player`), `is_downed()` (the old name the AI and the bots still ask), `die(killer)` (the local `died`, then freed), `downable`. Local signal `damaged(by, amount)`: Combat sends it with every `damage_dealt` that names the actor, for the actor's own components and controller (the AI's grudge, the looks' hurt pose, the Face's worried brows), so no actor listens to the bus for its hits. `caster_number()` and `open_caster_number()`/`close_caster_number()`: the actor's `id` while its caster is open in Spells (`Spells.open_caster`/`close_caster`; actors G3b).
- **`phases.gd` (`Phases`, enemy AI AI7, docs/milestones/M2-enemy-ai.md):** a boss's phases. The actor adds it itself when its `data` has `phases` (`Actor.phases`; no scene of its own) and steps it after Health. Each step the next `EnemyPhase` whose condition holds (`health_below` a fraction of the most, or `after_seconds` since the body appeared) starts, once, in order (`begin(index)` starts one at once): the body is protected for its `transition` (`Health.protect`), its staffs replaced (`Caster.replace_staffs`, each pool full; a button it no longer has is emptied), its `PhaseLook` applied (the Face's `restyle(style, glow)`, `Actor.set_size`; the sprite by its look, which hears `started(index, phase)`), and the controller told (`ActorController.phase_started(actor, phase)`: the AI stands through the transition and takes the phase's profile). It follows the body whoever drives it. `list`, `current`, `elapsed`. Test: `tests/gameplay/ai/test_ai_phases.gd`.
- **`actor_intent.gd` (`ActorIntent`):** what a controller wants this step: `move` (length ≤ 1), `aim` (zero keeps the aim), per staff `press(staff)`/`is_pressed` (this step only) and `hold(staff, on)`/`is_held`, kept as two bit masks (`pressed_bits`, `held_bits`, read only); `copy_from(other)` (a puppet). Made once per actor.
- **`actor_controller.gd` (`ActorController`):** `team`, `seat` (-1 for none), `think(actor, delta)` (writes `actor.intent`, nothing else), `started(actor)`, `stopped(actor)`, `set_aside(actor, seconds)` (a possession took its actor), `watch_home(home, delta)` (each step of its home actor while it does not drive it; read only: the seat's panel), `is_telegraphing()` (winding up a committed cast: the AI's windup; the base never does), `telegraph_staff()` (the staff it winds up; 0 in the base), `phase_started(actor, phase)` (a boss's phase began; the base does nothing); for possess, `home_actor`, `possessions` (oldest first), `possesses(body)`, `leader()` (itself; a puppet's leader) and `end_possessions()`.
- **`puppet_controller.gd` (`PuppetController`):** drives a body its `leader_controller` possesses but does not drive: each step it copies the intent of the actor the leader drives (nothing while the leader drives nothing). Its team is the leader's, it is no seat, and `leader()` is the leader, so a puppet's possess hits are the leader's.
- **`actor_component.gd` (`ActorComponent`):** the components' base: `actor`, `setup(actor)` once, `attach()` on entering the tree, `detach()` on leaving.
- **`health.gd` (`Health`):** `max_health`, `health`, `take(amount, killer)`, `heal(amount)`, `reset(amount)`, `protect(seconds)`, `is_protected()`, `is_depleted()`, `protecting` (true while a protection counts down: the actor steps Health only then); local signals `changed(health, max_health)` and `depleted(killer)`. Combat calls it (G5).
- **`hurtbox.gd` (`Hurtbox`):** `radius`, `offset`; registers the actor with `Combat.register_actor` (health, circle, Contact's damage) on attach, `unregister_actor` on detach and while the actor is down; `is_on()`, `registered` (the actor steps the hurtbox only when this equals its down state).
- **`contact.gd` (`Contact`):** `damage`, handed to Combat by the Hurtbox.
- **`mover.gd` (`Mover`):** `speed`, `body_radius`; walking (`speed` × the intent's move × Combat's pace) plus Combat's push, asked by the actor's own id (`Combat.own_pace`, `Combat.own_knockback`), and the caster kit, one implementation for every actor: dash, teleport, pull, swap, recoil from `caster_move_requested` (each with its own way and distance) whose tag's caster is the actor's caster number, all adding up in one list of moves (rules R4 F1: a teleport at once, a dash instead of the walk and slowed by statuses, the others on top); `caster_move_finished` for every move; `target_move_requested` naming the actor moves it at once. `is_moving()`, `move_direction()`, `stop()`. The rules are in docs/DECISIONS.md ("The actor kit").
- **`caster.gd` (`Caster`):** `staff_count` (1-4), `staffs` (a `StaffBase` per button, living staffs H3: a player's four heads) and `staff` (for a button without one), `staff_of(at)`, `current` (the staff on show: pressed or held last, an empty one too, set before it casts; a change sends `staff_shown`, the poof) and `tip()` (its `StaffBase.tip`), `start_cards`, `number`; one mana pool per staff (owner, 2026-11-01), sized by its staff (`StaffBase.max_mana`, `mana_regen`) and full when set up: `mana_of(at)`, `max_mana_of(at)`, `set_mana(at, value)`; `cast(staff)`, `set_staff(staff, cards)`, `replace_staffs(staffs, cards)` (a boss's phase: 1-4 new staffs, pools full, the rest emptied), `cards_of`, `slot_count(at)`, `plan_of`, `last_form`, `has_staff`, `is_holding`, `free_mana(at)` (that staff's pool less what its sustained cards hold), `next_cast_mana`/`_reach`/`_radius`/`_sense` (`CastSense`: what the next cast does, for the AI), `cast_ready(within, staff)`; local signals `cast_done(staff, origin, direction)` and `staff_built(staff)`; after every cast it sends `actor_cast(id, actor)` on the bus (Feel's squash, for every actor). Each step: mana back into every staff's pool (its staff's regen; siphon's `mana_refund_requested` for its actor id and that staff), the anchor and aim to the projectile manager and Spells when the body moved, turned or showed another staff (`Projectiles.place_caster`, `Spells.set_aim`; sent again before each cast), the casts the intent asks for (a press, or a hold while no sustained card is on). Compiled staffs are cached for every caster by Spells, staff and cards.
- **`actor_look.gd` (`ActorLook`):** a look's base (a `Node2D`): `setup(actor)`, `step(delta)`, read only. It also draws the actor's staff animation (its carrier card's `staff_pose`, played by `Spells.poses().staff_pose` and the rest) the same way for every look: `pose_offset(time)` (lift, push along the aim, shake, whole pixels: the look adds it to the body's place), `pose_scale()` (the stretch along the aim), and `apply_pose(sprite, face, keep_scale)`: the stretch (unless another look owns the scale, a dash), the glow (the shots' sprite shader keeping the sprite's colours, light round it in the infusion's colour else the ramp's; it never takes the sprite from Feel's hit flash) and the face's mood. The turn is the look's own (StaffLook's head columns; a creature has none).
- **`face.gd` (`Face`, living staffs H2, [docs/milestones/M2-living-staffs.md](../../docs/milestones/M2-living-staffs.md)):** the face every living staff wears, an `ActorLook` with two sprite layers it makes itself, the eyes (`art/faces/eyes.svg`) and the brows (`art/faces/brows.svg`), placed at `anchor` (the left eye's top-left pixel at rest). Inputs: `style` (`CHEER`, `EAGER`, `SLEEPY`, `STERN` for the four personalities; `STRAY`, `STRAY_ONE` for strays), `resting_mood` (`NEUTRAL`, `RAISED`, `ANGRY`, `FURIOUS`, `WORRIED`), `glow` (the eyes' tint: a stray's element colour), `body_offset` (the body's bob or hop this step, set by the body's look, which steps before the face). The pupils look one of nine ways from the actor's aim (`look()`). `mood()`, first that holds: down (worried, eyes shut), the controller `is_telegraphing` (furious), `show_mood(mood, seconds)` (a hit on the actor, its `damaged`, shows worried for `HURT_TIME`), an idle raised brow, `resting_mood`. The eyes trail the body by `LAG` and the brows trail the eyes, at most `MAX_LAG` px each, not over a jump of more than `SNAP` (`eye_offset()`, `brow_offset()`); the Caster's `cast_done` pops them up (`POP_EYES`, `POP_BROWS` more). Idle habits per style, seeded per actor: blinks always (`is_shut()`), glances (`is_glancing()`) and raised brows only after `IDLE_AFTER` seconds standing still; strays only blink. A step sets a layer's frame or place only when it changed. No actor wears it yet: the floating staff (H3) and the strays (H5) put it on.
- **Services:** `events`, `combat`, `projectiles`, `spells`, `actors` on the actor; tests set them before adding it, otherwise the autoloads are found by node path when it enters the tree.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. The kit may use `core/` and the Combat autoload's actor API (`register_actor`, `unregister_actor`, `pace`, `consume_knockback`, `own_pace`, `own_knockback`). It is the one stated exception to "areas never call each other directly": every area may use the kit's public scripts. `gameplay/player` and `gameplay/ai` build on it (their controllers, Downed, the looks), and `content/` scenes put the components together.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: `caster_move_finished` (Mover), `staff_shown` (Caster: another staff on show, before its cast).
  - Listens to: `caster_move_requested`, `target_move_requested` (Mover), `mana_refund_requested` (Caster). Hits come as the actor's own `damaged`, not from the bus.
- **Performance:** no process callback but the actor's; nothing allocates in `step` (the intent is made once, the recoils are packed arrays). A step runs for every actor every frame, and a GDScript call costs more than a few field reads, so each component is called only when it has something to do and sends the systems only what changed (performance pass, 2026-10-28, docs/DECISIONS.md).

## Allowed files

`.gd`, plus `.uid` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. The text shared by all sub-areas is in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Steps

Follow the steps and test layout in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md). A new kind of component extends `ActorComponent` (or `ActorLook` for a look) and is found by the actor as a child; the actor steps only the kinds it names, so a component that needs a step is added to `Actor.step` in its place in the order.

## Example to copy

`tests/gameplay/actor/actor_fixture.gd` puts actors together in code from the components, with a scripted controller (`Scripted`), its own bus, projectile manager, Spells, Combat and actor table, and steps them by hand. ## Review questions

Besides the general review:

- Does it hold for every actor, player and enemy alike, by one rule? Does nothing assume an actor id is a seat?
- Does a step stay free of allocations, with 200 actors in a room?
- Do components reach each other only through the actor, never by node path?
