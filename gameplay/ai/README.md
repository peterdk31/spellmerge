# AI

## Purpose

Shared enemy behaviours: one AI that plays whatever staffs an actor holds (docs/milestones/M2-enemy-ai.md), steering, targeting and the looks.

Built across M1 to M3 (the plan gives the range, not one milestone; see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Enemies in `content/enemies/` pick and configure behaviours from here instead of writing their own: an enemy is an actor (gameplay/actor) driven by the AI here and drawn by a look from here. Targeting chooses among every actor of another team, never "the player". The decoys and familiars of the staffs' cards live here too (staffs B5).

## Contract

- **Public entry scripts:** `gameplay/ai/ai.gd` (`AIController`), `brain.gd` (`AIBrain`), `behaviour.gd` (`AIBehaviour`), `behaviours.gd` (`AIBehaviours`), `team_board.gd` (`TeamBoard`), `sight.gd` (`AISight`), `creature_look.gd` (`CreatureLook`), `dummy_look.gd` (`DummyLook`), `decoy_controller.gd` (`DecoyController`), `decoy_look.gd` (`DecoyLook`) and the autoload `Entities` (`gameplay/ai/entities.gd`). Other code uses only these and the events they send and listen to; nothing else in the folder is public.
- **Enemies are actors (actors G6, docs/milestones/M2-actors.md):** an enemy scene's root is an `Actor` (gameplay/actor) with the kit's components: Health, Hurtbox (an imp's 11 px at (0, -12)), Contact, Mover and Caster, which the actor fills from its `data` (the enemy's `EnemyBase`: health, walking speed, contact damage, staff, cards, mana); the spawner sets `data` before adding it. Every enemy body has an actor id (the enemies' team, the enemy ramp, no seat) that owns its hurt circle, closed when it leaves the tree. The body walks, casts, is hurt, pushed and slowed by its components; this area only decides and draws.
- **`AISight` (`sight.gd`, owner 2026-11-01):** what an AI sees of walls, shared by the enemy AI and the test bots: `line_clear(projectiles, from, to)` (no room wall and no Wall card's solid shot on the segment: the projectile manager's wall grid, which shots stop on; `RoomGrid.line_clear`, through `Projectiles.grid()`, walks the whole segment), `detour(projectiles, from, goal, probe)` (of eight ways whose first `probe` pixels are free, the one that brings `goal` into view, else the free one ending nearest it) and `cover(projectiles, from, threat, probe)` (of the eight free ways, one whose end has a wall between it and `threat`, the farthest from it; ZERO when none is near). Static, nothing allocated. It asks nothing about what a wall is or who made it, and nothing about what an AI's own staffs cast (owner: which staff to fire needs more design). **Blocking props' ground** (boss plan OB14, decision 36: the OB8 broom ground into pillars): `ground_clear(projectiles, from, to)` (no ground of a body that cannot move, a pillar's footprint, `RoomGrid.ground_clear`, on the segment: feet to feet) and `walk_clear` (that and `line_clear`: a walk in a line); `detour` and `cover` probe their ways with `walk_clear` and see the goal (or the threat) along it, so they go round a pillar as round a wall, and a pillar is cover. **The run-up** (OB14): `run_along(projectiles, from, way, most, radius)` (how far a body of `radius` runs along `way` before its rim meets a wall or ground, at most `most`; `Projectiles.clear_run`) and `run_up_spot(projectiles, from, target, reach, radius)` (of `RUN_UP_WAYS` (16) ways round the target, the start back along each at the foot of what stands behind it, no farther than `RUN_UP_NEAR` (0.7) of the reach, where the body can stand (`circle_blocked`); scored by how long the run from there through the target goes, less `RUN_UP_WALK_COST` (0.5) a pixel walked to it, twice that when it cannot be walked to in a line; INF for none). **Holes** (hub rework HR-H2, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Holes" 6): two checks kept apart. Walking sees a room's holes as a pillar's ground: `walk_clear` (`Projectiles.walk_clear`: no ground and no hole, and no wall), `feet_clear` (the same without the walls: the brain's `ground_clear` and its walk to a point), `detour`, and the run-up's start (`circle_blocked` with holes on), so an AI walks round a crack and never into one. The line of fire and cover do not: `line_clear`, `ground_clear` and `shot_clear` (a wall or a pillar between: what `cover` looks for between its end and the threat), so it shoots across a crack and never hides behind one. With no pathfinder, a pit beside a pillar or against a room's edge, or a long low wall straight between an enemy and a wizard (a mite behind the middle of a wall longer than about twice `DETOUR_PROBE` turns back and forth there for good; a mite starting level with a pit's top or bottom lip, its target across the pit, is held at that lip, CR6), is where steering could pin an enemy, so every room template is played spawn by spawn (crypt rework rule 1, CR5 and CR6, docs/milestones/crypt-rework.md): a crypt mite from each enemy spawn must come within its reach of a wizard at each player spawn, and in a room with holes at each open spot round its pits, in ten seconds (or 1.6 times its straight walk for a far target), in the room's walls, holes and props, never falling. **The line of fire minds a prop's body** (2026-11-18: enemies fired into a pillar a wizard hid behind): a shot is spent on any hit shape of the world's, and a pillar's is its tall body (22 × 52), not its ground (26 × 12), so a shot level with the pillar's middle hits it though the feet-to-feet line is clear. `fire_clear(projectiles, from, to)` (`line_clear` and `Projectiles.props_clear`: no world hit shape on the segment, body to body) is what the brain's `line_clear` (as wide as its widest aimed shot, at most `FIRE_WIDTH_MOST`, 8) and its heal's line to a teammate ask; when it is not clear, `flank` (an aimed staff in reach) repositions by `flank(projectiles, from, goal, aim, eye, probe, width)` (of the eight free ways, one from whose end, its body `eye` above the feet, a shot `width` either side reaches `aim` and no ground lies on to `goal`, the one keeping its distance to `aim` most nearly; then twice as far, `FLANK_REACH`; ZERO for none), else by `detour`. Walking still minds only a prop's ground. Tests: `tests/gameplay/ai/test_ai_sight.gd`, `test_ai_run_up.gd`, `test_ai_holes.gd`, `test_ai_pit_rooms.gd`, `test_ai_prop_cover.gd`.
- **`AIController` (`ai.gd`)** is the enemy's home controller (an `ActorController` child of the actor): it writes the actor's intent and nothing else, by its `brain` (an `AIBrain`). Its profile is `profile` when set, else its home actor's data's (`EnemyBase.profile_or_fallback()`). It hears the driven actor's own `Actor.damaged` (grudges, guarding) and its Caster's `staff_built` (decide again at once); nothing listens on the bus per enemy. `state`, `target`, `grudge`, `grudge_left()`, `lookup_count`, `windup_time` and `recover_time` (the attack under way, else the next one of its telegraph staff), `is_telegraphing()`, `telegraph_progress()`, `telegraph_staff()`; `State` is `AIBrain.State`, and `RETARGET_TIME`, `GRUDGE_TIME`, `SIGHT`, `CLOSE_ENOUGH` name the brain's for the tests. A minion needs no code of its own: an `AIController` of the players' team handed the actor (`Actor.drive`) makes it fight the other enemies, with no windup, as an ally (`test_ai_staff.gd`); an enemy's AI that possesses a wizard plays the wizard's four staffs. `started(actor)` starts a fresh chase and `stopped(actor)` forgets it. Its team board is `Entities.board`, or `board` when a test sets it before adding the actor (the AI fixture gives its world one board, stepped with it). It names its home actor to `Entities` (`set_enemy`, by actor id) and ends its decoys and familiars when it leaves the tree (`enemy_gone`, before the actor's caster closes). All timing runs in `think(actor, delta)`, called by `Actor.step`, so tests drive actors with fixed deltas.
- **`AIBrain` (`brain.gd`, enemy AI AI5, docs/milestones/M2-enemy-ai.md):** the AI every enemy, minion, boss and test bot plays by. It **perceives** on its profile's `reaction` timer (staggered; at once after an attack or a new target): the target (the nearest standing actor of another team, `Projectiles.nearest_target`, looked for in rings nearest first, `LOOKS`, as the whole room's grid is dear: enemy AI L1, `tests/benchmark/ai_step.gd`; `nearest_foe(actor)`, static, which a decoy aims by too; a decoy is a target like any, no lure; `can_target(node)`: not freed, down, dying or out of the world; a teammate that hurt it for `GRUDGE_TIME`, 3 s), the distance and whether the line is clear (`AISight`: no wall between their bodies and, since boss plan OB14, no blocking prop's ground between their feet, `ground_clear`), the soonest shot coming (`Projectiles.threat`, with `dodge` > 0 or a staff that blocks), else its target's telegraph aimed at it (`TELEGRAPH_THREAT` seconds off, as a person reads it), its health (`health_of`; reported to the team board with where it stands, `TeamBoard.report`), and **what each staff casts next** (`Caster.next_cast_sense`, a `CastSense`; never a card id, `tests/core/test_ai_names_no_card.gd`), which are ready within their windup and which hold a sustained cast (left alone: a press would switch it off). The **range band** comes from its harmful casts' reach (`band_min` = reach × (0.75 − 0.6 × aggression), at least past its own blast; `band_max` = reach × (0.95 − 0.35 × aggression)): a bolt makes a kiter, a swing a rusher. It **decides** by asking every behaviour (`AIBehaviours.all()`) how much it `want`s to act (the running one gets `commitment` × 0.3; `randomness` sometimes takes the second best) and lets the winner `act`, which writes the plan (`plan_staff`, `plan_move`: STAND, TOWARD, AWAY or WAY with `plan_way`, `plan_urgent`, and POINT with `plan_point`, walked to with or without a target: an item to take, items I9, `plan_target`: what the staff is aimed at when not the target, a teammate to heal; the executor aims, leads and channels at it, and lets it go once it cannot be aimed at). Behaviours ask `reaches(sense, other)` (the target, or `other`) and `hurt_mate(sense)` (of the teammates the board has under `TeamBoard.HURT`, itself not, the most hurt that `sense` reaches along a clear line; a teammate is one the actor table puts on its team, so never a wizard for an enemy). **Spreading round a target (L3):** with `teamwork` above 0 it notes its target on the board at each decision (`TeamBoard.note_attack`) and reads back `attackers` (its team's on that target at the board's last count, itself too) and `slot_angle` (its slot round the target); behaviours ask `slot_off()` (how far round its slot is), `slot_way()` (the way to its slot at the middle of its band, at most `SLOT_TURN` round the target at a time, so it walks round and not through) and `spread_bonus()` (`teamwork` × `SPREAD_BONUS` / `attackers`). It **carries it out** in `state`: CHASE (walk as planned, aim at the target; wind up the planned staff once `Caster.cast_ready(windup)`, else wait), WINDUP (it presses the staff at once and stands while the cast winds up in Spells for its staff's and cards' `wind_up`, docs/milestones/cast-timing.md; its aim follows the target with lead and a seeded aim error of up to `AIM_ERROR` × (1 − accuracy), drawn once; owner 2026-11-06), the cast (`intent.press`), CHANNEL (a cast that `repeats_held`: hold the button while the target stays in its lane, the mana lasts and `channel_max` is not up, turning at `track_speed`), RECOVER (stand; a shot about to hit may cut it short; none at `rest_scale` 0, it goes on at once), REPOSITION (walk `AISight.detour` for `REPOSITION_TIME` or until the line is clear), TRANSITION (a boss's phase change) and CALL (L4: before the windup of a cast that harms, with `teamwork` above 0 (as often as `teamwork`), its team not calling within `TeamBoard.CALL_GAP` and a teammate within `CALL_REACH` on the board's reports (`nearest_mate`), and a windup above 0: it calls (`TeamBoard.call_attack`), its actor's Face glances at the teammate with raised brows (`Face.glance`) for `CALL_TIME`, it stands aiming at the target, then winds up if the staff is still ready). A brain with teamwork that sees a newer call of its team (`called_at`) decides at once, and its Fight is keener (`beat` above 1) within `CALL_REACH` of the caller, so it joins in. **The aim lock** (boss plan OB14, decision 26): with its profile's `aim_lock` above 0, its aim stops following the target in the WINDUP once the cast has no more than that left in Spells (`Caster.wind_left`, so a chill or a freeze that stretches the wind-up moves the lock with it; at least the wind-up: at its start), and the brain sends `aim_locked(actor, staff)` (only one that `warns()`), on which Feel's lane beats. **Turns** (OB14): with `turns` above 0, each attack notes its target (by actor key, `TURN_MEMORY` (8) of them, with its `clock`) and rolls whether it turns after it; it does as the attack is over (its rest over, `_attack_over`; never as a stand, a call or a walk round ends): the foe it aimed at longest ago (`_longest_ago_foe`: every open actor of another team it may aim at, one never aimed at first, the nearest of those) is its target until its next attack (a grudge still comes first). **Round blocking props** (OB14, decision 36): a plan that walks at the target (TOWARD) while a pillar's ground stands between their feet, or to a point (POINT, with a target) past one, walks the `AISight.detour` way instead until its next decision (`_steer`; not an urgent one); a REPOSITION ends once the line is clear of both; a target on its own ground (a body with no Mover) is walked at up to that ground's edge (`_walk_goal`), so its own footprint never hides it. The run-up asks `run_here(sense)` (how far a move of `sense` runs from here at the target) and `run_up(sense)` (where to start it, and its run there, a Vector3; `AISight.run_up_spot`) and keeps its start in `run_up_point` between its `want` and its `act`. Windups grow with danger: the cast's wind-up (`CastSense.wind_up`: an enemy's telegraph is its staff's data, cast timing T6); rests `REST_BASE` + half the delay, × `rest_scale`; in whole sixteenths of a second (`TIME_GRAIN`). **Enemies telegraph, allies don't** (owner, 2026-11-01; cast timing T6, 2026-11-06): how long a cast winds up is its staff's, whoever holds it (an enemy's staff has a `wind_up`, a player's none); only a brain driving an actor of another team than the players' shows it (`warns()`, `is_telegraphing`, read from `Caster.is_winding` so it ends the step the cast leaves): the telegraph and the furious face. An enemy's AI possessing a wizard casts the wizard's staffs as they are, with no wind-up and no warning. A new driver cancels the body's winding casts (`Actor.drive`); a chill stretches a wind-up and a freeze holds it (`Caster`, `Spells.hold_winds`). **Pressing on** (`AIProfile.press_on`, 0-1; the test bots' profile has 1, enemies 0): wherever an enemy stands in a fight (a plan that stands, the windup, the channel, the rest) it walks toward its target at `press_on` of its speed until their hurt circles touch (`_press_on`). An urgent plan presses a staff whose next cast moves the caster once, with no windup, and the move goes the way it walks. **No move of its own ends over a hole** (hub rework HR-H2): a dodge's dash, or a planned cast that moves it (close in, the run-up), is not cast while its end (its `move_reach` along the way it goes, short of the first wall; a homing one no farther than the target) is over a hole (`_lands_on_ground`); it walks as planned, and its walk stops at the lip. Timers slow by `Combat.pace`. **Harmful floor** (ground cover GC7, docs/milestones/ground-cover.md, rule 8): it asks where the floor round it would harm it through the bus (`look_harm(reach)`: `cover_danger_looked` fills a `CoverSight`, core/base, a window of the room's cover cells marked where their state gives a status; it never calls the cover area). At each decision it notes `in_harm` (it stands in such a cell) and `harm_exit` (the middle of the nearest cell that does not harm with no wall between, within `HARM_EXIT_LOOK`), for `evade`. Every step it walks (`_steer_off_harm`, after the state's move) it probes every cell its move crosses to `HARM_PROBE` (24 px) ahead, past the cell it stands in (`CoverSight.harms_along`, a corner clipped counting), and when a cell harms, turns to the nearest of `AISight.DIRECTIONS` whose cells do not and whose line is clear of walls, at the same speed, the side it turned last first (`HARM_SIDE_KEEP`, kept until its way is clear again, so it walks round a fire one way and does not swing between two); with no such way it walks on as it would. No pathfinder: steering, beside `AISight.detour`. A look in a room with no harmful cell costs one ask. Nothing allocates per step. Tests: `test_ai_cover.gd` (round a burning strip to its target, straight through grass at rest, out of burning grass with and without a target, a test bot too, ringed by fire walking on), `test_ai_brain.gd`, `test_ai_combos.gd` (every combo file played by an enemy: cast, never into a wall, never killing itself; a combo's `allies` stand by it as hurt teammates, so the heal-only combo is cast too), `test_ai_team.gd` (the team board, heal ally, spreading, the call); `tests/screenshots/shot_call.gd` shows two strays mid-call.

- **`TeamBoard` (`team_board.gd`, enemy AI AI6):** what a team's AIs share, one for every team, kept and stepped by `Entities` (`board`; cleared on a new room). `started(team, who)` at each windup; `beat(team, who, teamwork)` makes a teammate's attack a little less wanted for `BEAT` seconds after (never its own), so a barrage reads as a sequence. **Reports (L2):** each brain reports where it stands and its health at each decision (`report(team, who, at, health, hint)`, which returns its slot); a team keeps `PLACES` (16) for `REPORT_LIFE` (2 s), a new one taking a free slot, else the oldest of a teammate that is not hurt, else the oldest, so a hurt one stays on the board in a crowd; read with `mate(team, slot)` (-1 for none or too old), `mate_place`, `mate_health`. **Spreading (L3):** `note_attack(team, who, target, target_at, at, hint)` (a target's instance id; `ATTACKS`, 16 a team, the oldest out); every `RECOUNT` (0.25 s, in `step`) it counts per target the attackers noted within `NOTE_LIFE` (2 s) and gives each a slot: ranked by the angle it stands at round the target, the slots 360° / count apart, the ring turned to the mean of their angles less their slots' (each walks the least, none crosses another); `attackers(team, slot, who)` (0 before the count or for another's slot) and `slot_angle(team, slot)`. **The call (L4):** `call_attack(team, who, at)`, `is_calling(team)` (for `CALL_TIME`, 0.5 s), `can_call(team)` (none in the last `CALL_GAP`, 3 s), `called_at(team)`, `nearest_mate(team, who, at, reach)` (from the reports; `Vector2.INF` for none); while a call lasts, `beat(team, who, teamwork, at)` is 1 + `CALL_BOOST` × teamwork for a teammate within `CALL_REACH` (160 px) of the caller (not the caller). The poses are optional: the strays (living staffs H5) have none, since their `Face` carries the mood (angry at rest, furious while the controller `is_telegraphing`, worried when hit). **The hop:** a stray walking faster than `HOP_SPEED` hops `HOP_HEIGHT` px `HOP_RATE` times a second on the sprite's `offset` (the hurt circle stays put), and bobs `HOP_IDLE` px standing; the actor's `Face` (stepped after this look) gets the same lift as its `body_offset`; `lift()`. On top it plays its cast's staff animation (ActorLook's `pose_offset` and `apply_pose`: the carrier card's `staff_pose`, so a stray with a slam raises its staff glowing as a wizard's does). Players float, so the two sides read apart by motion. A step sets nothing on the nodes that did not change (the pose, the whole-pixel lift). All of an enemy's drawing is here, none in the AI. Its nodes are exports (`sprite`, `animation`), else the actor's children of those names. **Poses from states and layered bodies (boss plan OB8):** for a layered body (its `Sprite` then shows no art: the strips and a phase's body sprite are a single sprite's) each step it steps a `BodyPose` (gameplay/actor) with what it knows (its AI `is_telegraphing`, its brain's TRANSITION, `is_hurt()`, the staff animation's aim): `body_pose()`, `body()`. A layered body (`BodyPart`s under its `Sprite`, `parts()`) is drawn by it each step, at `full_speed` px/s whole; its lift then moves the `Sprite` (every part with it), and the part that `carries_face` takes the `Face` (`body_offset` from that part's place against its rest). `hops` false: a body that hovers only bobs. Test: `tests/gameplay/ai/test_ai_poses.gd`.
- **`DummyLook`** shows what the training dummy took (an actor with Health, a Hurtbox, an `AIController` with the `post` profile and no staff and no Mover, so it stands; on the enemies' team): damage per second over the last 5 s from its health in Combat (burn ticks count), its statuses and the last reaction at its body; `dps()`, `status_text()`, `total_damage`. Its damage numbers are every actor's (`DamageNumbers`, gameplay/actor; props W5).
- `Entities` (staffs B5) keeps the decoys and familiars cards make, in a fixed pool (four of each kind per player; one more ends the oldest). **A decoy is an actor** (gameplay/actor; owner, 2026-11-02): a copy of its caster's body (`DecoyLook`, `decoy_look.gd`: drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_sprite.gdshader` in its owner's colour, part fallen apart, with a glow; never see-through, the glow look 2026-10-27) with Health (`DECOY_HEALTH`, 15), a Hurtbox, a Mover, a Caster with no cards (it tells the systems where it is and aims) and a `DecoyController` (`decoy_controller.gd`), which aims it at the nearest standing actor of another team it can see (`AIBrain.nearest_foe`, looked up every `AIBrain.RETARGET_TIME`; when that one is behind a wall, the nearest of the nearer ones with a clear line, `AISight.line_clear`, else still the nearest), so what its card's triggers cast goes at its foes (core/spells fires a payload from an actor's body along that actor's aim). A copy of a living staff (a head sheet of `StaffLook.DIRECTIONS` columns) turns to its aim. Its card's `on hurt` fires when the decoy is hurt, not its owner (owner, 2026-11-02). It is a regular actor (owner, 2026-11-06): a shield on its card is a barrier Combat keeps on it for its life and a deflect turns the shots round it once, as it appears (core/spells' `CarrierDecoy`, once the decoy is in the world); Entities guards nothing itself. What its card's payloads fire is cast by its body (owner, 2026-11-06): a dash, teleport, pull or swap in them moves the decoy (its Mover takes its owner's card's move when the event names it, `Mover._owns`), an orbit or familiar circles it, recoil pushes it, and a Pulse in them is round it (docs/CARRIERS.md, "Pulse: a ring from the cast point"). It winds up nothing: only the casts an AI presses are telegraphed (owner, 2026-11-02), and what a decoy's triggers fire comes on its card's clock, an enemy's decoy too. A decoy placed again starts with an empty intent, so it aims the way it was placed until it finds a foe. It is on its owner's team and belongs to its owner (`Actor.belongs_to`): its owner's shots pass it, every other actor's hurt it, area hits too. There is no lure: the other teams chase it when it is the nearest actor of another team, as any. Homing on its card makes it creep toward its target within the homing reach at `SpellLimits.HOMING_DRIFT`, as a homing rune does; otherwise it stands (hits still push it). It ends when its health is gone (it is `downable`, so Entities ends it: no kill, no `enemy_died`) or its time runs out. Each row makes its decoy actor the first time it holds one and keeps it: an ended decoy leaves the tree (its id, hurt circle and caster close) and is placed again later under a fresh id; Entities steps its decoys in `step` (`Actor.step`, then `Actor.move`: against the walls, by `delta`). Entities names the decoy's actor id to Spells while the spawn is asked for (`Spells.entity_body`). `decoy_of(caster)` (tests). A familiar (drawn as an orbit shot by `core/projectiles/shot_orbit.gdshader`: a small white-hot core in its player's colours and a tail bending round its circle) circles its caster (`entity_spawn_requested`'s `caster`: its player; the shot whose trigger made it, followed while it flies; or the point it was made at, once that shot is gone), starting on the side where its card put it; it casts nothing itself (core/spells fires what its triggers give it, rules R4 F3). `seconds` of INF (or 0 or less) lasts until `entity_end_requested`. A new room or the end of the run clears them all without `entity_ended`. `step(delta)` does the work. **Entities by actor (actors G3b):** a spawn belongs to its `caster`, an actor id; an owner a seat drives uses the players' rows, any other the enemies' (seated or not, `Actors.seat_of`). **G8:** the caster's body (what a decoy copies and a familiar circles) is the node the actor table knows (`Actors.node_of`), or the body the AI named (`set_enemy`); no group or player index is read. **Enemies' entities (F8d):** a spawn from an enemy's actor belongs to that enemy. Its decoy copies the enemy, in the enemy ramp, on the enemies' team and belonging to the enemy: every shot but its own enemy's hurts it (a decoy is a regular actor: a shield on its card is a barrier on it, a deflect turns the shots round it as it appears; owner, 2026-11-06), players' homing picks it, and it aims at the nearest wizard. Its familiar circles the enemy. Each enemy keeps two of a kind; enemies share 32 rows of their own after the players' 32, so a crowd never takes a player's (full, the oldest enemy entity ends). The AI names its body for its actor when it opens it (`set_enemy`) and, as it closes it, ends its entities where they are with `entity_ended` (`enemy_gone`), so even a `hold` one goes with the enemy.
- **Staffs (staffs F8a, docs/milestones/M2-staffs.md "Enemy staffs"):** an enemy whose data has a `staff` and `cards` casts them like a player: its Caster (gameplay/actor) installs them as staff 0 under its actor id (compiled once per staff and cards for every caster), gets mana back (its staff's `mana_regen`: the pool is the staff's, `StaffBase.max_mana`; plus siphon's `mana_refund_requested`), and casts what the AI presses. Caster-side cards (F8c: dash, teleport, pull, swap, recoil) move it through its Mover, one implementation for every actor. Decoys and familiars (F8d): see `Entities` above. **Everything hurts everything (actors G3c):** an enemy's spells hit the other enemies too, and its blasts hurt it; teams only aim.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: `cover_danger_looked` (every brain, as it walks and at each decision, GC7; heard by the cover area, which fills the look at once); `aim_locked` (an enemy's AI stops following its target with the cast it winds up: its profile's `aim_lock`; boss plan OB14; heard by Feel, the lane's beat); `ground_looked` and `actor_take_requested` (a brain whose profile takes items, items I9; heard by the Items area); `entity_ended` (a decoy broke or ran out, a familiar ended; for the end point; Entities).   - Listens to: `entity_spawn_requested`, `entity_end_requested`, `room_entered` and `run_ended` (Entities); `damage_dealt` and `reaction_triggered` (`DummyLook`, the training dummy). ## Review questions

A good PR here adds or improves one reusable behaviour that several enemies can configure, targets any of the 1–4 players, and stays cheap with 100–200 enemies. Besides the general review:

- Are behaviours reusable by several enemies? Do 100–200 enemies stay within the frame budget?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
