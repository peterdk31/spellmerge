# Feel

## Purpose

Hit-stop, screen shake and juice presets.

Built in M1 (see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Feel only reacts: it never changes damage or outcomes. Hit-stop is its only effect on timing; it is short and the same for every player.

**Freezes in co-op (melee I7, owner 2026-10-29) are not Feel's:** since online-ready N4 hit-stop is a rule of the game, `gameplay/combat/hit_stop.gd` (Combat's `HitStop`): a hit or a kill holds only its attacker, target and shot, a body rests after a stop, and only a downed player and a boss kill stand the whole game still, for a number of ticks (`Sim.hold`). Feel holds nothing and never touches `Engine.time_scale`; it draws what the holds do (the flash, the shake, the sound). A cue's `hold`, `hold_target`, `hold_shot` and `stop_game` are read by `HitStop`, not by Feel.

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/feel/feel.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. It is the autoload `Feel`. The setting `Feel.shake_enabled` turns screen shake off (the pause menu's "Screen shake" switch sets it through `screen_shake_changed` and saves the choice; on by default); hit-stop, particles and sound stay. The limit `Feel.PARTICLE_LIMIT` (48 live hit particles in all, across every player) is the one other code reads.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: none.
  - Listens to (caster-keyed events carry the actor id; Feel reads its ramp, seat and budget from the actor table, actors G3b): `damage_dealt`, `enemy_died`, `player_downed`, `spell_cast`, `staff_shown`, `possess_started`, `possess_ended`, `boss_shown` (a boss entered play, boss plan OB16: the cue `boss_intro` on its drawn body, under the HUD's banner; for a boss with an intro only when none played for it, `BossIntroWait`, since the overlay's own sounds are its entrance), `boss_intro_opened` and `boss_intro_closed` (`boss_intro_sound.gd`, below), `projectile_impact`, `carrier_shown`, `aim_locked` (an enemy's AI locked its aim on the cast it winds up, boss plan OB14: the cast's telegraph lane, `lanes.gd`, flashes for `BEAT_TIME`, 0.2 s, through `telegraph.gdshader`'s `telegraph_beat`), `effect_slots_changed`, `mote_cap_changed`, `mote_density_changed`, `spell_light_changed`, `room_entered`, `room_built` (the room's ground cover, `cover.gd`), `cover_changed` (the cover area's cells' states, GC5: each cell's tufts show it, `show_state`), `screen_shake_changed`, `damage_number_shown` and `damage_numbers_changed` (the damage numbers' drawer), `projectile_expired`, `reaction_triggered`, `discharge_arc`, `status_applied`, `hit_landed`, `heal_dealt` and `barrier_changed` (a barrier's bubble of motes, `barriers.gd`: the owner's pick of 2026-11-06, the mote shell; drawn by the motes' shader, rewritten every frame round the actor, its knocked-off motes thrown into the pool; a shot with shield charges gets the same shell, read from the projectile manager's guarded shots each frame, a wall's a capsule round its row, and its block, `shot_blocked` naming the shielded shot, plays `shield_block` and knocks its share off; the shell's size and motes follow what it guards). A still carrier card that names `look_motes` (the wall's stream rising off its seam, the pool's motes rising off its mire; the owner, 2026-11-09: "use motes for everything you can") has its shots give them off all the time while they stand, from where the projectile manager draws the card's body (a typed view of its still shots, `watch_body`, `still_shots_drawn`, `StillShotView`: no row of the manager's is read here; `still_motes.gd`, the spectacle's), in the card's `look_shape` (the oval or the seam its shader draws by the same numbers), one stream for each `CardMoteStream` the card lists (its kind, rate, where from, how high, from when and until when), thinning while a shot comes and as it goes (`look_open`, `look_fade`), parting for a moment where a shot or a beam strikes it (`projectile_impact` on no target: a few motes knocked out, births there held back), in its colours, its infusions in turn and a share as its infusions' own kinds. The world's things give off motes all the time, as art (`source_motes.gd`, the spectacle's; how to use it: content/motes/README.md, "How to make something in the world give off motes"; props W7, docs/milestones/props.md; the owner, 2026-11-10: "motes coming off almost everything"): every node in the group `mote_source` with a `MoteSource` (core/base/mote_source.gd) as its metadata, the room's own (the hub's island edge, a hidden `Line2D`: the owner's pick "A, mixed elements", 2026-11-10) and those a prop's look adds for the state it is in. It follows the tree as spell light follows its spots (`watch`: a node that enters the group is read at the next frame, so a mask's sprite that enters after it is there; one that leaves is let go; `room_entered` reads them all again), each read once into its own copy (its place in the world, a line's points, a mask's lit pixels, its ramps or its art's colours), no node held. Each frame a source within `VIEW_MARGIN` of the view (`Spectacle.view_rect`) gives off its rate's share of its kind's motes from a point, along a line (thrown outward), in a box, on an oval's rim or from a mask's lit pixels, between its `start` and `stop` after it appeared, each in one of its ramps, as motes of many a frame that give way first in a crowded one; a standing mask places a mote on every lit pixel each frame instead (`placed_motes.gd`, the spectacle's `StandingMotes`), each cooling through its ramp in its own turn, so carvings twinkle. Changing or removing a look is the node's data or the kind, never code. A source is read once, where its node is when it appears (it does not follow a node that moves), unless its node says it moves (`MoteSource.put` with `follow`, metadata `mote_follow`; boss plan OB8: a boss's burning head's sparks): then its places move each frame by how far the node went since it was read (not its turn), and a node gone gives off nothing. Where a mote leaves from is one class a kind of place in `mote_places.gd` (read once, picked from each mote), so `source_motes.gd` names none; the places in an oval there are shared with the still cards' motes. A source that gives off nothing says why once, naming its node. The portals' gates are mote rifts (`portal_look.gd`, the owner's pick of 2026-11-09), read once a frame from the portal carrier (`CarrierPortal.read_view`, a `PortalView`: no getter called by name): each gate's lens is a quad drawn by the portal card's shader (`Carrier.LOOK_PORTAL`, `content/spells/carriers/portal/portal.gdshader`; `core/projectiles/shot_portal.gdshader` the plain one; one batch, so one shader, as Entities' orbs), sized by how open it is; its lips (a lip an infusion), its loose motes spiralling in and thrown out, and the dotted line to each exit are real `ward` motes, placed each frame by `placed_motes.gd`, which a shield's shell (`barriers.gd`) places its motes by too.
- **Hits on characters:** when a shot hits an enemy or a player, it shows a spray of sparks (a flash, `KIND_SPRAY`) on along the way the shot flew (`projectile_impact` comes just before the damage and names the direction and the spot), by the hit actor (actors G8: the body's `Actor.id`, its ramp from `Actors.ramp_of`): in its own colours for an actor of a hat ramp (a player), in ember for one of the enemy ramp (or a body that is no actor), a zoom bigger for much more damage than 5. Damage with no impact (a contact hit) is a puff all round (`KIND_PUFF`). A kill turns the hit's spray into a bigger puff in the same slot.
- **Hit kinds (staffs B3):** `hit_landed` comes just before a hit's `damage_dealt` and names its kind; `FeelPreset.kind_looks` gives each kind (`shot`, `ray`, `swing`, `burst`, `reaction`, `status`) a default shake and sound id (a swing's: about 2 px of shake, the melee playground's; each kind's stop is `HitStop.KIND_STOP`), so a new card feels like something before it has its own sound (a `status` tick has none of them). An actor hit by its own spell (`hit_landed` whose caster is the hit actor's id; any actor, actors G8) also gets the cue `self_hit` (a ring in its own colour) on the frame of the hit, so a self-hit is never taken for an enemy's. `heal_dealt` plays the cue `heal`: a soft green ring, with no stop and no shake. `player_downed` plays the cue `player_downed` (its shake and sound; its game-wide stop is `HitStop`'s; it draws nothing, the event names no position). When `player_downed` arrives, Feel asks Combat for the seat's own body (`home_actor_of(seat)`, also while the seat drives another actor) and its `downed_note_of(actor)`, and raises that text ("downed by your own Blast") over that body (`Actors.node_of`), in its ramp's light shade, for `downed_note_seconds`; it shares the reaction name labels.
- **Walls and pillars:** a shot that ends on a wall sheds motes (below) back along where it came from and shows the shot's flash (see Flashes below): the kinds of its cards' look layers in its own ramp (`Motes.shed_kinds`, from `Projectiles.impact_look`, `impact_core` and `impact_tag`), and the room's surface kinds (`RoomBase.surface_kinds`, else every surface kind) in its wall colours (`RoomBase.surface_colors`, read on `room_entered`; none before a room). There is no other look for them: every particle is a mote (S3).
- **The spectacle** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S1): the motes, the floor marks and spell light below are `spectacle.gd`'s, a child of Feel (`spectacle()`) that owns them, the ramp table they share, their settings (each through its event: `mote_cap_changed`, `mote_density_changed`, `floor_marks_changed`, `spell_light_changed`) and the room they are in (`room_entered`: the walls' colours, the mark layer's fit). Feel says when (its hit, end, wall and death handlers call `shed_hit`, `shed_end`, `shed_on_wall`, `body_source` with what they already worked out, in the order they draw; `step` once a frame) and the spectacle says what. Feel keeps `motes()`, `marks()`, `light()`, `ramp_table()`, `use_motes` and the settings' `set_*` and values (`mote_cap`, `mote_density`, `floor_marks`, `spell_light`) as forwards. A new drawn-only system goes in the spectacle the same way: made in its `_ready`, its setting through its own event, its cue parts in `cue_parts`.
- **Motes** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1a): single-pixel particles with no game logic, in `mote.gd` (the spectacle's, `motes()` for tests; always there, S3). **Kinds are data:** one `MoteKind` file each in `content/motes/` (core/base/mote_kind.gd: how it flies and how a burst throws it), read from the registry when the pool is made; their numbers go to the shader as a texture, a row a kind (`kind_table`, `KIND_COLUMNS` texels: motion, sway, shape; `MAX_KINDS`, 256, so content grows with no shader edit) and their shapes as `shape_atlas` (each frame a 5x5 square, a kind's frames along its row), so a new kind, or a new look for one, is a new file. A shape's frames play over its life, loop, or are picked per mote, and a turning shape follows the way the mote flies (8 ways, read over the last 0.03 s; at rest, the way it was thrown). **Kinds come from the spells' cards** (spell-spectacle-2.md, S2): a kind names the cards it is the motes of by id (`MoteKind.cards`) or by look layer (`look_layers`); a card's kinds are those that name it, else its look layer's, and a shot sheds every kind of its spell's cards, its burst split between them (layer 0's kinds when they have none). The spell comes from the shot's tag: `card_kinds.gd` (the spectacle's `cards`) asks `Spells.profile(tag)` and keeps each spell's kinds by tag, checked against the spell once a frame (a tag a rebuilt staff reuses after 8 builds gets its new spell's) and each answer kept for the frame, so a hit costs about what `kinds_of_look` did; the tags come with the events (`Projectiles.impact_tag` while an impact is sent, `projectile_expired`'s tag, a reaction's or a death's shot, `Spells.cue_tag` while a blast's `cue_shown` is sent, `CueContext.tag`) and with each trail birth (`SPARK_TAG`, two floats). A shot with no spell (an enemy's) sheds by its look (`kinds_of_look`), and while no kind names a card nothing is looked up. A shed asks its kinds only when its hit sprays (`Motes.open_hit`, see below). A kind that names nothing is thrown only where something names it (a cue part, a room's `surface_kinds`). **No colour is kept here:** a spell's motes cool through the shot's own ramp, picked in the shader by the shot shaders' `look_of` (the caster's hat ramp for a plain core, else the palette material nearest the core, or the exact colour; `spell_source`); other colours (a room's walls) are rows of the ramp table (`table_source`, a texture of `RAMPS` rows of white-hot, light, base, shadow). A row is written once (`burst`, `throw`, `shed`) and `mote.gdshader` works out its whole flight from it and the time since its birth on Feel's clock: along the floor with drag, a fake height that rises or falls, one bounce, lying still, cooling step by step through its ramp (no partial alpha), then gone; `Motes.flight` is the same on the CPU. The pool is a ring of `mote_cap` rows (the pause menu's "Particles" slider through `mote_cap_changed`: `MoteCap` in core/settings, Minimal 2,048, Low 8,192, Medium 16,384 or High 32,768, no Off since S3 (a saved 0 loads as Minimal); the platform's by default, `set_mote_cap`; 0 makes no motes and no nodes): a new mote overwrites the oldest. It is cut into `Motes.CHUNK` (1,024) row chunks, one batch each (`Batches.multimesh()`, made the first time a mote lands in it); `step` uploads only the chunks written since the last frame and hides a chunk once its motes are all gone (by the longest life each of its bursts could give; at most `LIFE_MAX`, 4 s). A mote with no glow draws a quad only as big as its shape (`Motes.reach_of`, handed to the shader in `kind_sway.w`): most are a pixel or two, and their fragments were most of their GPU cost. At most `Motes.FRAME_ROWS` (256) are written between two steps and a burst past that is cut short; a shot's hit, end or wall hit (`shed`, `throw`: a few motes each, about 2.5 us a mote with its call in a fight) stops once only `Motes.MOMENT_ROWS` (128) are left, which the moments keep (a cue's or a reaction's `throw_part`, tens of motes in one burst at about 0.5 us each), so a blast in a storm still throws its motes. **A hit's motes are shared by staff** (the owner, 2026-11-05; `hit_shares.gd`): every hit is one spray (its shed, its wall's chips, its cue's `often` sparks, its damage's spray; Feel frames it with `Spectacle.begin_hit`/`end_hit`, the damage follows with `hold_hit`), in its caster's staff's group, and the frame's hit rows (`Motes.hit_rows()`) are shared out with `fair_level`, planned from the frame before: a lone cast's hits spray whole, a crowded staff's are a random subset that spray whole, and beside a crowd a staff not thinned may take `FRESH_ROWS` of the moments' rows. The table of the frame's rows by class is at `Motes.FRAME_ROWS`. The numbers are from the storm benchmark (P1c, docs/DECISIONS.md; the hub storm, `hub_storm.gd`, prints the motes' CPU time, `mote_write.gd` the cost of each call, `shot_gpu.gd --motes` the GPU's). They draw at `MOTE_Z`, under the floor marks, beams, flashes and telegraphs. `mote_land(row)` gives where and when a mote comes to rest (INF for one that never does), for the floor marks (P2b). **One writer:** every row is written by `burst` at the named offsets `ROW_*` (`shed`, `throw`, `throw_part` and the trails' `give_off` all end there), so a mote is counted, kept drawn and settled the same way whatever threw it; `mote_land` and the settles share one sum (`_land`), and a test holds it, `flight` and the shader's `mote_flight` to the same lines. **Where motes come from** (P1b; Feel listens, nothing new is sent): a shot ending on a wall sheds its cards' kinds and the wall's chips (P1a); a hit on a body sheds the shot's kinds back along the way it came (`hit_mote_share` of their count, a preset number); a bolt or orbit whose life runs out puffs them on along its way (its cue `expire`: a `motes` part with a `share` of their count, S6, one of many a frame like a hit's); a reaction's cue throws a ring of the reacting shot's kinds (its `shot`, read while the event is sent) in the reaction's exact colour (S6: a `motes` part in the cue's own `color`, its count and `radius` times the cue's `power`); and a cue throws its `motes` parts (`CueBase`; `throw_part`: the cue's cards' kinds in its ramp, the surface kinds in the colours of what broke, or a named kind, out to `reach` times its size). A body that dies is what broke: its death cue's chips take the colours of its own art (the frame it shows, `ArtPixels.colours_of`, read once per texture), and its spell motes the killing shot's cards. Every burst is times the "Particle amount" setting (`MoteDensity`: None, Half, Full, Double; `mote_density_changed`, `set_mote_density`; `Motes.density`, a share of a mote thrown that often so the average holds). With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown. **A shot's card particles in flight are motes too** (P1d; since S3 the only way they are drawn; a dash's are a `stream` cue part since 2026-11-05): while Feel has motes it sets `Projectiles.spark_motes`, and each frame Feel throws one mote for each particle the manager hands out (`spark_births`; `Motes.give_off`: a kind of the particle's look layer in its shot's spell, `CardKinds.layer_motes`, else layer 0's, given off back along the shot at `TRAIL_SPEED` of the kind's burst speed, in the shot's ramp; a one-mote `burst` each, read from the birth's named offsets `SPARK_*`), before its motes step. **Tails as motes** (S5 step 4; the owner, 2026-11-05: "if only the motes could look more like a tail"): with the tails off, the manager's births start with every flying shot's tail, laid by distance (`SPARK_TAIL`, core/README.md); `give_off` makes each a mote of its tail code's kind (`Motes.tail_kind_of`: the `MoteKind.tail` kinds in id order, A `tail`, B `tail_streak`, C `tail_long`; each shot picks one by its sequence, the owner's pick of 2026-11-05), and the spectacle gives the motes `tail_births_cap` more rows a frame while tails are laid, so a tail never takes the moments' rows. `tests/screenshots/shot_tail_motes.gd` shows held streams of atoms and fire bolts, each shot's tail in its own tail kind (a filmstrip). **The births are written natively** (`MoteKernel`, native/README.md; `Motes.native`): `key_births` names each birth's (spell tag, look kind) pair, `_give_off_native` looks up each pair's kinds once (the cards lookup is a script), the kernel picks a kind for each birth and writes its row as `burst` would, and the settling motes' marks come back in `_settle_out` for `Marks.settle`; about 0.27 us a birth against 2.4 in GDScript. The GDScript `give_off` stays as the reference and the fallback, and `test_native_motes.gd` holds the two to the byte. **What Feel draws itself gives off births too** (S6 item 10): a swing's sparks and card particles and a beam's splash and card particles, which their shot shaders drew until then, are births in the same format (`births.gd`, the spectacle's `births`: `add` with the SPARK_* offsets, `roll` for a rate's share of a frame, `emitting_layer` for a card particle's look kind), written while Feel draws them and given off by `Spectacle.step` after the trails' (`_drawn_motes`), so the native kernel writes them and they take their spell's cards' motes by its tag (`swing_tag`, `beam_tag`); at most `SPARK_BIRTHS_MAX` a frame. **A status's particles are its card's motes** (S6 item 11): `Spectacle.status_motes` throws a few a second round the body (see Statuses). **Flashes' sparks and dead bodies** (S3): a hit's spray and a puff (a hit with no way) are the cues `hit` and `hit_puff` (S6 2): the flash a flash part, its sparks a `motes` part of the kind `spark` marked `often` (one of many a frame, so they leave the moments' rows alone, as a `share` part does); a flash shown alone (`show_flash`) throws no sparks, so a cue that wants them names them (a reaction's, a conjure's, a death's puff), an explosion's fragments are its cue's surface motes and its scorch a mark, and a dead body breaks into motes of its own art (`Spectacle.break_body`, `Motes.crumble`: the preset's `death_mote_*` share of its art's pixels, `ArtPixels.pixels_of`, each shown where its pixel was at the body's rest scale, in its art's colours, falling to its feet; a death is a moment and may take the frame's last rows). With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown.
- **Falls** (hub rework HR-H2, docs/milestones/hub-rework.md, "Holes" 4): `fall_look.gd` (`FallLook`, view only) draws a body falling into a hole from its state alone (`Actor.fall_share`), so a machine that joins mid-fall or a restored snapshot draws it: each frame it walks the actor table's open bodies and draws each falling one shrinking toward its feet (`SHRINK`) and darkening into the void (`VOID_TINT`, `FADE`) by writing its `scale` and `modulate` (drawing only; nothing in the simulation reads them, its size is `Actor.size`), with `MOTES_PER_SECOND` of the `drift` look's motes off it (the spectacle's `status_motes`); a body whose fall is over gets its own scale and colour back. No floor mark is laid over a hole: the marks' mask is the room grid's `wall_image(true)` (`Projectiles.grid()`), walls and holes. A frame with no body falling allocates nothing (`tests/gameplay/actor/test_fall.gd`).
- **Spawns** (enemy spawning EA2, docs/milestones/enemy-spawning.md, "The look"; spawn styles AS1, docs/milestones/spawn-styles.md): `spawn_look.gd` (`SpawnLook`, view only, a top-level child of Feel at the floor marks' z, under the bodies) draws an enemy spawning from its state alone (`Actor.spawn_share`), so a machine that joins mid-spawn or a restored snapshot draws it where it is; Feel steps it each frame over the actor table's open bodies, as `FallLook` (`step`: the marks and the cues), and the bodies are drawn just before each frame is drawn (`cut`, on `RenderingServer.frame_pre_draw`, after every `_process` and deferred call), so a body added after Feel's step (a run started as a load ends) is never drawn whole for a frame. **Its numbers are a style's** (`SpawnStyle`, core/base; content/spawn_styles/): Feel hands the look a lookup of the styles by id (`style_of`, the registry's `spawn_styles`; each looked up once, forgotten when the registry changes, `forget_styles`), and draws each body by its own style (`style_for`: `Actor.spawn_style`, state, which Flow picked with `SpawnStyle.pick`, spawn styles AS2); an id it does not find (a style missing on this machine, an old snapshot) is drawn as `rise`, and with no `rise` as `SpawnStyle.DEFAULT`'s numbers. A body whose style's motion changes mid-spawn (a snapshot restored over it) is put back by the motion that drew it before the new one draws it (`cut`). **What the body does is the style's motion** (`SpawnLook.MOTIONS`: one object each, in `spawn_motions/`, keyed by the names of `SpawnStyle.MOTIONS`, a test holds the two lists together; a motion the look does not have is drawn as `rise`, `FALLBACK_MOTION`, so a body is never drawn whole mid-spawn). A motion (`spawn_motions/spawn_motion.gd`) answers `apply(body, share, style)`, called every frame the body spawns in, and `put_back(key, body)` once it spawns in no more or went (`body` null), and holds the measures every motion needs (`body_height`: its body sprite's top, its hurt shape's or twice its body's centre, at its size, and a margin; `body_bottom`: a spitter's frame reaches 2 px under its feet), each body's `size_top` (from its body's centre and its hurt shape) worked out when it is first drawn and kept while it spawns in, again only if its size changes (`Actor.set_size`, the one thing that moves them in play); its sprite is read every frame, since its look's bob moves its art while it spawns in. What every style shares is the look's: up to the style's `mark_end` (`rise`: 0.4) a mark grows on the floor at the body's feet, from a dot to its footprint's width in `mark_frames` (4) frames, a hole in the room's floor (`RoomBase.floor_colors`, from `room_entered`: its shadow darkened) ringed in the light shade of the style's `colours` (`shades_for`: the floor's own for `floor`, else the palette material's; spawn styles AS3), its rim drawn as far round as the motion says (`rim_share`: all of it for `rise`); a style with a `mark_strip` (spawn styles AS4) has its art in place of the ring: its frames in order over the mark stage, its last held, then `close_frames` of them backwards to its first as the mark closes (`SpawnStyle.strip_frame`), drawn with `draw_texture_rect_region` at whole pixels centred on the feet (`strip_corner`) at a whole-number scale of the footprint (`SpawnStyle.strip_scale`, 1 to 4), each mark's strip, frame and scale kept in the arrays sized once in `_init` (`tests/gameplay/feel/test_spawn_strip.gd`); from `close_from` (0.92) it closes in `close_frames` (3) frames down to a dot as the feet land (`SpawnLook.mark_radius(radius, share, style)`); when the motion says (`cue_share`: `rise`'s as it starts, `materialize`'s as it is whole) the style's `cue` plays at the body's feet (`play_cue(cue, at, weighted)`; `rise`'s `enemy_spawn`, content/cues: dust off the mark, the soft sound `enemy_spawn`), its sound at most every `SOUND_EVERY` (0.1 s, across every style; played unweighted in between, its dust only), so a group does not stack it. **`rise`** (`spawn_motions/rise.gd`, EA2's code moved unchanged): through the mark the body is not drawn; then it rises out of it, drawn shifted down by whole pixels from its full height (and the rows its art has under its feet) to 0 (`shift(height, share, style)`) and cut off at the line of its art's lowest row, so nothing appears in one jump as it lands. No fade, no blur, no scale: its own sprite and pose all the way. **The cut:** the body's root is made a clip parent (`CanvasItem.clip_children`), its own canvas item drawing one rect above the feet's line as the mask (`mask_rect`), and each of its direct drawn children (not a top-level one) is drawn shifted (`RenderingServer.canvas_item_set_transform`, every frame; a part's own parts go with it), so one cut serves any body, layered or not; checked first on a virtual display with the game's renderer against the world's y-sort and the light pass (both hold: the root is never moved, so the body keeps its own place in the y-sort and stays behind a wizard just in front of its mark, and the light pass lights it as any body), so the plan's fallback, a cut per sprite, was not needed. - **The ground, drawn as a room is laid out** (connected biomes CB4, docs/milestones/connected-biomes.md, decision 6): `ground/` draws a room's ground in the game, as `tools/ground_shape.py` bakes it, from the room's shape file as the room is placed, mirrored either way or not. `ground/ground_layout.gd` (Feel's `Ground` child) hears `room_built` and, for a room whose `RoomBase.ground_shape` names its `<room>.shape.json` (every crypt room; the hub names none and keeps its baked art), draws it and puts it on the layout's `Floor` before it is shown (`GroundShape.apply`): the floor, glow, rock face and wall art become drawn textures, and the rims' and drips' mote lines are made again (the motes read them as the room enters the tree). - **Floor marks** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): scorches, cracks and frost that stay on the floor until the room ends, in `marks.gd` (the spectacle's, `marks()` for tests; null with the setting off). **The layer** is a `SubViewport` the size of the room's bounds (the projectile manager's `bounds()`), one texel a world pixel (the arena's 704x448 is about 1.2 MB of RGBA8, a 1344x784 room about 4.2 MB; at most `Marks.MAX_SIDE`, 4,096, a side), with `CLEAR_MODE_NEVER` and `UPDATE_DISABLED`: only a frame with new stamps renders it (`UPDATE_ONCE`), so a frame with none costs nothing. Its texture is one `Sprite2D` at `MARK_Z` (`RoomBase.FLOOR_Z + 1`: over the rooms' `Floor` sprite at `RoomBase.FLOOR_Z`, under every body at 0, the motes and Feel's floor flashes). **A cue's mark part** (`"part": "mark"`, core/base/cue_base.gd) queues a stamp (`stamp`); `step` draws up to `STAMPS_PER_FRAME` (512) of them in one batch inside the viewport (`Batches.multimesh()`, `mark.gdshader`; the batch shows only its frame's rows, so a later render never draws them twice) and the rest wait for later frames (at most `PENDING_MAX` wait; past that a stamp is counted in `left_out`). **Kinds are data** (`MarkKind`, content/marks): their forms and numbers go to the shader as `kind_form` and `kind_colour`; with `cards` a part stamps the marks of the cue's cards (`kinds_of_look`) instead of its own when they have one. **No colour is kept here:** a part's colour is the floor's own (`set_floor_colors`, from `RoomBase.floor_colors` on `room_entered`) or the spell's ramp, picked in the shader by `look_of` as the motes pick theirs (`Motes.spell_source`). **Off the walls:** the shader is clipped by `floor_mask`, the room grid's walls (`RoomGrid.wall_image()`, one texel a wall cell). `room_entered` wipes the layer (one cleared render) and fits it to the room (`_fit_marks`); the "Floor marks" switch (`FloorMarks` in core/settings, `floor_marks_changed`, `set_floor_marks`) makes or frees it, and off there are no nodes. **Settled motes** (P2b): a mote kind that names a mark (`MoteKind.settles`) leaves it where each of its motes comes to rest: `Motes.burst` works out the point and time as it writes the row (`_land`, `mote_land`'s sum) and hands them to `Marks.settle`, which keeps it in a wheel of `SETTLE_SLOTS` slots of `SETTLE_SLOT` (1/30 s) and stamps it once its slot is past, at most `SETTLED_PER_FRAME` (512) a frame (the rest fade, `settles_faded`). A settle costs about a row, so it takes one of the frame's rows, and at most `Motes.SETTLE_ROWS` (24) settle a frame, what a quiet frame leaves kept up to `SETTLE_MOST` (128): a storm's flood leaves 24 specks a frame and skips the rest (`settles_skipped`), a blast in a quiet room settles all its chips. A settle carries its mote's serial, and the motes tell the layer which motes the ring wrote over (`forget_settles_before`), so no speck is left where nothing was seen to land. `Motes.settle_into` links the two, and Feel links them whenever either is made. **The ramp table** (`ramp_table.gd`) is Feel's, shared by the motes and the marks: a stamp's colour source may be a table row (a settled chip's), and its `surface` a second one (a dying body's art, else the room's walls) for a kind's `surface` parts, such as ichor.
- **Spell light** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P3a, P3b): shots, flashes, beams and torches light the floor, walls and bodies round them, in `spell_light.gd` (the spectacle's, `light()` for tests; null with the pause menu's "Dynamic lighting" switch off, once "Spell light", `SpellLightSetting` in core/settings through `spell_light_changed`, `set_spell_light`, and then no node of it exists). The batches are fed by the projectile manager (`mirror_batch`): each frame a light batch is handed the very buffer its projectile batch uploads, drawn with a quad of its own, so no row is written twice and the light costs four vertices a shot (sharing the batch's MultiMesh would have run the trails' whole mesh, hundreds of vertices a shot, again). **Applying it:** one rect over the whole world at `LIGHT_Z` (just under the motes: above the room and every body, under every spell, flash, telegraph and shot), drawn by `light_apply.gdshader`: the screen under it times the room's ambient and the colour of its dark (`RoomBase.ambient` and `RoomBase.shade`, kept by the spectacle from `room_entered`: 1.0 by default, so a room looks as it did until something glows; below it, darker) plus the light, in the light's hue, cut by the light's brightest channel into the steps `bands` sets (`set_bands`: 3 by default, dim with nothing drawn, lit, bright; 0 smooth, through the same light: the owner's pick between the two is open, `tests/screenshots/shot_spell_light_rooms.gd`). It reads the screen rather than blending with a multiply, because a multiply blend cannot brighten (its factor is clamped to 1 in an 8-bit target). **Flashes and beams light too (P3b), from the rows Feel already uploads:** at the end of `_step_flashes` Feel hands the flares', the explosions' and the arcs' buffers to the spectacle (`Spectacle.light_flashes`, on to `SpellLight.light_flashes`), and each is drawn again as light (how much each kind lights at its start, fading over its life: `FLASH_LIGHT` and `BLAST_LIGHT`, data by Feel's KIND_* codes, a test names each; chips, a dash, a status mark and a scorch give none); each live beam hands `Spectacle.light_beam` the path it just gave the beam's own shader, drawn by `light_beam.gdshader` as a band `BEAM_REACH` wide each side, rounded past its ends. **The room's lights are data (and the light painted into its art, the group `painted_light`, is hidden while Spell light is on, so a torch is lit once; S6 item 12):** any node in the group `light_spot` (a `Marker2D` in `content/rooms/crypt/decals/torch.tscn` at the flame; rooms carry no scripts) is a light, its reach, strength, flicker, art and tint as metadata read by `core/base/light_spot.gd`; its colour is its art's lightest common colour (`ArtPixels.colours_of`, the torch's flame: orange). The spots are written to one batch only when one enters or leaves the tree (`node_added`/`node_removed`), not every frame; they flicker in the shader. **A cue can light (S6, item 4):** a cue's `light` part (`Spectacle.play_light_part`, in the part table) is `SpellLight.flash`: a soft light round the cue's point, `strength` bright and fading over its `seconds`, in the colour a flash of the cue would have (an exact `color`, `plain`, `bare`, the cue's own colour, or its ramp, tint and cards), up to `CUE_LIGHTS` (64) at once, its rows the spell light's own (ROWS_CUE), written each frame while one lasts (`step_cues`). A cue with a light part lights by it alone: while its parts play (`_cue_unlit`) Feel marks the flashes it shows unlit (`_flash_unlit`), coding their age below their kind in the row, which spell light reads as no light (the flash shaders read the age either side, `coded_age`). The heal, the level-up and the revive light by their parts.

- **The room's air** (docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, B): what drifts over a whole room, its `RoomBase.air` (a `MoteSource`; none by default), given off by `air_motes.gd` (the spectacle's `air`, told the room's air by `use_room` and stepped in `step` with the room's bounds, `ProjectileManager.bounds`, and the view): over the part of the room within `VIEW_MARGIN` of the view, its rate for every `RoomBase.AIR_AREA` of it, each mote from a point there at random in one of its colours, as motes of many a frame (`often`), and never more than `RING_SHARE` (an eighth) of the motes' ring alive at once, whatever the "Particles" setting. - **Shadows** (docs/milestones/atmosphere.md, C): **light is blocked** by the room's standing walls and the props that block light. `light_blockers.gd` (the spectacle's `blockers`) works out up to `MAX` (32) boxes: the walls of the layout Flow builds (`room_built`, `RoomPlaces.wall_rects`) that stand inside the room (`standing`: not joined to its outer edge through other walls, so a floating slab's void edge never cuts its pools), then each prop whose data says `blocks_light` (the crypt's pillars), found in the actor table each frame, blocking by its ground's box while that blocks, with its shaft (`PropBase.shaft_size`, its art's box; else its hurt box) as the column drawn over it, its state kept by the actor's key and node (a new body under an old id, or one made again from a snapshot, comes as it stands). A prop comes with the room at full strength (and as it stands when the light is made anew, `refresh`); when it stops blocking (a pillar crumbles into rubble) its strength eases out over `FADE_SECONDS` (0.6 s) and its column shrinks down to its base, so the light comes back a band at a time as the shaft falls. Whenever the boxes change the spectacle hands them to the spell light (`SpellLight.set_blockers`, which knows nothing of rooms), and `light.gdshader` stops each light at them: a box blocks the way from a pixel to the light's middle unless it holds either end (a wall's own face; a light standing in a prop); a pixel in a column is lit as the floor in front of its base and its own box blocks only a light behind it (a shaft's face is lit from before and beside, not from behind); a spot row is blocked from where it stands on the floor (`SpellLight.write_spot_row`'s `ground`: a carried light from its body's feet, so a wizard behind a pillar lights round itself and its tip over a wall never opens it), a flash or a cue from its middle, moved in front of the base when it is on a shaft (a blast on the pillar's face). The vertex stage marks the boxes near each light (a bit each), so a light near none costs what it did. **The shots' and runes' lights are not blocked** (`SpellLight.SHOTS_BLOCKED`: thousands of rows, and blocking them tripled the light buffer's time on llvmpipe, `tests/benchmark/shot_gpu.gd -- --light --blockers`); flashes, cues, spots and carried lights are. A beam's light is not blocked either. `LightBlockers.lit_as` and `shade_between` are the shader's pick in GDScript (a test checks the shader's constants and pick line by line by text; headless runs compile no shader). **Bodies cast short hard shadows:** `body_shadows.gd` (the spectacle's `shadows`, made and freed with the spell light, at `MARK_Z` under every body) draws one row a body that walks (wizards, enemies, bosses; props stand on their own art, and the pillars block light instead) with `body_shadow.gdshader`: the flat disc it stands on (`RADIUS_PAD` past its footprint, `FLAT` deep, `DROP` below the feet, the Outline's colour at 0.45), swept up to `LENGTH` (3) px away from where the lights near it stand: each of the room's spots and the carried lights (handed over each frame, `begin_lights`, `add_lights`) pulls it away as much as it lights the feet, less what blocks it (`LightBlockers.shade_from`; a body behind a pillar from a candle casts nothing from it), and the pulls add up, so two lights either side share it and it never flips; a body's own carried light casts none (`CarriedLights.owners`). It eases at `LENGTH` per `EASE_SECONDS`, drawn in whole pixels (each pixel in or out by its middle), so it moves a pixel at a time; a body that comes takes its cast at once, a spawning one's shadow grows as its style's motion draws it out of its mark (`rise_share`, by its own `SpawnStyle.mark_end`), it shrinks as it falls into a hole, and a gone body's shrinks away where it was over `GONE_SECONDS`. Shots, flashes and cues cast no body shadow. While it is in the tree the shadows a body's own look draws (the staff's soft one, the group `LightSpot.DRAWN_SHADOW`) are hidden, and shown again with spell light off, as the painted light is. ## Review questions

A good PR here adds or tunes one juice preset (shake strength, flashes), stays readable with four players hitting at once, and never changes damage or outcomes (hit-stop is the game's: `gameplay/combat/hit_stop.gd`). Besides the general review:

- Is the flash readable with 4 players on screen? Does it avoid per-frame allocations and go through the flash batch (the glow look), never a node of its own?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
