# Feel

## Purpose

Hit-stop, screen shake and juice presets.

Built in M1 (see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Feel only reacts: it never changes damage or outcomes. Hit-stop is its only effect on timing; it is short and the same for every player.

**Freezes in co-op (melee I7, owner 2026-10-29) are not Feel's:** since online-ready N4 hit-stop is a rule of the game, `gameplay/combat/hit_stop.gd` (Combat's `HitStop`): a hit or a kill holds only its attacker, target and shot, a body rests after a stop, and only a downed player and a boss kill stand the whole game still, for a number of ticks (`Sim.hold`). Feel holds nothing and never touches `Engine.time_scale`; it draws what the holds do (the flash, the shake, the sound). A cue's `hold`, `hold_target`, `hold_shot` and `stop_game` are read by `HitStop`, not by Feel.

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/feel/feel.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. It is the autoload `Feel`. The setting `Feel.shake_enabled` turns screen shake off (the pause menu's "Screen shake" switch sets it through `screen_shake_changed` and saves the choice; on by default); hit-stop, particles and sound stay. The limit `Feel.PARTICLE_LIMIT` (48 live hit particles in all, across every player) is the one other code reads.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: none.
  - Listens to (caster-keyed events carry the actor id; Feel reads its ramp, seat and budget from the actor table, actors G3b): `damage_dealt`, `enemy_died`, `player_downed`, `spell_cast`, `staff_shown`, `possess_started`, `possess_ended`, `projectile_impact`, `form_shown`, `effect_slots_changed`, `mote_cap_changed`, `mote_density_changed`, `spell_light_changed`, `room_entered`, `screen_shake_changed`, `projectile_expired`, `reaction_triggered`, `discharge_arc`, `status_applied`, `hit_landed`, `heal_dealt` and `barrier_changed` (a barrier's bubble of motes, `barriers.gd`: the owner's pick of 2026-11-06, the mote shell; drawn by the motes' shader, rewritten every frame round the actor, its knocked-off motes thrown into the pool; a shot with shield charges gets the same shell, read from the projectile manager's guarded shots each frame, a wall's a capsule round its row, and its block, `shot_blocked` naming the shielded shot, plays `shield_block` and knocks its share off; the shell's size and motes follow what it guards). A still carrier card that names `look_motes` (the wall's stream rising off its seam, the pool's motes rising off its mire; the owner, 2026-11-09: "use motes for everything you can") has its shots give them off all the time while they stand, from the rows the projectile manager draws the card's body by (`rows_drawn`; `still_motes.gd`, the spectacle's), thinning while a shot comes and as it goes, in its colours, its infusions in turn and a share as its infusions' own kinds. The portals' gates are mote rifts (`portal_rings.gd`, the owner's pick of 2026-11-09): a dark lens, its lips streams of twinkling motes, a lip an infusion, loose motes spiralling in and thrown out.
- **Hits on characters:** when a shot hits an enemy or a player, it shows a spray of sparks (a flash, `KIND_SPRAY`) on along the way the shot flew (`projectile_impact` comes just before the damage and names the direction and the spot), by the hit actor (actors G8: the body's `Actor.id`, its ramp from `Actors.ramp_of`): in its own colours for an actor of a hat ramp (a player), in ember for one of the enemy ramp (or a body that is no actor), a zoom bigger for much more damage than 5. Damage with no impact (a contact hit) is a puff all round (`KIND_PUFF`). A kill turns the hit's spray into a bigger puff in the same slot.
- **Hit kinds (staffs B3):** `hit_landed` comes just before a hit's `damage_dealt` and names its kind; `FeelPreset.kind_looks` gives each kind (`shot`, `ray`, `swing`, `burst`, `reaction`, `status`) a default shake and sound id (a swing's: about 2 px of shake, the melee playground's; each kind's stop is `HitStop.KIND_STOP`), so a new card feels like something before it has its own sound (a `status` tick has none of them). An actor hit by its own spell (`hit_landed` whose caster is the hit actor's id; any actor, actors G8) also gets the cue `self_hit` (a ring in its own colour) on the frame of the hit, so a self-hit is never taken for an enemy's. `heal_dealt` plays the cue `heal`: a soft green ring, with no stop and no shake. `player_downed` plays the cue `player_downed` (its shake and sound; its game-wide stop is `HitStop`'s; it draws nothing, the event names no position). When `player_downed` arrives, Feel asks Combat for the seat's own body (`home_actor_of(seat)`, also while the seat drives another actor) and its `downed_note_of(actor)`, and raises that text ("downed by your own Blast") over that body (`Actors.node_of`), in its ramp's light shade, for `downed_note_seconds`; it shares the reaction name labels.
- **Walls and pillars:** a shot that ends on a wall sheds motes (below) back along where it came from and shows the shot's flash (see Flashes below): the kinds of its cards' look layers in its own ramp (`Motes.shed_kinds`, from `Projectiles.impact_look`, `impact_core` and `impact_tag`), and the room's surface kinds (`RoomBase.surface_kinds`, else every surface kind) in its wall colours (`RoomBase.surface_colors`, read on `room_entered`; none before a room). There is no other look for them: every particle is a mote (S3).
- **The spectacle** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle-2.md, S1): the motes, the floor marks and spell light below are `spectacle.gd`'s, a child of Feel (`spectacle()`) that owns them, the ramp table they share, their settings (each through its event: `mote_cap_changed`, `mote_density_changed`, `floor_marks_changed`, `spell_light_changed`) and the room they are in (`room_entered`: the walls' colours, the mark layer's fit). Feel says when (its hit, end, wall and death handlers call `shed_hit`, `shed_end`, `shed_on_wall`, `body_source` with what they already worked out, in the order they draw; `step` once a frame) and the spectacle says what. Feel keeps `motes()`, `marks()`, `light()`, `ramp_table()`, `use_motes` and the settings' `set_*` and values (`mote_cap`, `mote_density`, `floor_marks`, `spell_light`) as forwards. A new drawn-only system goes in the spectacle the same way: made in its `_ready`, its setting through its own event, its cue parts in `cue_parts`.
- **Motes** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1a): single-pixel particles with no game logic, in `mote.gd` (the spectacle's, `motes()` for tests; always there, S3). **Kinds are data:** one `MoteKind` file each in `content/motes/` (core/base/mote_kind.gd: how it flies and how a burst throws it), read from the registry when the pool is made; their numbers go to the shader as a texture, a row a kind (`kind_table`, `KIND_COLUMNS` texels: motion, sway, shape; `MAX_KINDS`, 256, so content grows with no shader edit) and their shapes as `shape_atlas` (each frame a 5x5 square, a kind's frames along its row), so a new kind, or a new look for one, is a new file. A shape's frames play over its life, loop, or are picked per mote, and a turning shape follows the way the mote flies (8 ways, read over the last 0.03 s; at rest, the way it was thrown). **Kinds come from the spells' cards** (spell-spectacle-2.md, S2): a kind names the cards it is the motes of by id (`MoteKind.cards`) or by look layer (`look_layers`); a card's kinds are those that name it, else its look layer's, and a shot sheds every kind of its spell's cards, its burst split between them (layer 0's kinds when they have none). The spell comes from the shot's tag: `card_kinds.gd` (the spectacle's `cards`) asks `Spells.profile(tag)` and keeps each spell's kinds by tag, checked against the spell once a frame (a tag a rebuilt staff reuses after 8 builds gets its new spell's) and each answer kept for the frame, so a hit costs about what `kinds_of_look` did; the tags come with the events (`Projectiles.impact_tag` while an impact is sent, `projectile_expired`'s tag, a reaction's or a death's shot, `Spells.cue_tag` while a blast's `cue_shown` is sent, `CueContext.tag`) and with each trail birth (`SPARK_TAG`, two floats). A shot with no spell (an enemy's) sheds by its look (`kinds_of_look`), and while no kind names a card nothing is looked up. A shed asks its kinds only when its hit sprays (`Motes.open_hit`, see below). A kind that names nothing is thrown only where something names it (a cue part, a room's `surface_kinds`). **No colour is kept here:** a spell's motes cool through the shot's own ramp, picked in the shader by the shot shaders' `look_of` (the caster's hat ramp for a plain core, else the palette material nearest the core, or the exact colour; `spell_source`); other colours (a room's walls) are rows of the ramp table (`table_source`, a texture of `RAMPS` rows of white-hot, light, base, shadow). A row is written once (`burst`, `throw`, `shed`) and `mote.gdshader` works out its whole flight from it and the time since its birth on Feel's clock: along the floor with drag, a fake height that rises or falls, one bounce, lying still, cooling step by step through its ramp (no partial alpha), then gone; `Motes.flight` is the same on the CPU. The pool is a ring of `mote_cap` rows (the pause menu's "Particles" slider through `mote_cap_changed`: `MoteCap` in core/settings, Minimal 2,048, Low 8,192, Medium 16,384 or High 32,768, no Off since S3 (a saved 0 loads as Minimal); the platform's by default, `set_mote_cap`; 0 makes no motes and no nodes): a new mote overwrites the oldest. It is cut into `Motes.CHUNK` (1,024) row chunks, one batch each (`Batches.multimesh()`, made the first time a mote lands in it); `step` uploads only the chunks written since the last frame and hides a chunk once its motes are all gone (by the longest life each of its bursts could give; at most `LIFE_MAX`, 4 s). A mote with no glow draws a quad only as big as its shape (`Motes.reach_of`, handed to the shader in `kind_sway.w`): most are a pixel or two, and their fragments were most of their GPU cost. At most `Motes.FRAME_ROWS` (256) are written between two steps and a burst past that is cut short; a shot's hit, end or wall hit (`shed`, `throw`: a few motes each, about 2.5 us a mote with its call in a fight) stops once only `Motes.MOMENT_ROWS` (128) are left, which the moments keep (a cue's or a reaction's `throw_part`, tens of motes in one burst at about 0.5 us each), so a blast in a storm still throws its motes. **A hit's motes are shared by staff** (the owner, 2026-11-05; `hit_shares.gd`): every hit is one spray (its shed, its wall's chips, its cue's `often` sparks, its damage's spray; Feel frames it with `Spectacle.begin_hit`/`end_hit`, the damage follows with `hold_hit`), in its caster's staff's group, and the frame's hit rows (`Motes.hit_rows()`) are shared out with `fair_level`, planned from the frame before: a lone cast's hits spray whole, a crowded staff's are a random subset that spray whole, and beside a crowd a staff not thinned may take `FRESH_ROWS` of the moments' rows. The table of the frame's rows by class is at `Motes.FRAME_ROWS`. The numbers are from the sandbox storm (P1c, docs/DECISIONS.md; `sandbox_storm.gd` prints the motes' CPU time, `mote_write.gd` the cost of each call, `shot_gpu.gd --motes` the GPU's). They draw at `MOTE_Z`, under the floor marks, beams, flashes and telegraphs. `mote_land(row)` gives where and when a mote comes to rest (INF for one that never does), for the floor marks (P2b). **One writer:** every row is written by `burst` at the named offsets `ROW_*` (`shed`, `throw`, `throw_part` and the trails' `give_off` all end there), so a mote is counted, kept drawn and settled the same way whatever threw it; `mote_land` and the settles share one sum (`_land`), and a test holds it, `flight` and the shader's `mote_flight` to the same lines. **Where motes come from** (P1b; Feel listens, nothing new is sent): a shot ending on a wall sheds its cards' kinds and the wall's chips (P1a); a hit on a body sheds the shot's kinds back along the way it came (`hit_mote_share` of their count, a preset number); a bolt or orbit whose life runs out puffs them on along its way (its cue `expire`: a `motes` part with a `share` of their count, S6, one of many a frame like a hit's); a reaction's cue throws a ring of the reacting shot's kinds (its `shot`, read while the event is sent) in the reaction's exact colour (S6: a `motes` part in the cue's own `color`, its count and `radius` times the cue's `power`); and a cue throws its `motes` parts (`CueBase`; `throw_part`: the cue's cards' kinds in its ramp, the surface kinds in the colours of what broke, or a named kind, out to `reach` times its size). A body that dies is what broke: its death cue's chips take the colours of its own art (the frame it shows, `RoomBase.colours_of_art`, read once per texture), and its spell motes the killing shot's cards. Every burst is times the "Particle amount" setting (`MoteDensity`: None, Half, Full, Double; `mote_density_changed`, `set_mote_density`; `Motes.density`, a share of a mote thrown that often so the average holds). With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown. **A shot's card particles in flight are motes too** (P1d; since S3 the only way they are drawn; a dash's are a `stream` cue part since 2026-11-05): while Feel has motes it sets `Projectiles.spark_motes`, and each frame Feel throws one mote for each particle the manager hands out (`spark_births`; `Motes.give_off`: a kind of the particle's look layer in its shot's spell, `CardKinds.layer_motes`, else layer 0's, given off back along the shot at `TRAIL_SPEED` of the kind's burst speed, in the shot's ramp; a one-mote `burst` each, read from the birth's named offsets `SPARK_*`), before its motes step. **Tails as motes** (S5 step 4; the owner, 2026-11-05: "if only the motes could look more like a tail"): with the tails off, the manager's births start with every flying shot's tail, laid by distance (`SPARK_TAIL`, core/README.md); `give_off` makes each a mote of its tail code's kind (`Motes.tail_kind_of`: the `MoteKind.tail` kinds in id order, A `tail`, B `tail_streak`, C `tail_long`; each shot picks one by its sequence, the owner's pick of 2026-11-05), and the spectacle gives the motes `tail_births_cap` more rows a frame while tails are laid, so a tail never takes the moments' rows. `tests/screenshots/shot_tail_motes.gd` shows held streams of atoms and fire bolts, each shot's tail in its own tail kind (a filmstrip). **The births are written natively** (`MoteKernel`, native/README.md; `Motes.native`): `key_births` names each birth's (spell tag, look kind) pair, `_give_off_native` looks up each pair's kinds once (the cards lookup is a script), the kernel picks a kind for each birth and writes its row as `burst` would, and the settling motes' marks come back in `_settle_out` for `Marks.settle`; about 0.27 us a birth against 2.4 in GDScript. The GDScript `give_off` stays as the reference and the fallback, and `test_native_motes.gd` holds the two to the byte. **What Feel draws itself gives off births too** (S6 item 10): a swing's sparks and card particles and a beam's splash and card particles, which their shot shaders drew until then, are births in the same format (`births.gd`, the spectacle's `births`: `add` with the SPARK_* offsets, `roll` for a rate's share of a frame, `emitting_layer` for a card particle's look kind), written while Feel draws them and given off by `Spectacle.step` after the trails' (`_drawn_motes`), so the native kernel writes them and they take their spell's cards' motes by its tag (`swing_tag`, `beam_tag`); at most `SPARK_BIRTHS_MAX` a frame. **A status's particles are its card's motes** (S6 item 11): `Spectacle.status_motes` throws a few a second round the body (see Statuses). **Flashes' sparks and dead bodies** (S3): a hit's spray and a puff (a hit with no way) are the cues `hit` and `hit_puff` (S6 2): the flash a flash part, its sparks a `motes` part of the kind `spark` marked `often` (one of many a frame, so they leave the moments' rows alone, as a `share` part does); a flash shown alone (`show_flash`) throws no sparks, so a cue that wants them names them (a reaction's, a conjure's, a death's puff), an explosion's fragments are its cue's surface motes and its scorch a mark, and a dead body breaks into motes of its own art (`Spectacle.break_body`, `Motes.crumble`: the preset's `death_mote_*` share of its art's pixels, `RoomBase.pixels_of_art`, each shown where its pixel was at the body's rest scale, in its art's colours, falling to its feet; a death is a moment and may take the frame's last rows). With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown.
- **Floor marks** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): scorches, cracks and frost that stay on the floor until the room ends, in `marks.gd` (the spectacle's, `marks()` for tests; null with the setting off). **The layer** is a `SubViewport` the size of the room's bounds (the projectile manager's `bounds()`), one texel a world pixel (the arena's 704x448 is about 1.2 MB of RGBA8, a 1344x784 room about 4.2 MB; at most `Marks.MAX_SIDE`, 4,096, a side), with `CLEAR_MODE_NEVER` and `UPDATE_DISABLED`: only a frame with new stamps renders it (`UPDATE_ONCE`), so a frame with none costs nothing. Its texture is one `Sprite2D` at `MARK_Z` (`RoomBase.FLOOR_Z + 1`: over the rooms' `Floor` sprite at `RoomBase.FLOOR_Z`, under every body at 0, the motes and Feel's floor flashes). **A cue's mark part** (`"part": "mark"`, core/base/cue_base.gd) queues a stamp (`stamp`); `step` draws up to `STAMPS_PER_FRAME` (512) of them in one batch inside the viewport (`Batches.multimesh()`, `mark.gdshader`; the batch shows only its frame's rows, so a later render never draws them twice) and the rest wait for later frames (at most `PENDING_MAX` wait; past that a stamp is counted in `left_out`). **Kinds are data** (`MarkKind`, content/marks): their forms and numbers go to the shader as `kind_form` and `kind_colour`; with `cards` a part stamps the marks of the cue's cards (`kinds_of_look`) instead of its own when they have one. **No colour is kept here:** a part's colour is the floor's own (`set_floor_colors`, from `RoomBase.floor_colors` on `room_entered`) or the spell's ramp, picked in the shader by `look_of` as the motes pick theirs (`Motes.spell_source`). **Off the walls:** the shader is clipped by `floor_mask`, the projectile manager's wall grid (`wall_image()`, one texel a wall cell). `room_entered` wipes the layer (one cleared render) and fits it to the room (`_fit_marks`); the "Floor marks" switch (`FloorMarks` in core/settings, `floor_marks_changed`, `set_floor_marks`) makes or frees it, and off there are no nodes. **Settled motes** (P2b): a mote kind that names a mark (`MoteKind.settles`) leaves it where each of its motes comes to rest: `Motes.burst` works out the point and time as it writes the row (`_land`, `mote_land`'s sum) and hands them to `Marks.settle`, which keeps it in a wheel of `SETTLE_SLOTS` slots of `SETTLE_SLOT` (1/30 s) and stamps it once its slot is past, at most `SETTLED_PER_FRAME` (512) a frame (the rest fade, `settles_faded`). A settle costs about a row, so it takes one of the frame's rows, and at most `Motes.SETTLE_ROWS` (24) settle a frame, what a quiet frame leaves kept up to `SETTLE_MOST` (128): a storm's flood leaves 24 specks a frame and skips the rest (`settles_skipped`), a blast in a quiet room settles all its chips. A settle carries its mote's serial, and the motes tell the layer which motes the ring wrote over (`forget_settles_before`), so no speck is left where nothing was seen to land. `Motes.settle_into` links the two, and Feel links them whenever either is made. **The ramp table** (`ramp_table.gd`) is Feel's, shared by the motes and the marks: a stamp's colour source may be a table row (a settled chip's), and its `surface` a second one (a dying body's art, else the room's walls) for a kind's `surface` parts, such as ichor.
- **Spell light** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P3a, P3b): shots, flashes, beams and torches light the floor, walls and bodies round them, in `spell_light.gd` (the spectacle's, `light()` for tests; null with the pause menu's "Spell light" switch off, `SpellLightSetting` in core/settings through `spell_light_changed`, `set_spell_light`, and then no node of it exists). A **light buffer** (a SubViewport a quarter of the view each way, cleared to black every frame, its canvas transform the view's scaled down, fitted before every draw by `follow`) holds one batch per projectile batch, drawn by `light.gdshader`: a soft light round each live shot in its glow colour (`look_of`), a dimmer one round a rune, every shot by one rule, the enemies' too; a trail left behind and a dash give none. The shader's `rows` (ROWS_* in spell_light.gd) says how a batch's rows read: trails, runes, Feel's flashes or the room's light spots. The batches are fed by the projectile manager (`mirror_batch`): each frame a light batch is handed the very buffer its projectile batch uploads, drawn with a quad of its own, so no row is written twice and the light costs four vertices a shot (sharing the batch's MultiMesh would have run the trails' whole mesh, hundreds of vertices a shot, again). **Applying it:** one rect over the whole world at `LIGHT_Z` (just under the motes: above the room and every body, under every spell, flash, telegraph and shot), drawn by `light_apply.gdshader`: the screen under it times the room's ambient (`RoomBase.ambient`, kept by the spectacle from `room_entered`: 1.0 by default, so a room looks as it did until something glows; the sandbox's is 0.85) plus the light, in the light's hue, cut by the light's brightest channel into the steps `bands` sets (`set_bands`: 3 by default, dim with nothing drawn, lit, bright; 0 smooth, through the same light: the owner's pick between the two is open, `tests/screenshots/shot_spell_light_rooms.gd`). It reads the screen rather than blending with a multiply, because a multiply blend cannot brighten (its factor is clamped to 1 in an 8-bit target). **Flashes and beams light too (P3b), from the rows Feel already uploads:** at the end of `_step_flashes` Feel hands the flares', the explosions' and the arcs' buffers to the spectacle (`Spectacle.light_flashes`, on to `SpellLight.light_flashes`), and each is drawn again as light (how much each kind lights at its start, fading over its life: `FLASH_LIGHT` and `BLAST_LIGHT`, data by Feel's KIND_* codes, a test names each; chips, a dash, a status mark and a scorch give none); each live beam hands `Spectacle.light_beam` the path it just gave the beam's own shader, drawn by `light_beam.gdshader` as a band `BEAM_REACH` wide each side, rounded past its ends. **The room's lights are data (and the light painted into its art, the group `painted_light`, is hidden while Spell light is on, so a torch is lit once; S6 item 12):** any node in the group `light_spot` (a `Marker2D` in `content/rooms/crypt/decals/torch.tscn` at the flame; rooms carry no scripts) is a light, its reach, strength, flicker, art and tint as metadata read by `core/base/light_spot.gd`; its colour is its art's lightest common colour (`RoomBase.colours_of_art`, the torch's flame: orange). The spots are written to one batch only when one enters or leaves the tree (`node_added`/`node_removed`), not every frame; they flicker in the shader. **A cue can light (S6, item 4):** a cue's `light` part (`Spectacle.play_light_part`, in the part table) is `SpellLight.flash`: a soft light round the cue's point, `strength` bright and fading over its `seconds`, in the colour a flash of the cue would have (an exact `color`, `plain`, `bare`, the cue's own colour, or its ramp, tint and cards), up to `CUE_LIGHTS` (64) at once, its rows the spell light's own (ROWS_CUE), written each frame while one lasts (`step_cues`). A cue with a light part lights by it alone: while its parts play (`_cue_unlit`) Feel marks the flashes it shows unlit (`_flash_unlit`), coding their age below their kind in the row, which spell light reads as no light (the flash shaders read the age either side, `coded_age`). The heal, the level-up and the revive light by their parts.
- **Live beams:** a beam lives its lifetime and moves with its caster, so feel draws `Spells`' live beams every frame from its plain queries (`beam_slots`, `beam_on`, `beam_origin`, `beam_bend`, `beam_end`, `beam_reach`, `beam_tint`, `beam_look`, `beam_since_hit`, `beam_fade`): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`LIVE_BEAMS`, 64, made once), the beam mesh (`beam_mesh()`, `BEAM_QUADS`: a light quad and a pixels quad for each piece of the path, which the shader places along it; its box is the whole world, so it is never culled), along the curve from the staff tip (its path, up to `LIVE_BEAM_POINTS` points, each with how far along the beam it is, in the shader's `beam_path`), pulled toward `beam_bend` (a homing beam bends onto its target), cut where it got, `BEAM_WIDTH` (16 px) wide and drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_beam.gdshader` (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a one-pixel white-hot core in the ramp of its element, a rim that breathes along it, motes of light running down it, flares at the staff and where it lands and a glow; lightning's is jagged, storm's sways, fire's wavers, void's core is dark. Its splash where it lands and its cards' particles are motes (S6 item 10, `_beam_motes`: `BEAM_SPLASH_RATE` sparks a second thrown back off its end while it is more than `BEAM_SPLASH_FADE` whole, and each card particle, 4 and 2 a card kind, `BEAM_PARTICLE_RATE` times a second from a random point along it, out to a side; a beam shown whole gives off the same). Feel hands it its clock (`sim_time`, so it moves), its ends (`beam_start`, `beam_end`: the flares are upright crosses on the world's pixels) and the shader draws it `BEAM_LEAD` past each end so the flares are whole; its lines are one pixel per step whatever its angle. It flares on each hit (`beam_flare`, `beam_flare_share`) and flickers (`beam_flicker_*`), both as `beam_swell` (its rim and glow, never its pixels), and over the end of its life its carrier gives (`beam_fade`, `Carrier.fade`: a beam's last quarter, `SpellLimits.BEAM_FADE`, when it no longer zaps) it dissolves into pixels (the shader's `beam_fade`); `beam_alpha` is how strongly it glows.
- **Slams (melee I8):** a slam's wind-up shows only on the staff (it rises glowing; no ring on the floor, which distracted from the strike, owner 2026-10-30); `form_shown` `slam` (its impact) plays the cue its card's impact look names (`cue`: the slam's is `slam`), as big as its ring: a white-hot star (no expanding ring, which looked too uniform) and the ground struck.

Feel's own player is `flash` (`_play_flash`), the spectacle's are `motes` and `mark` (`Spectacle.cue_parts`). **A cue is read once for playing** (S6 2, `cue_plan.gd`, `_plan_of`): the first time it plays, each part's player is looked up and the part prepared by its type's `prepare` (`add_cue_part(type, player, prepare)`: a flash into `CuePlan.Flash`, a motes part into `Spectacle.MotesPart`, typed fields), and every later play calls the players with what was prepared, reading no key (a cue is on every hit and impact; a cue with no weight skips the holds, shake and sound; `forget_cues` and a new player read them again); a part of a type nothing plays is skipped, counted (`unknown_cue_parts`) and warned of once. **A body shows the looks of a few hits a frame** (`body_looks.gd`, `BodyLooks.PER_BODY` hits of one staff; each staff apart): a hit past them shows no spray, impact, sparks, squash or sound, and its damage follows its impact's answer. **Screen shake never piles up** (`_shake`): every shake, a hit's or a cue's, raises the screen's to its own. **A hit's look that shows nothing is not played** (`_hides_hit`, the plan's `shows_always`, `has_flashes`, `has_spray_parts`): a hit past the frame's share of hit flashes skips its flashes and one whose motes do not spray skips the parts that need a spray, so a cue made only of those is skipped whole for such a hit (most of a crowd's hits). A part type says which of its parts need a spray with `add_cue_part`'s fourth argument (`Spectacle.motes_need_spray`: the `often` motes); a type without one always plays, so a new kind of part is never skipped by mistake. A new kind of part is a player added to the table and a row in `CueBase.PART_TYPES` (its keys; its own checks in `CueBase._type_problems`). The `flash` parts are flashes (`CUE_FLASHES` maps each name to its flash kind, the pixels a step of its zoom is for a part's `reach`, and its seconds), each sized fixed (`zoom`) or from the cue's `size` (`reach`), lasting its `seconds` or the cue's `size` (`lasts_size`: a telegraph), in the caster's ramp with its tint and cards, an exact `color`, or `plain`, facing the cue's way, `back`, `right` or `up`, and left out with particles off when `particles`; then its weight: `hold` (the caster) and `hold_target` local stops, `hold_shot`, `stop_game`, `shake` and `kick` (`down`, `away` from the caster's body, `back`, `aim`; both off with the shake setting) and its `sound`. Cues are read from the registry once and kept (`cue_of`).

- **Swing trails (melee I4):** a swing lives a wind-up and a sweep and moves with its caster's hand, so feel draws `Spells`' live swings every frame from their plain queries (`swing_slots`, `swing_on`, `swing_born`, `swing_actor`, `swing_origin`, `swing_dir`, `swing_side`, `swing_arc`, `swing_reach`, `swing_age`, `swing_wind`, `swing_sweep`, `swing_turned`, `swing_tint`, `swing_look`, `swing_tag`) in `swing_trails.gd` (a child of Feel; `swing_trails()` for tests): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`TRAILS_START`, 16, more when more swing at once), the swing mesh (`swing_mesh()`, `SwingTrails.SWING_QUADS`; its box is the whole world), drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_swing.gdshader` (the owner's pick A3 from the melee mockup): a brief one-pixel line at the reach that follows the tip and cools through the ramp (in the caster's ramp, every fourth pixel in its infusion's), and its light. - **Deflect and parry (melee I5, the owner's picks from the melee mockup):** on `shot_deflected` the shot flashes big and white where it turned, the cue `deflect`: a `burst` (the white-hot cross) in the deflecting actor's ramp, aimed the way it now flies (Feel reads it from the event's `shot` and passes it as the cue's direction), zoom 4, bigger than a hit's spray or a landing flare (1 to 3), and its sound. A swing's block is a parry: `shot_blocked` while `Projectiles.impact_form` is `swing` (`block_shot` tells the carrier's form; a beam's is `beam`, a shot's shield none) plays the cue `parry`: a `burst` four-point star in exactly Spark base `#f2c230`, zoom 5, and a `hold` of the parrying actor alone (0.06 s, a local stop; the stopped shot is gone). Other blocks show nothing of their own.

Its path is a `stream` part of `dash_start` (the owner, 2026-10-28: the dash look went the aim's way, not the player's): `streams.gd` (the spectacle's `streams`) follows the caster's anchor (`Projectiles.get_anchor`) from `form_shown` until the dash's `carrier_ended` (its caster and tag; `Spectacle.end_streams`) and gives off its speed streaks (the `streak` mote kind, `MoteKind.dash_path`) and its cards' particles (each a shot's rate for its layer; fire's embers and frost's flecks from its feet) as births back along the way the player really goes. The spell runtime's `cue_tag` is the dash's tag while its `form_shown` and `carrier_ended` are sent, so its cues throw its cards' motes (S2). The player's own afterimages stay `gameplay/player`'s. Each player has `FORMS_PER_PLAYER` (24, the most shots one cast makes) slots, and the enemies one share of 24 between them (`ENEMY_FORMS`, staffs F7), 120 in all; a player's oldest slot is the one taken over, so nobody pushes out another player's beam and no enemy a player's. ## Example to copy

`gameplay/feel/presets/punchy.tres`, a `FeelPreset` (`presets/feel_preset.gd`): shake strength and decay, the sizes and seconds of the flashes, and the sound ids (the point moments' numbers are their cues', `content/cues/`). ## Review questions

A good PR here adds or tunes one juice preset (shake strength, flashes), stays readable with four players hitting at once, and never changes damage or outcomes (hit-stop is the game's: `gameplay/combat/hit_stop.gd`). Besides the general review:

- Is the flash readable with 4 players on screen? Does it avoid per-frame allocations and go through the flash batch (the glow look), never a node of its own?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
