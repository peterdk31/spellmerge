# Feel

## Purpose

Hit-stop, screen shake and juice presets.

Built in M1 (see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Feel only reacts: it never changes damage or outcomes. Hit-stop is its only effect on timing; it is short and the same for every player.

**Freezes in co-op (melee I7, owner 2026-10-29):** a hit or a kill is a *local* stop: the attacker, the target and the shot hold still for `hit_stop_seconds` (`Actors.hold`, which `Actor.step` obeys, and `Projectiles.freeze_shot` for the shot of the hit, the `shot` its event names) while everyone else, the other players and every other shot, runs on. A body holds again only after `hit_stop_rest` (kept per actor), so a stream of hits cannot freeze it for ever; a kill and a reaction hold their body at once. `Engine.time_scale` drops (`stop_left`, `hit_stop_scale`) only for a downed player and a boss kill (`EnemyBase.boss`).

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/feel/feel.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. It is the autoload `Feel`. The setting `Feel.shake_enabled` turns screen shake off (the pause menu's "Screen shake" switch sets it through `screen_shake_changed` and saves the choice; on by default); hit-stop, particles and sound stay. The limit `Feel.PARTICLE_LIMIT` (48 live hit particles in all, across every player) is the one other code reads.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: none.
  - Listens to (caster-keyed events carry the actor id; Feel reads its ramp, seat and budget from the actor table, actors G3b): `damage_dealt`, `enemy_died`, `player_downed`, `spell_cast`, `staff_shown`, `possess_started`, `possess_ended`, `projectile_impact`, `form_shown`, `effect_slots_changed`, `mote_cap_changed`, `mote_density_changed`, `spell_light_changed`, `room_entered`, `screen_shake_changed`, `caster_move_finished`, `projectile_expired`, `reaction_triggered`, `discharge_arc`, `status_applied`, `hit_landed` and `heal_dealt`.
- **Hits on characters:** when a shot hits an enemy or a player, it shows a spray of sparks (an effect, `KIND_SPRAY`) on along the way the shot flew (`projectile_impact` comes just before the damage and names the direction and the spot), by the hit actor (actors G8: the body's `Actor.id`, its ramp from `Actors.ramp_of`): in its own colours for an actor of a hat ramp (a player), in ember for one of the enemy ramp (or a body that is no actor), a zoom bigger for much more damage than 5. Damage with no impact (a contact hit) is a puff all round (`KIND_PUFF`). A kill turns the hit's spray into a bigger puff in the same slot.
- **Hit kinds (staffs B3):** `hit_landed` comes just before a hit's `damage_dealt` and names its kind; `FeelPreset.kind_looks` gives each kind (`shot`, `ray`, `swing`, `burst`, `reaction`, `status`) a default hit-stop, shake and sound id (a swing's: a 0.05 s local stop and about 2 px of shake, the melee playground's), so a new card feels like something before it has its own sound (a `status` tick has none of them). An actor hit by its own spell (`hit_landed` whose caster is the hit actor's id; any actor, actors G8) also gets the cue `self_hit` (a ring in its own colour) on the frame of the hit, so a self-hit is never taken for an enemy's. `heal_dealt` plays the cue `heal`: a soft green ring, with no stop and no shake. `player_downed` plays the cue `player_downed` (the game-wide stop, shake and sound; it draws nothing, the event names no position). When `player_downed` arrives, Feel asks Combat for the seat's own body (`home_actor_of(seat)`, also while the seat drives another actor) and its `downed_note_of(actor)`, and raises that text ("downed by your own Blast") over that body (`Actors.node_of`), in its ramp's light shade, for `downed_note_seconds`; it shares the reaction name labels.
- **Walls and pillars:** a shot that ends on a wall sheds motes (below) back along where it came from and shows the shot's effect (see Effects below): the kinds of its cards' look layers in its own ramp (`Motes.shed`, from `Projectiles.impact_look` and `impact_core`), and the surface kinds in the room's wall colours (`RoomBase.surface_colors`, read on `room_entered`; none before a room). With the "Particles" setting off, the chips effect instead (`KIND_CHIPS`: Stone pixels that fall as they fly, with no glow).
- **Motes** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P1a): single-pixel particles with no game logic, in `mote.gd` (a child of Feel, `motes()` for tests; null with the setting off). **Kinds are data:** one `MoteKind` file each in `content/motes/` (core/base/mote_kind.gd: how it flies and how a burst throws it), read from the registry when the pool is made; their numbers go to the shader as `kind_motion`, `kind_sway` and `kind_shape` (`MAX_KINDS`, 16) and their shapes as `shape_atlas` (each frame a 5x5 square, a kind's frames along its row), so a new kind, or a new look for one, is a new file. A shape's frames play over its life, loop, or are picked per mote, and a turning shape follows the way the mote flies (8 ways, read over the last 0.03 s; at rest, the way it was thrown). **Kinds come from the spells:** a kind names the cards' look layers it is the motes of; a shot sheds every kind of its cards' layers (`kinds_of_look`; layer 0's kinds when none has one), its burst split between them, so a combination sheds a mix and a new card brings its motes through the look layer it already has. **No colour is kept here:** a spell's motes cool through the shot's own ramp, picked in the shader by the shot shaders' `look_of` (the caster's hat ramp for a plain core, else the palette material nearest the core, or the exact colour; `spell_source`); other colours (a room's walls) are rows of the ramp table (`table_source`, a texture of `RAMPS` rows of white-hot, light, base, shadow). A row is written once (`burst`, `throw`, `shed`) and `mote.gdshader` works out its whole flight from it and the time since its birth on Feel's clock: along the floor with drag, a fake height that rises or falls, one bounce, lying still, cooling step by step through its ramp (no partial alpha), then gone; `Motes.flight` is the same on the CPU. The pool is a ring of `mote_cap` rows (the pause menu's "Particles" slider through `mote_cap_changed`: `MoteCap` in core/settings, Off, 8,192, 16,384 or 32,768; the platform's by default, `set_mote_cap`; 0 makes no motes and no nodes): a new mote overwrites the oldest. It is cut into `Motes.CHUNK` (1,024) row chunks, one batch each (`Batches.multimesh()`, made the first time a mote lands in it); `step` uploads only the chunks written since the last frame and hides a chunk once its motes are all gone (by the longest life each of its bursts could give; at most `LIFE_MAX`, 4 s). A mote with no glow draws a quad only as big as its shape (`Motes.reach_of`, handed to the shader in `kind_sway.w`): most are a pixel or two, and their fragments were most of their GPU cost. At most `Motes.FRAME_ROWS` (256) are written between two steps and a burst past that is cut short; a shot's hit, end or wall hit (`shed`, `throw`: a few motes each, about 2.5 us a mote with its call in a fight) stops once only `Motes.MOMENT_ROWS` (128) are left, which the moments keep (a cue's or a reaction's `throw_part`, tens of motes in one burst at about 0.5 us each), so a blast in a storm still throws its motes. The numbers are from the sandbox storm (P1c, docs/DECISIONS.md; `sandbox_storm.gd` prints the motes' CPU time, `mote_write.gd` the cost of each call, `trail_gpu.gd --motes` the GPU's). They draw at `MOTE_Z`, under the floor marks, beams, flashes and telegraphs. `mote_land(row)` gives where and when a mote comes to rest (INF for one that never does), for the floor marks (P2b). **One writer:** every row is written by `burst` at the named offsets `ROW_*` (`shed`, `throw`, `throw_part` and the trails' `give_off` all end there), so a mote is counted, kept drawn and settled the same way whatever threw it; `mote_land` and the settles share one sum (`_land`), and a test holds it, `flight` and the shader's `mote_flight` to the same lines. **Where motes come from** (P1b; Feel listens, nothing new is sent): a shot ending on a wall sheds its cards' kinds and the wall's chips (P1a); a hit on a body sheds the shot's kinds back along the way it came (`hit_mote_share` of their count, a preset number); a bolt or orbit whose life runs out puffs them on along its way (`expire_mote_share`); a reaction throws a ring of the reacting shot's kinds (its `shot`, read while the event is sent) in the reaction's exact colour (`reaction_mote_count`, `reaction_mote_reach`, times its power); and a cue throws its `motes` parts (`CueBase`; `throw_part`: the cue's cards' kinds in its ramp, the surface kinds in the colours of what broke, or a named kind, out to `reach` times its size). A body that dies is what broke: its death cue's chips take the colours of its own art (the frame it shows, `RoomBase.colours_of_art`, read once per texture), and its spell motes the killing shot's cards. Every burst is times the "Particle amount" setting (`MoteDensity`: None, Half, Full, Double; `mote_density_changed`, `set_mote_density`; `Motes.density`, a share of a mote thrown that often so the average holds). With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown. **A shot's card particles in flight are motes too** (P1d): while Feel has motes it sets `Projectiles.spark_motes`, the trails drop their particle slots, and each frame Feel throws one mote for each particle the manager hands out (`spark_births`; `Motes.give_off`: a kind of the particle's look layer, else layer 0's, given off back along the shot at `TRAIL_SPEED` of the kind's burst speed, in the shot's ramp; a one-mote `burst` each, read from the birth's named offsets `SPARK_*`), before its motes step; with `particles_enabled` off the trails keep no particle slots and none are thrown. With `particles_enabled` off (the stress benchmark) none are thrown.
- **Floor marks** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P2a): scorches, cracks and frost that stay on the floor until the room ends, in `marks.gd` (a child of Feel, `marks()` for tests; null with the setting off). **The layer** is a `SubViewport` the size of the room's bounds (the projectile manager's `bounds()`), one texel a world pixel (the arena's 704x448 is about 1.2 MB of RGBA8, the horde's 1344x784 about 4.2 MB; at most `Marks.MAX_SIDE`, 4,096, a side), with `CLEAR_MODE_NEVER` and `UPDATE_DISABLED`: only a frame with new stamps renders it (`UPDATE_ONCE`), so a frame with none costs nothing. Its texture is one `Sprite2D` at `MARK_Z` (`RoomBase.FLOOR_Z + 1`: over the rooms' `Floor` sprite at `RoomBase.FLOOR_Z`, under every body at 0, the motes and Feel's floor flashes). **A cue's mark part** (`"part": "mark"`, core/base/cue_base.gd) queues a stamp (`stamp`); `step` draws up to `STAMPS_PER_FRAME` (512) of them in one batch inside the viewport (`Batches.multimesh()`, `mark.gdshader`; the batch shows only its frame's rows, so a later render never draws them twice) and the rest wait for later frames (at most `PENDING_MAX` wait; past that a stamp is counted in `left_out`). **Kinds are data** (`MarkKind`, content/marks): their forms and numbers go to the shader as `kind_form` and `kind_colour`; with `cards` a part stamps the marks of the cue's cards (`kinds_of_look`) instead of its own when they have one. **No colour is kept here:** a part's colour is the floor's own (`set_floor_colors`, from `RoomBase.floor_colors` on `room_entered`) or the spell's ramp, picked in the shader by `look_of` as the motes pick theirs (`Motes.spell_source`). **Off the walls:** the shader is clipped by `floor_mask`, the projectile manager's wall grid (`wall_image()`, one texel a wall cell). `room_entered` wipes the layer (one cleared render) and fits it to the room (`_fit_marks`); the "Floor marks" switch (`FloorMarks` in core/settings, `floor_marks_changed`, `set_floor_marks`) makes or frees it, and off there are no nodes. **Settled motes** (P2b): a mote kind that names a mark (`MoteKind.settles`) leaves it where each of its motes comes to rest: `Motes.burst` works out the point and time as it writes the row (`_land`, `mote_land`'s sum) and hands them to `Marks.settle`, which keeps it in a wheel of `SETTLE_SLOTS` slots of `SETTLE_SLOT` (1/30 s) and stamps it once its slot is past, at most `SETTLED_PER_FRAME` (512) a frame (the rest fade, `settles_faded`). A settle costs about a row, so it takes one of the frame's rows, and at most `Motes.SETTLE_ROWS` (24) settle a frame, what a quiet frame leaves kept up to `SETTLE_MOST` (128): a storm's flood leaves 24 specks a frame and skips the rest (`settles_skipped`), a blast in a quiet room settles all its chips. A settle carries its mote's serial, and the motes tell the layer which motes the ring wrote over (`forget_settles_before`), so no speck is left where nothing was seen to land. `Motes.settle_into` links the two, and Feel links them whenever either is made. **The ramp table** (`ramp_table.gd`) is Feel's, shared by the motes and the marks: a stamp's colour source may be a table row (a settled chip's), and its `surface` a second one (a dying body's art, else the room's walls) for a kind's `surface` parts, such as ichor.
- **Spell light** (docs/milestones/spell-spectacle.md, P3a): shots light the floor, walls and bodies round them, in `spell_light.gd` (a child of Feel, `light()` for tests; null with the pause menu's "Spell light" switch off, `SpellLightSetting` in core/settings through `spell_light_changed`, `set_spell_light`, and then no node of it exists). A **light buffer** (a SubViewport a quarter of the view each way, cleared to black every frame, its canvas transform the view's scaled down, fitted before every draw by `follow`) holds one batch per projectile batch, drawn by `light.gdshader`: a soft light round each live shot in its glow colour (`look_of`), a dimmer one round a rune; a trail left behind and a dash give none. The batches are fed by the projectile manager (`mirror_batch`): each frame a light batch is handed the very buffer its projectile batch uploads, drawn with a quad of its own, so no row is written twice and the light costs four vertices a shot (sharing the batch's MultiMesh would have run the trails' whole mesh, hundreds of vertices a shot, again). **Applying it:** one rect over the whole world at `LIGHT_Z` (just under the motes: above the room and every body, under every spell, flash, telegraph and shot), drawn by `light_apply.gdshader`: the screen under it times the ambient (1.0 for now, so the room looks as it did until something glows; a room's own ambient is P3b) plus the light, cut into three steps by the light's brightest channel (dim: nothing drawn; lit; bright), in the light's hue. It reads the screen rather than blending with a multiply, because a multiply blend cannot brighten (its factor is clamped to 1 in an 8-bit target). Blasts, beams, effects and torches give no light yet (P3b).
- **Live beams:** a beam lives its lifetime and moves with its caster, so feel draws `Spells`' live beams every frame from its plain queries (`beam_slots`, `beam_on`, `beam_origin`, `beam_bend`, `beam_end`, `beam_reach`, `beam_tint`, `beam_look`, `beam_since_hit`, `beam_fade`): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`LIVE_BEAMS`, 64, made once), the beam mesh (`beam_mesh()`, `BEAM_QUADS`: small quads the shader places along the path, PARTICLES ARE GEOMETRY in `shot_beam.gdshader`; its box is the whole world, so it is never culled), along the curve from the staff tip (its path, up to `LIVE_BEAM_POINTS` points, each with how far along the beam it is, in the shader's `beam_path`), pulled toward `beam_bend` (a homing beam bends onto its target), cut where it got, `BEAM_WIDTH` (16 px) wide and drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_beam.gdshader` (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a one-pixel white-hot core in the ramp of its element, a rim that breathes along it, motes running down it, flares at the staff and where it lands, its cards' particles and a glow; lightning's is jagged, storm's sways, fire's wavers, void's core is dark. Feel hands it its clock (`sim_time`, so it moves), its ends (`beam_start`, `beam_end`, `beam_end_dir`: the flares are upright crosses on the world's pixels, sparks splash back where it lands) and the shader draws it `BEAM_LEAD` past each end so the flares are whole; its lines are one pixel per step whatever its angle. It flares on each hit (`beam_flare`, `beam_flare_share`) and flickers (`beam_flicker_*`), both as `beam_swell` (its rim and glow, never its pixels), and over the end of its life its carrier gives (`beam_fade`, `Carrier.fade`: a beam's last quarter, `SpellLimits.BEAM_FADE`, when it no longer zaps) it dissolves into pixels (the shader's `beam_fade`); `beam_alpha` is how strongly it glows.
- **Slams (melee I8):** a slam's wind-up shows only on the staff (it rises glowing; no ring on the floor, which distracted from the strike, owner 2026-10-30); `form_shown` `slam` (its impact) plays the cue its card's impact look names (`cue`: the slam's is `slam`), as big as its ring: a white-hot star (no expanding ring, which looked too uniform) and the ground struck.
- **Cues** (`play_cue(id, caster, at, dir, size, tint, look, target)`, public; `cue_shown` calls it): a moment's look and weight as data, one `CueBase` `.tres` per cue in `content/cues/` (core/base/cue_base.gd says every key), so a new moment is a new file. Its `parts` are flashes (`CUE_EFFECTS` maps each name to its effect kind, the pixels a step of its zoom is for a part's `reach`, and its seconds), each sized fixed (`zoom`) or from the cue's `size` (`reach`), lasting its `seconds` or the cue's `size` (`lasts_size`: a telegraph), in the caster's ramp with its tint and cards, an exact `color`, or `plain`, facing the cue's way, `back`, `right` or `up`, and left out with particles off when `particles`; then its weight: `hold` (the caster) and `hold_target` local stops, `hold_shot`, `stop_game`, `shake` and `kick` (`down`, `away` from the caster's body, `back`, `aim`; both off with the shake setting) and its `sound`. Cues are read from the registry once and kept (`cue_of`).
- **The ground struck** (cue `quake`): its mark on the floor (`KIND_QUAKE`, in a batch of its own at `FLOOR_Z`, under the enemies: cracks out to the reach that open white-hot and cool to dark stone over `QUAKE_SECONDS`, a glow, rubble thrown up that lands and lies, dust rolling out flat), stone chips, the caster held a moment (hit or miss), a shake and a punch down the screen. Any carrier plays a cue with `"cue": id` in its card's `impact_look` (`FormBase`): where its shot ends on no body (a wall, the end of its way), as big as its `radius`, and where a carrier with no shot ends (`carrier_ended`: a slam's strike, as big as its ring; a teleport or dash landing; a rain falling; a decoy or familiar going); and one in its `cast_look` where the carrier shows itself (`form_shown`: a nova's ring, a rain's telegraph, where a teleport leaves, each end of a swap, a decoy or familiar appearing). The slam's cue (`slam`) is its star and this ground strike.
- **Deaths** (cues `enemy_death` and `boss_death`): `enemy_died` starts the death animation (below), then plays `enemy_death` (a white-hot ring, the killer and the shot held for the kill, shake, sound) or, for an enemy whose `EnemyBase.boss` is true, `boss_death` (the same and the game-wide stop). The puff stays in code: the killing blow's own spray at that spot becomes a puff `death_particle_size` big (one slot, and the hit's sound gives way to the death's), else a new puff in `enemy_hit_color` after the ring.
- **Explosions** (cues `blast` and `fireball`): the parts `shell` (the ragged shell, `KIND_SHELL`), `fireball` (the rising fireball, `KIND_FIREBALL`), `debris` (`KIND_DEBRIS`: twelve hot chunks and twelve of stone flung past the radius, up to twice it, landing and lying until they crumble) and `scorch` (`KIND_SCORCH`, under the bodies at `FLOOR_Z`: one frame of the radius lit, then a dark burn with embers), each at `reach` 1 of the radius; a shake and a punch `away` from the caster (down the screen without one). The bodies caught hold through their own `burst` hits. `blast` is the blast modifier's (core/spells sends it wherever a blast goes off; owner's picks 2026-10-29); `fireball` is for anything else that explodes (an exploding barrel, not built yet). All of them are drawn by `shot_blast.gdshader` (`blast_mesh`: a light and 24 pieces), the radius as the zoom x 2 (up to 126 px).
- **Swing trails (melee I4):** a swing lives a wind-up and a sweep and moves with its caster's hand, so feel draws `Spells`' live swings every frame from their plain queries (`swing_slots`, `swing_on`, `swing_born`, `swing_actor`, `swing_origin`, `swing_dir`, `swing_side`, `swing_arc`, `swing_reach`, `swing_age`, `swing_wind`, `swing_sweep`, `swing_tint`, `swing_look`) in `swing_trails.gd` (a child of Feel; `swing_trails()` for tests): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`TRAILS_START`, 16, more when more swing at once), the swing mesh (`swing_mesh()`, `SwingTrails.SWING_QUADS`; its box is the whole world), drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_swing.gdshader` (the owner's pick A3 from the melee mockup): a brief one-pixel line at the reach that follows the tip and cools through the ramp (in the caster's ramp, every fourth pixel in its infusion's), pixel sparks shed outward as the tip passes, slowing and cooling, and its card particles. The sweep's start is kept on Feel's clock from the swing's age every frame (a local stop that holds the swing holds its trail), and a trail stays `TRAIL_SECONDS` (0.6) past its sweep, after the swing itself is over, where it ended. A hit of a swing is the usual spray along the way the sweep moved there (its streaks) and the `swing` kind's stop and shake.
- **Deflect and parry (melee I5, the owner's picks from the melee mockup):** on `shot_deflected` the shot flashes big and white where it turned, the cue `deflect`: a `burst` (the white-hot cross) in the deflecting actor's ramp, aimed the way it now flies (Feel reads it from the event's `shot` and passes it as the cue's direction), zoom 4, bigger than a hit's spray or a landing flare (1 to 3), and its sound. Its longer tail needs nothing from feel: it flies 1.7x faster and its tail is a pixel per pixel of travel. A swing's block is a parry: `shot_blocked` while `Projectiles.impact_form` is `swing` (`block_shot` tells the carrier's form; a beam's is `beam`, a shot's shield none) plays the cue `parry`: a `burst` four-point star in exactly Spark base `#f2c230`, zoom 5, and a `hold` of the parrying actor alone (0.06 s, a local stop; the stopped shot is gone). Other blocks show nothing of their own.
- **Beams, nova rings and dashes:** `form_shown` (from `core/spells`, with the cast's tint and look) makes a short beam flash (a chain jump: a beam mesh of the same shader along a path of two points, `BEAM_TEXTURE_HEIGHT` (32 px) wide, for as far as it got), a nova's ring (the cue `nova`: `KIND_RING_OUT`, 120 px across, in the element's ramp) or a dash's dust where it left (an effect, `KIND_DASH`, `DASH_SECONDS`; the owner, 2026-10-28: the dash look went the aim's way, not the player's, so its streaks and cards are now the caster's trail, drawn by the projectile manager where the player really goes). `caster_move_finished` for a dash lands it: a ring in the player's colours where the player stopped, an arc from where it left for lightning, and a small flare of its element for an infused dash. The player's own afterimages stay `gameplay/player`'s. Each player has `FORMS_PER_PLAYER` (24, the most shots one cast makes) slots, and the enemies one share of 24 between them (`ENEMY_FORMS`, staffs F7), 120 in all; a player's oldest slot is the one taken over, so nobody pushes out another player's beam and no enemy a player's. An enemy's (NO_PLAYER) beam, ring and dash look like a player's, in the enemy ramp where a player's colour would show. A rain (`form_shown` `&"rain"`, a player's or an enemy's) plays the cue `telegraph`: a ring closing on its point for the seconds until it falls, so a rain is never unseen, and where it falls `rainfall`. The nova's ring and the telegraphs are cues their cards' cast looks name (`form_shown`'s `length` as their size; no table in Feel); beams and dashes keep state of their own and stay in code. The preset names the beam's art and seconds (`beam_*`). Feel gives the manager only the bolt art (the shared `texture`) and the hostile art.
- **Effects** (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a player's cast flash and the flare where a player's shot ends or hits are drawn by the shots' own shaders (core/README.md, "The shot shaders"), one `MultiMeshInstance2D` per shader: the flares, rings, sprays, puffs, chips and dash dust by `shot_effect.gdshader` (`_effect_node`; each effect an instance of the effect mesh, `effect_mesh()`, `EFFECT_QUADS`: its light, its card particles, its sparks, a poof's clouds and its flash as small quads the shader places, PARTICLES ARE GEOMETRY there), the arcs by `shot_arc.gdshader` (`_arc_node`), the statuses by `shot_status.gdshader` (`_status_node`); the three share `effect_slots` (a setting, the pause menu's "Effect slots" slider through `effect_slots_changed`: `EffectSlots` in core/settings: 64 to 1024 in steps of 64, `set_effect_slots`; 256 by default, about 850 bursts a second; a new effect takes the oldest slot when all are busy), each with its age beside its kind. An effect takes the ramp and look of the shot it comes from (`Projectiles.impact_core` and `impact_look` while the impact is sent; a beam's and a cast's are white, the player's ramp), so it glows and throws that shot's card particles. A cast is a small white-hot cross and sparks along the aim (`CAST_SECONDS`); a flying or orbiting shot whose life runs out pops (`projectile_expired`, by `Projectiles.impact_body`: a small flare of its own, `POP_ZOOM`, `POP_SECONDS`); where a shot lands, a shrinking cross and sparks thrown back that cool as they fly (`BURST_SECONDS`); a rune goes off in a dotted flash, a diamond ring widening to twice its size and sparks all round (`RUNE_BURST_SECONDS`); a column of light pulled up off a spot (`KIND_RISE`, `RISE_SECONDS`: it swells, thins to a line and lifts off the top, motes drawn in and up it, a flat ring gathering in) or struck down onto it (`KIND_FALL`, `FALL_SECONDS`: a streak falls, the column flashes and thins away, a cross, a flat ring thrown out, motes flung along the floor), the cues' `rise` and `fall` (teleport, swap, rain, decoy, familiar). `show_effect(kind, at, dir, core, player, look, zoom, seconds)` starts one; `live_effects`, `effects_of` and `effect_dress` read them for tests.
- **Form looks:** `spell_cast` names the form, and the impact the form of the shot itself (`Projectiles.impact_form`). Both looks are data on the carrier's card (`FormBase.cast_look` and `impact_look`, dictionaries; staffs B6b); Feel asks the registry (folder `spells`) once per card id and keeps the answer. The effects read `ring` (a nova's cast rings out, a rune's or an orbit's gathers in, by the sign of `grow`; a nova's flare adds a ring) and `size` (the zoom of a flare); a rune's flare is always its burst, and a card with no look gets the plain cast flash and burst. A beam opens extra wide (`beam_flare` for the first `beam_flare_share` of its life), thins, and flickers (`beam_flicker_rate`, `beam_flicker_depth`). The bodies in flight are not part of this yet. An enemy's shot (NO_PLAYER) lands like any spell (staffs F7: one look for every spell), with its own core and cards, in the enemy ramp where a player's colour would show; the ember burst is gone. A new form brings its looks in its own `.tres`; the preset has none.
- **Possess (actors G9, G8):** `possess_started` shows a ring closing on the possessed actor's body (`KIND_RING_IN`, `POSSESS_ZOOM`, `POSSESS_SECONDS`) in the driver's ramp and plays the sound `possess`; `possess_ended` a ring going out from it (when its body is still there).
- **Colours:** Feel keeps none of its own: a ramp's base and light shades are the projectile manager's (`RAMP_COLORS`, `ramp_light`).
- **The poof (living staffs H4):** `staff_shown` (another staff of an actor went on show: the personality swap) gives a puff with the `POOF` look bit along the staff (`POOF_SECONDS` 0.18, zoom `POOF_ZOOM`, its middle `POOF_ALONG` from the hand along the aim), moving with the actor: bone clouds that swell to their biggest at 35% (where `StaffLook` changes the body and face) and dot away, with a few sparks in the actor's ramp. A followed effect sits at an offset from its node (`_effect_offset`: a status rises above it, a poof sits on the staff).
- **Reactions:** `reaction_triggered` gives a puff (`KIND_PUFF`) bigger than a hit's and a ring (`KIND_RING_OUT`), both in exactly the reaction's colour (the `EXACT` look bit: the ramp is made from the colour, not the nearest palette material, so the reactions stay apart), a local stop on the reaction's caster at least as long as a death's, shake, the name rising and the sound `reaction_<id>`; `FeelPreset.reaction_power` scales them by id. The sound and shake come at most every `REACTION_REPEAT` seconds per reaction. A discharge (`discharge_arc`) shows a jagged line of lightning (`KIND_ARC`, re-rolled 30 times a second, burning out over `arc_seconds`) from the struck enemy to each enemy zapped, in the colour of `discharge`.
- **Statuses:** a character with burn, chill, freeze, shock, hex or static shows it as an effect that follows it (`KIND_STATUS`): its element's particles round it (`FeelPreset.status_looks`: `look`, burn fire's embers, chill and freeze frost's flecks, shock storm's gusts, hex void's pull, static lightning's sparks) and a pulsing glow in its `color`; the first of `status_order` shows. `status_applied` starts it; Feel reads `Combat.status_left` (the `combat` var) every `STATUS_POLL` seconds and ends it with the status. At most `STATUS_SLOTS` (16) characters show one. A crowd of other effects can take its slot; the next step shows it again.
- **What it shows:** hit-stop, shake, the effects above, a white flash and squash on the hit node's `Sprite` child (name your sprite `Sprite` to get it), a white-hot ring where an enemy dies and a death animation (a copy of the dead enemy's `Sprite`, drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_sprite.gdshader`'s `dissolve`: white-hot, then pixel by pixel gone, never stretched or faded; the enemy is freed at once), a cast flash at the staff tip, and a small camera kick on a cast (how the caster's body moves is its carrier card's `staff_pose`, drawn by its look: gameplay/actor's `ActorLook`). Every hit sprite reacts, however many at once (owner, 2026-10-31: no limit on interactions): one shared flash material, bookkeeping that grows to the largest crowd seen and is reused, a reaction found by its actor id. A body without a `Sprite` (a horde swarmer) can name the node that draws it under the `SPRITE_GIVER` metadata; its `sprite_of(body)` gives the death animation a Sprite2D to copy. `body_replaced` (the swarm's promotion) moves a shown status to the new body. If the sprite's owner changes its scale during a squash, Feel takes that as the new size to return to. Sounds are asked from `core/registry` and stay silent when missing.
- Areas never call each other directly; they talk through events. [gameplay/README.md](../README.md) has the rules every sub-area shares.

## Example to copy

`gameplay/feel/presets/punchy.tres`, a `FeelPreset` (`presets/feel_preset.gd`): hit-stop length, shake strength and decay, the sizes and seconds of the effects, and the sound ids (the point moments' numbers are their cues', `content/cues/`). Copy it, change the numbers and load it as `Feel.preset` before Feel's `_ready` (the art is read once, then). On `reaction_triggered` feel shows a puff and a ring in exactly the reaction's colour (`reaction_colors` by id; keep the colours far apart), its name rising from the spot, a little shake, and plays the sound `reaction_<id>` (silent until Audio adds it). A preset only tunes reactions; it has no field that touches damage. It also names the art the projectile manager is given (`bolt_texture`), so a re-skin is a preset, never an edit to `feel.gd`. Tests: `tests/gameplay/feel/test_feel.gd`.

## Review questions

A good PR here adds or tunes one juice preset (hit-stop length, shake strength), stays readable with four players hitting at once, and never changes damage or outcomes, with hit-stop kept short and the same for every player. Besides the general review:

- Is the effect readable with 4 players on screen? Does it avoid per-frame allocations and go through the effect batch (the glow look), never a node of its own?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
