# Feel

## Purpose

Hit-stop, screen shake and juice presets.

Built in M1 (see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Feel only reacts: it never changes damage or outcomes. Hit-stop is its only effect on timing; it is short and the same for every player.

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/feel/feel.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. It is the autoload `Feel`. The setting `Feel.shake_enabled` turns screen shake off (kept in memory; saving it belongs to `core/save`, not built yet); hit-stop, particles and sound stay. The limit `Feel.PARTICLE_LIMIT` (48 live hit particles in all, across every player) is the one other code reads.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: none.
  - Listens to: `damage_dealt`, `enemy_died`, `player_downed`, `spell_cast`, `wizard_cast`, `projectile_impact`, `form_shown`, `effect_slots_changed`, `combo_cast`, `reaction_triggered`, `discharge_arc`, `status_applied`, `hit_landed` and `heal_dealt`.
- **Hits on characters:** when a shot hits an enemy or a wizard, it shows a spray of sparks (an effect, `KIND_SPRAY`) on along the way the shot flew (`projectile_impact` comes just before the damage and names the direction and the spot), in the ember ramp for an enemy and in the wizard's own player ramp for a wizard (the node in group `players` with an `index`), a zoom bigger for much more damage than 5. Damage with no impact (a contact hit) is a puff all round (`KIND_PUFF`). A kill turns the hit's spray into a bigger puff in the same slot.
- **Hit kinds (staffs B3):** `hit_landed` comes just before a hit's `damage_dealt` and names its kind; `FeelPreset.kind_looks` gives each kind (`shot`, `ray`, `burst`, `reaction`, `status`) a default hit-stop, shake and sound id, so a new card feels like something before it has its own sound (a `status` tick has none of them). A wizard hit by their own spell (`hit_landed` with the same player and target) also gets a ring in `self_hit_color` on the frame of the hit, so a self-hit is never taken for an enemy's. `heal_dealt` shows a soft ring in `heal_color`, with no stop and no shake. When `player_downed` arrives, Feel asks `Combat.downed_note(index)` and raises that text ("downed by your own Blast") over the wizard for `downed_note_seconds`; it shares the reaction name labels.
- **Walls and pillars:** a shot that ends on a wall knocks stone chips back along where it came from (`KIND_CHIPS`: Stone pixels that fall as they fly, with no glow) and shows the shot's effect (see Effects below).
- **Live beams:** a beam lives its lifetime and moves with its caster, so feel draws `Spells`' live beams every frame from its plain queries (`beam_slots`, `beam_on`, `beam_origin`, `beam_bend`, `beam_end`, `beam_reach`, `beam_tint`, `beam_look`, `beam_since_hit`, `beam_left`): one pooled `Line2D` each (`LIVE_BEAMS`, 64, made once; `LIVE_BEAM_POINTS` points), along the curve from the staff tip, pulled toward `beam_bend` (a homing beam bends onto its target), cut where it got, `BEAM_WIDTH` (16 px) wide and drawn by the shot shader's beam mode (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a one-pixel white-hot core in the ramp of its element, a rim that breathes along it, motes running down it, flares at the staff and where it lands, its cards' particles and a glow; lightning's is jagged, storm's sways, fire's wavers, void's core is dark. It flares on each hit (`beam_flare`, `beam_flare_share`) and flickers (`beam_flicker_*`), both as `beam_swell` (its rim and glow, never its pixels), and over the last quarter of its life it dissolves into pixels (`beam_fade`); `beam_alpha` is how strongly it glows.
- **Beams, nova rings and dashes:** `form_shown` (from `core/spells`, with the cast's tint and look) makes a short beam flash (a chain jump or a fissure: a `Sprite2D` whose region is the beam, drawn by the same beam mode for as far as it got), a nova's ring (an effect, `KIND_RING_OUT`, `nova_ring_size` across, in the element's ramp) or a dash's trail (an effect, `KIND_DASH`, along its way as far as the walls let it: speed streaks in the player's colours behind the head, dust where it left, a ring where it lands, its cards' particles along the way and, for lightning, an arc from end to end; `DASH_SECONDS`). The wizard's own afterimages stay `gameplay/player`'s. Each player has `FORMS_PER_PLAYER` (24, the most shots one cast makes) slots, 96 in all; a player's oldest slot is the one taken over, so nobody pushes out another player's beam. A negative player index (an enemy) is ignored. The preset names the art and the seconds (`beam_*`, `nova_*`). Feel gives the manager only the bolt art (the shared `texture`) and the hostile art.
- **Effects** (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a player's cast flash and the flare where a player's shot ends or hits are drawn by the shots' own shader (`core/projectiles/shot_core.gdshader`, kinds `KIND_BURST` to `KIND_CAST`), in one `MultiMeshInstance2D` of `effect_slots` (a setting, the pause menu's "Effect slots" slider through `effect_slots_changed`: `EffectSlots` in core/settings: 64 to 1024 in steps of 64, `set_effect_slots`; 256 by default, about 850 bursts a second; a new effect takes the oldest slot when all are busy), each with its age beside its kind. An effect takes the ramp and look of the shot it comes from (`Projectiles.impact_core` and `impact_look` while the impact is sent; a beam's and a cast's are white, the player's ramp), so it glows and throws that shot's card particles. A cast is a small white-hot cross and sparks along the aim (`CAST_SECONDS`); where a shot lands, a shrinking cross and sparks thrown back that cool as they fly (`BURST_SECONDS`); a rune goes off in a dotted flash, a diamond ring widening to twice its size and sparks all round (`RUNE_BURST_SECONDS`). `show_effect(kind, at, dir, core, player, look, zoom, seconds)` starts one; `live_effects`, `effects_of` and `effect_dress` read them for tests.
- **Form looks:** `spell_cast` names the form, and the impact the form of the shot itself (`Projectiles.impact_form`). Both looks are data on the carrier's card (`FormBase.cast_look` and `impact_look`, dictionaries; staffs B6b); Feel asks the registry (folder `spells`) once per card id and keeps the answer. The effects read `ring` (a nova's cast rings out, a rune's or an orbit's gathers in, by the sign of `grow`; a nova's flare adds a ring) and `size` (the zoom of a flare); a rune's flare is always its burst, and a card with no look gets the plain cast flash and burst. A beam opens extra wide (`beam_flare` for the first `beam_flare_share` of its life), thins, and flickers (`beam_flicker_rate`, `beam_flicker_depth`). The bodies in flight are not part of this yet. An enemy's shot (negative index) always ends in one ember mark turned on its corner, never a player's flare. A new form brings its looks in its own `.tres`; the preset has none.
- **Combo marks:** `combo_cast` (from `core/spells`, only for a cast that is more than a plain shot) puts a flat mark at the spot of the cast (the staff tip, rounded to whole pixels; it stays there and does not follow the wizard) for `FeelPreset.combo_mark_seconds` in the element's colour: a ring of ticks (`boosted`), chevrons toward the nearest of four directions to the aim (`on_hit`), a diamond (`on_expire`), a dashed ring with a ticking hand (`timer`), a ring over a diamond (`stacked`). The pictures are built in code (`combo_marks.gd`, one flat white picture per class), drawn at their own size then at 2x, one sprite per player (`COMBO_SLOTS`, a new cast takes over). They are sprites, outside `PARTICLE_LIMIT`.
- **Reactions:** `reaction_triggered` gives a puff (`KIND_PUFF`) bigger than a hit's and a ring (`KIND_RING_OUT`), both in exactly the reaction's colour (the `EXACT` look bit: the ramp is made from the colour, not the nearest palette material, so the reactions stay apart), a hit-stop longer than a death's, shake, the name rising and the sound `reaction_<id>`; `FeelPreset.reaction_power` scales them by id. The sound and shake come at most every `REACTION_REPEAT` seconds per reaction. A discharge (`discharge_arc`) shows a jagged line of lightning (`KIND_ARC`, re-rolled 30 times a second, burning out over `arc_seconds`) from the struck enemy to each enemy zapped, in the colour of `discharge`.
- **Statuses:** a character with burn, chill, freeze, shock, hex or static shows it as an effect that follows it (`KIND_STATUS`): its element's particles round it (`FeelPreset.status_looks`: `look`, burn fire's embers, chill and freeze frost's flecks, shock storm's gusts, hex void's pull, static lightning's sparks) and a pulsing glow in its `color`; the first of `status_order` shows. `status_applied` starts it; Feel reads `Combat.status_left` (the `combat` var) every `STATUS_POLL` seconds and ends it with the status. At most `STATUS_SLOTS` (16) characters show one. A crowd of other effects can take its slot; the next step shows it again.
- **What it shows:** hit-stop, shake, the effects above, a white flash and squash on the hit node's `Sprite` child (name your sprite `Sprite` to get it), a white-hot ring where an enemy dies and a death animation (a copy of the dead enemy's `Sprite`, drawn by the shot shader's `dissolve`: white-hot, then pixel by pixel gone, never stretched or faded; the enemy is freed at once), a cast flash at the staff tip, a thin-and-tall squash of the caster's `Sprite` (from `wizard_cast`) and a small camera kick on a cast. At most `REACT_SLOTS` (16) sprites react at once; past that a hit skips its flash and squash. If the sprite's owner changes its scale during a squash, Feel takes that as the new size to return to. Sounds are asked from `core/registry` and stay silent when missing.
- Areas never call each other directly; they talk through events. [gameplay/README.md](../README.md) has the rules every sub-area shares.

## Allowed files

`.gd`, `.tres` and `.gdshader`, plus `.uid`, `.import` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. The text shared by all sub-areas is in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Steps

The first preset is `presets/punchy.tres`. Follow the steps and test layout in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Example to copy

`gameplay/feel/presets/punchy.tres`, a `FeelPreset` (`presets/feel_preset.gd`): hit-stop length, shake strength and decay, the sizes and seconds of the effects, and the sound ids. Copy it, change the numbers and load it as `Feel.preset` before Feel's `_ready` (the art is read once, then). On `reaction_triggered` feel shows a puff and a ring in exactly the reaction's colour (`reaction_colors` by id; keep the colours far apart), its name rising from the spot, a little shake, and plays the sound `reaction_<id>` (silent until Audio adds it). A preset only tunes reactions; it has no field that touches damage. It also names the art the projectile manager is given (`bolt_texture`, `hostile_texture`), so a re-skin is a preset, never an edit to `feel.gd`. Tests: `tests/gameplay/feel/test_feel.gd`.

## Review questions

A good PR here adds or tunes one juice preset (hit-stop length, shake strength), stays readable with four players hitting at once, and never changes damage or outcomes, with hit-stop kept short and the same for every player. Besides the general review:

- Is the effect readable with 4 players on screen? Does it avoid per-frame allocations and go through the effect batch (the glow look), never a node of its own?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
