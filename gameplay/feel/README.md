# Feel

## Purpose

Hit-stop, screen shake and juice presets.

Built in M1 (see "Gameplay sub-areas" in docs/PLAN.md).

Feel only reacts: it never changes damage or outcomes. Hit-stop is its only effect on timing; it is short and the same for every player.

**Freezes in co-op (melee I7, owner 2026-10-29):** a hit or a kill is a *local* stop: the attacker, the target and the shot hold still for `hit_stop_seconds` (`Actors.hold`, which `Actor.step` obeys, and `Projectiles.freeze_shot` for the shot of the hit being sent, `event_slot`) while everyone else, the other players and every other shot, runs on. A body holds again only after `hit_stop_rest` (kept per actor), so a stream of hits cannot freeze it for ever; a kill and a reaction hold their body at once. `Engine.time_scale` drops (`stop_left`, `hit_stop_scale`) only for a downed player and a boss kill (`EnemyBase.boss`).

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/feel/feel.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. It is the autoload `Feel`. The setting `Feel.shake_enabled` turns screen shake off (the pause menu's "Screen shake" switch sets it through `screen_shake_changed` and saves the choice; on by default); hit-stop, particles and sound stay. The limit `Feel.PARTICLE_LIMIT` (48 live hit particles in all, across every player) is the one other code reads.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: none.
  - Listens to (caster-keyed events carry the actor id; Feel reads its ramp, seat and budget from the actor table, actors G3b): `damage_dealt`, `enemy_died`, `player_downed`, `spell_cast`, `staff_shown`, `possess_started`, `possess_ended`, `projectile_impact`, `form_shown`, `effect_slots_changed`, `screen_shake_changed`, `caster_move_finished`, `projectile_expired`, `reaction_triggered`, `discharge_arc`, `status_applied`, `hit_landed` and `heal_dealt`.
- **Hits on characters:** when a shot hits an enemy or a player, it shows a spray of sparks (an effect, `KIND_SPRAY`) on along the way the shot flew (`projectile_impact` comes just before the damage and names the direction and the spot), by the hit actor (actors G8: the body's `Actor.id`, its ramp from `Actors.ramp_of`): in its own colours for an actor of a hat ramp (a player), in ember for one of the enemy ramp (or a body that is no actor), a zoom bigger for much more damage than 5. Damage with no impact (a contact hit) is a puff all round (`KIND_PUFF`). A kill turns the hit's spray into a bigger puff in the same slot.
- **Hit kinds (staffs B3):** `hit_landed` comes just before a hit's `damage_dealt` and names its kind; `FeelPreset.kind_looks` gives each kind (`shot`, `ray`, `swing`, `burst`, `reaction`, `status`) a default hit-stop, shake and sound id (a swing's: a 0.05 s local stop and about 2 px of shake, the melee playground's), so a new card feels like something before it has its own sound (a `status` tick has none of them). An actor hit by its own spell (`hit_landed` whose caster is the hit actor's id; any actor, actors G8) also gets the cue `self_hit` (a ring in its own colour) on the frame of the hit, so a self-hit is never taken for an enemy's. `heal_dealt` plays the cue `heal`: a soft green ring, with no stop and no shake. `player_downed` plays the cue `player_downed` (the game-wide stop, shake and sound; it draws nothing, the event names no position). When `player_downed` arrives, Feel asks Combat for the seat's own body (`home_actor_of(seat)`, also while the seat drives another actor) and its `downed_note_of(actor)`, and raises that text ("downed by your own Blast") over that body (`Actors.node_of`), in its ramp's light shade, for `downed_note_seconds`; it shares the reaction name labels.
- **Walls and pillars:** a shot that ends on a wall knocks stone chips back along where it came from (`KIND_CHIPS`: Stone pixels that fall as they fly, with no glow) and shows the shot's effect (see Effects below).
- **Live beams:** a beam lives its lifetime and moves with its caster, so feel draws `Spells`' live beams every frame from its plain queries (`beam_slots`, `beam_on`, `beam_origin`, `beam_bend`, `beam_end`, `beam_reach`, `beam_tint`, `beam_look`, `beam_since_hit`, `beam_left`): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`LIVE_BEAMS`, 64, made once), the beam mesh (`beam_mesh()`, `BEAM_QUADS`: small quads the shader places along the path, PARTICLES ARE GEOMETRY in `shot_beam.gdshader`; its box is the whole world, so it is never culled), along the curve from the staff tip (its path, up to `LIVE_BEAM_POINTS` points, each with how far along the beam it is, in the shader's `beam_path`), pulled toward `beam_bend` (a homing beam bends onto its target), cut where it got, `BEAM_WIDTH` (16 px) wide and drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_beam.gdshader` (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a one-pixel white-hot core in the ramp of its element, a rim that breathes along it, motes running down it, flares at the staff and where it lands, its cards' particles and a glow; lightning's is jagged, storm's sways, fire's wavers, void's core is dark. Feel hands it its clock (`sim_time`, so it moves), its ends (`beam_start`, `beam_end`, `beam_end_dir`: the flares are upright crosses on the world's pixels, sparks splash back where it lands) and the shader draws it `BEAM_LEAD` past each end so the flares are whole; its lines are one pixel per step whatever its angle. It flares on each hit (`beam_flare`, `beam_flare_share`) and flickers (`beam_flicker_*`), both as `beam_swell` (its rim and glow, never its pixels), and over the last quarter of its life it dissolves into pixels (`beam_fade`); `beam_alpha` is how strongly it glows.
- **Slams (melee I8):** `form_shown` `slam_mark` (a slam winding up) shows a ring closing on where it will strike (`KIND_RING_IN`, `RAIN_MARK_ZOOM`) for its wind-up, so nobody is hit by a slam they could not see coming; `slam` (its impact) a white-hot star (no expanding ring, which looked too uniform) and, as its card's impact look says `quake`, the ground struck out to its radius.
- **Cues** (`play_cue(id, caster, at, dir, size, tint, look, target)`, public; `cue_shown` calls it): a moment's look and weight as data, one `CueBase` `.tres` per cue in `content/cues/` (core/base/cue_base.gd says every key), so a new moment is a new file. Its `parts` are flashes (`CUE_EFFECTS` maps each name to its effect kind, the pixels a step of its zoom is for a part's `reach`, and its seconds), each sized fixed (`zoom`) or from the cue's `size` (`reach`), in the caster's ramp with its tint and cards, an exact `color`, or `plain`, facing the cue's way, `back`, `right` or `up`, and left out with particles off when `particles`; then its weight: `hold` (the caster) and `hold_target` local stops, `hold_shot`, `stop_game`, `shake` and `kick` (`down`, `away` from the caster's body, `back`, `aim`; both off with the shake setting) and its `sound`. Cues are read from the registry once and kept (`cue_of`).
- **The ground struck** (cue `quake`): its mark on the floor (`KIND_QUAKE`, in a batch of its own at `FLOOR_Z`, under the enemies: cracks out to the reach that open white-hot and cool to dark stone over `QUAKE_SECONDS`, a glow, rubble thrown up that lands and lies, dust rolling out flat), stone chips, the caster held a moment (hit or miss), a shake and a punch down the screen. Any carrier gets it with `"quake": n` in its card's `impact_look` (`FormBase`): where its shot ends on no body (a wall, the end of its way) the ground is struck out to its `radius` times n; a slam strikes at its impact, out to its ring times n.
- **Deaths** (cues `enemy_death` and `boss_death`): `enemy_died` starts the death animation (below), then plays `enemy_death` (a white-hot ring, the killer and the shot held for the kill, shake, sound) or, for an enemy whose `EnemyBase.boss` is true, `boss_death` (the same and the game-wide stop). The puff stays in code: the killing blow's own spray at that spot becomes a puff `death_particle_size` big (one slot, and the hit's sound gives way to the death's), else a new puff in `enemy_hit_color` after the ring.
- **Explosions** (cues `blast` and `fireball`): the parts `shell` (the ragged shell, `KIND_SHELL`), `fireball` (the rising fireball, `KIND_FIREBALL`), `debris` (`KIND_DEBRIS`: twelve hot chunks and twelve of stone flung past the radius, up to twice it, landing and lying until they crumble) and `scorch` (`KIND_SCORCH`, under the bodies at `FLOOR_Z`: one frame of the radius lit, then a dark burn with embers), each at `reach` 1 of the radius; a shake and a punch `away` from the caster (down the screen without one). The bodies caught hold through their own `burst` hits. `blast` is the blast modifier's (core/spells sends it wherever a blast goes off; owner's picks 2026-10-29); `fireball` is for anything else that explodes (an exploding barrel, not built yet). All of them are drawn by `shot_blast.gdshader` (`blast_mesh`: a light and 24 pieces), the radius as the zoom x 2 (up to 126 px).
- **Swing trails (melee I4):** a swing lives a wind-up and a sweep and moves with its caster's hand, so feel draws `Spells`' live swings every frame from their plain queries (`swing_slots`, `swing_on`, `swing_born`, `swing_actor`, `swing_origin`, `swing_dir`, `swing_side`, `swing_arc`, `swing_reach`, `swing_age`, `swing_wind`, `swing_sweep`, `swing_tint`, `swing_look`) in `swing_trails.gd` (a child of Feel; `swing_trails()` for tests): one pooled `MeshInstance2D` each (`TRAILS_START`, 16, more when more swing at once), the swing mesh (`swing_mesh()`, `SwingTrails.SWING_QUADS`; its box is the whole world), drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_swing.gdshader` (the owner's pick A3 from the melee mockup): a brief one-pixel line at the reach that follows the tip and cools through the ramp (in the caster's ramp, every fourth pixel in its infusion's), pixel sparks shed outward as the tip passes, slowing and cooling, and its card particles. The sweep's start is kept on Feel's clock from the swing's age every frame (a local stop that holds the swing holds its trail), and a trail stays `TRAIL_SECONDS` (0.6) past its sweep, after the swing itself is over, where it ended. A hit of a swing is the usual spray along the way the sweep moved there (its streaks) and the `swing` kind's stop and shake.
- **Deflect and parry (melee I5, the owner's picks from the melee mockup):** on `shot_deflected` the shot flashes big and white where it turned, the cue `deflect`: a `burst` (the white-hot cross) in the deflecting actor's ramp, aimed the way it now flies (Feel reads it from `Projectiles.event_slot`, the shot, and passes it as the cue's direction), zoom 4, bigger than a hit's spray or a landing flare (1 to 3), and its sound. Its longer tail needs nothing from feel: it flies 1.7x faster and its tail is a pixel per pixel of travel. A swing's block is a parry: `shot_blocked` while `Projectiles.impact_form` is `swing` (`block_shot` tells the carrier's form; a beam's is `beam`, a shot's shield none) plays the cue `parry`: a `burst` four-point star in exactly Spark base `#f2c230`, zoom 5, and a `hold` of the parrying actor alone (0.06 s, a local stop; the stopped shot is gone). Other blocks show nothing of their own.
- **Beams, nova rings and dashes:** `form_shown` (from `core/spells`, with the cast's tint and look) makes a short beam flash (a chain jump or a fissure: a beam mesh of the same shader along a path of two points, `BEAM_TEXTURE_HEIGHT` (32 px) wide, for as far as it got), a nova's ring (an effect, `KIND_RING_OUT`, `nova_ring_size` across, in the element's ramp) or a dash's dust where it left (an effect, `KIND_DASH`, `DASH_SECONDS`; the owner, 2026-10-28: the dash look went the aim's way, not the player's, so its streaks and cards are now the caster's trail, drawn by the projectile manager where the player really goes). `caster_move_finished` for a dash lands it: a ring in the player's colours where the player stopped, an arc from where it left for lightning, and a small flare of its element for an infused dash. The player's own afterimages stay `gameplay/player`'s. Each player has `FORMS_PER_PLAYER` (24, the most shots one cast makes) slots, and the enemies one share of 24 between them (`ENEMY_FORMS`, staffs F7), 120 in all; a player's oldest slot is the one taken over, so nobody pushes out another player's beam and no enemy a player's. An enemy's (NO_PLAYER) beam, ring and dash look like a player's, in the enemy ramp where a player's colour would show. A rain (`form_shown` `&"rain"`, a player's or an enemy's) shows a ring closing on its point (`KIND_RING_IN`, `RAIN_MARK_ZOOM`) for the seconds until it falls, so a rain is never unseen. The preset names the art and the seconds (`beam_*`, `nova_*`). Feel gives the manager only the bolt art (the shared `texture`) and the hostile art.
- **Effects** (the glow look, 2026-10-27): a player's cast flash and the flare where a player's shot ends or hits are drawn by the shots' own shaders (core/README.md, "The shot shaders"), one `MultiMeshInstance2D` per shader: the flares, rings, sprays, puffs, chips and dash dust by `shot_effect.gdshader` (`_effect_node`; each effect an instance of the effect mesh, `effect_mesh()`, `EFFECT_QUADS`: its light, its card particles, its sparks, a poof's clouds and its flash as small quads the shader places, PARTICLES ARE GEOMETRY there), the arcs by `shot_arc.gdshader` (`_arc_node`), the statuses by `shot_status.gdshader` (`_status_node`); the three share `effect_slots` (a setting, the pause menu's "Effect slots" slider through `effect_slots_changed`: `EffectSlots` in core/settings: 64 to 1024 in steps of 64, `set_effect_slots`; 256 by default, about 850 bursts a second; a new effect takes the oldest slot when all are busy), each with its age beside its kind. An effect takes the ramp and look of the shot it comes from (`Projectiles.impact_core` and `impact_look` while the impact is sent; a beam's and a cast's are white, the player's ramp), so it glows and throws that shot's card particles. A cast is a small white-hot cross and sparks along the aim (`CAST_SECONDS`); a bolt or orbit shot whose life runs out pops (`projectile_expired`: a small flare of its own, `POP_ZOOM`, `POP_SECONDS`); where a shot lands, a shrinking cross and sparks thrown back that cool as they fly (`BURST_SECONDS`); a rune goes off in a dotted flash, a diamond ring widening to twice its size and sparks all round (`RUNE_BURST_SECONDS`). `show_effect(kind, at, dir, core, player, look, zoom, seconds)` starts one; `live_effects`, `effects_of` and `effect_dress` read them for tests.
- **Form looks:** `spell_cast` names the form, and the impact the form of the shot itself (`Projectiles.impact_form`). Both looks are data on the carrier's card (`FormBase.cast_look` and `impact_look`, dictionaries; staffs B6b); Feel asks the registry (folder `spells`) once per card id and keeps the answer. The effects read `ring` (a nova's cast rings out, a rune's or an orbit's gathers in, by the sign of `grow`; a nova's flare adds a ring) and `size` (the zoom of a flare); a rune's flare is always its burst, and a card with no look gets the plain cast flash and burst. A beam opens extra wide (`beam_flare` for the first `beam_flare_share` of its life), thins, and flickers (`beam_flicker_rate`, `beam_flicker_depth`). The bodies in flight are not part of this yet. An enemy's shot (NO_PLAYER) lands like any spell (staffs F7: one look for every spell), with its own core and cards, in the enemy ramp where a player's colour would show; the ember burst is gone. A new form brings its looks in its own `.tres`; the preset has none.
- **Possess (actors G9, G8):** `possess_started` shows a ring closing on the possessed actor's body (`KIND_RING_IN`, `POSSESS_ZOOM`, `POSSESS_SECONDS`) in the driver's ramp and plays the sound `possess`; `possess_ended` a ring going out from it (when its body is still there).
- **Colours:** Feel keeps none of its own: a ramp's base and light shades are the projectile manager's (`RAMP_COLORS`, `ramp_light`).
- **The poof (living staffs H4):** `staff_shown` (another staff of an actor went on show: the personality swap) gives a puff with the `POOF` look bit along the staff (`POOF_SECONDS` 0.18, zoom `POOF_ZOOM`, its middle `POOF_ALONG` from the hand along the aim), moving with the actor: bone clouds that swell to their biggest at 35% (where `StaffLook` changes the body and face) and dot away, with a few sparks in the actor's ramp. A followed effect sits at an offset from its node (`_effect_offset`: a status rises above it, a poof sits on the staff).
- **Reactions:** `reaction_triggered` gives a puff (`KIND_PUFF`) bigger than a hit's and a ring (`KIND_RING_OUT`), both in exactly the reaction's colour (the `EXACT` look bit: the ramp is made from the colour, not the nearest palette material, so the reactions stay apart), a local stop on the reaction's caster at least as long as a death's, shake, the name rising and the sound `reaction_<id>`; `FeelPreset.reaction_power` scales them by id. The sound and shake come at most every `REACTION_REPEAT` seconds per reaction. A discharge (`discharge_arc`) shows a jagged line of lightning (`KIND_ARC`, re-rolled 30 times a second, burning out over `arc_seconds`) from the struck enemy to each enemy zapped, in the colour of `discharge`.
- **Statuses:** a character with burn, chill, freeze, shock, hex or static shows it as an effect that follows it (`KIND_STATUS`): its element's particles round it (`FeelPreset.status_looks`: `look`, burn fire's embers, chill and freeze frost's flecks, shock storm's gusts, hex void's pull, static lightning's sparks) and a pulsing glow in its `color`; the first of `status_order` shows. `status_applied` starts it; Feel reads `Combat.status_left` (the `combat` var) every `STATUS_POLL` seconds and ends it with the status. At most `STATUS_SLOTS` (16) characters show one. A crowd of other effects can take its slot; the next step shows it again.
- **What it shows:** hit-stop, shake, the effects above, a white flash and squash on the hit node's `Sprite` child (name your sprite `Sprite` to get it), a white-hot ring where an enemy dies and a death animation (a copy of the dead enemy's `Sprite`, drawn by `core/projectiles/shot_sprite.gdshader`'s `dissolve`: white-hot, then pixel by pixel gone, never stretched or faded; the enemy is freed at once), a cast flash at the staff tip, and a small camera kick on a cast (how the caster's body moves is its carrier card's `staff_pose`, drawn by its look: gameplay/actor's `ActorLook`). At most `REACT_SLOTS` (16) sprites react at once; past that a hit skips its flash and squash. If the sprite's owner changes its scale during a squash, Feel takes that as the new size to return to. Sounds are asked from `core/registry` and stay silent when missing.
- Areas never call each other directly; they talk through events. [gameplay/README.md](../README.md) has the rules every sub-area shares.

## Allowed files

`.gd`, `.tres`, `.gdshader` and `.gdshaderinc`, plus `.uid`, `.import` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. The text shared by all sub-areas is in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Steps

The first preset is `presets/punchy.tres`. Follow the steps and test layout in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Example to copy

`gameplay/feel/presets/punchy.tres`, a `FeelPreset` (`presets/feel_preset.gd`): hit-stop length, shake strength and decay, the sizes and seconds of the effects, and the sound ids (the point moments' numbers are their cues', `content/cues/`). Copy it, change the numbers and load it as `Feel.preset` before Feel's `_ready` (the art is read once, then). On `reaction_triggered` feel shows a puff and a ring in exactly the reaction's colour (`reaction_colors` by id; keep the colours far apart), its name rising from the spot, a little shake, and plays the sound `reaction_<id>` (silent until Audio adds it). A preset only tunes reactions; it has no field that touches damage. It also names the art the projectile manager is given (`bolt_texture`), so a re-skin is a preset, never an edit to `feel.gd`. Tests: `tests/gameplay/feel/test_feel.gd`.

## Review questions

A good PR here adds or tunes one juice preset (hit-stop length, shake strength), stays readable with four players hitting at once, and never changes damage or outcomes, with hit-stop kept short and the same for every player. Besides the general review:

- Is the effect readable with 4 players on screen? Does it avoid per-frame allocations and go through the effect batch (the glow look), never a node of its own?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
