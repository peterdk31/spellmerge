# Rewards

## Purpose

Per-player rolls from loot tables, unspent rewards and shops.

Built by connected biomes CB10 ([docs/milestones/connected-biomes.md](../../docs/milestones/connected-biomes.md), decisions 16 to 18 and 26); its shops and fountains by CB11 (decisions 19 and 20).

Reward tables are content data read through the registry (`content/rewards/`, `RewardTable`), so new ones are added without touching this code. Each player rolls their own rewards.

## What it does

- **A roll per player** (DESIGN rule 3): on `room_rewards_due` (Flow's, a cleared room whose kind names a reward table: a fight's `clear`, the boss's `boss`, a reward room's `reward`, ...; however its foes left it, the owner's answer of 2026-11-19) every seat with a wizard gets its own offer (`RewardOffer`), its choices rolled from that table by `RewardRoll` (by weight, never one twice in an offer; the run's chance, purpose `reward`, keyed by the seat and counted by its rolls, so one seat's roll never shifts another's), as many as the player's `reward_choices` run stat (3: "pick 1 of 3", decision 17, raised from 2 by the owner on 2026-11-19).
- **A reward room's type** (the owner's ask of 2026-11-19; `RewardType`, core/base): a reward room's type is decided as its biome is made (gameplay/run, the generator's step 7) and comes with `room_rewards_due` (`type`, a row of item kind, card kind and element). Each seat still rolls its own choices, but `RewardRoll` rolls each choice among the items of the type's narrowest level first (the element's one card, the card kind's cards, the item kind's lines) and widens a level at a time where its tries find nothing the offer has not, down to the whole table: a fire room offers Fire and another infusion, a modifier room two modifiers, a potion room two of its potions, a coin room its coins and then anything (the table has one coin line). Each level rolls under purposes of its own (`RewardRoll.LEVEL_PURPOSES`), so a room of no type (every other kind, a shop) rolls as before. The cards a roll may give are `RewardCards` (core/base: ids, kinds, complexities in id order), the same list the generator reads.
- **Here or not** (rule 4): `here` when the seat's wizard is in the cleared room (`Actor.room`). **A present player's reward lies as an item** (the owner, 2026-11-19: nothing pops up): its offer lies at its seat's spot of `room_rewards_due`'s `spots` (`RewardOffer.lying`, `at`; `reward_item_changed`, Feel's chest in the player's colours); the seat's own driven standing body within `REWARD_REACH` is told of it (`reward_near_changed`, state `_reward_near`), and the HUD opens that offer's pick on its A. A player who was not there finds the offer waiting, lying nowhere, an unspent count on their corner panel, opened with A anywhere. A reward item left unopened stays in its room while the biome lasts, and is lost with it (the owner, 2026-11-19): a new world (and a run's start) takes every item off the floor and its offer goes; an offer waiting lying nowhere still waits. A pick by command is taken wherever the player stands (the bots pick on the bus). Offers wait all run until picked; nobody waits for anyone.
- **A pick** (`reward_pick_requested(player, offer, choice)`, a command): the seat's own offer of that number puts the choice into the seat's wizard's inventory and goes (`reward_taken`). A full bag keeps it unspent (`reward_refused`).
- **A reward reroll** (the owner, 2026-11-19: X in the reward picker; `reward_reroll_requested(player, offer)`, a command): the seat's own offer of that number, with a reward reroll (`RerollBase.REWARD`, content/rerolls) in its wizard's currency bag, spends one and every choice is rolled again from the table and the reward room's type the offer came from (`RewardOffer.table`, `type_row`), as many as it offered, under its own purpose (`reward_reroll`, keyed by the seat, counted by `_rerolls`), so a reroll never shifts the seat's later offers; only that offer changes (`reward_rerolled`). With none held nothing is spent or changed (`reward_reroll_refused`). Every player's wizard starts a run with `start_reward_rerolls` of them (run stats, 1 for now), given with its `start_coins` (once a run, `_funded`).
- **A reward is an item** (the owner, 2026-11-19: "Every reward is something that can be placed in a player's inventory"): a choice is an item kind (a row of `ItemKinds.RULES`), its content's id and a count (`RewardEntry`): a card's copies (into the card bag), potions (`PotionBase`, content/potions: a heal, more most health, kept and drunk from the inventory when the player likes, gameplay/player), coins (onto the coin stack). Nothing here knows what an item does or how it looks: a choice's view is its kind, id and count with the item's own name, icon and description (`ItemLooks`, core/items), all the reward menu's cards show. A card upgrade waits for card levels.
- **Run stats** (`RunStats`, core/base): the run's own changes arrive with `run_stats_ruled` (from `run`); `stat_of(seat, stat)` is those, then the relics in the seat's wizard's relics bag (`RelicBase.stat_changes`). Another area that reads a run stat hears `run_stats_ruled` itself and works the stat out with `RunStats` (areas never call each other); `spend` is for this area's own shop (CB11). Each seat's stats are told to the view by name (`run_stats_told`: at a run's start, as the run's changes come, as the seat's wizard comes and after a restore), so a per-player look (the Y map's `secret_sense`) needs no state of its own.
- **A shop** (CB11, decision 19): on `special_room_opened` of a shop (Flow's, as it is first entered: its shelf table and spots) its shelf is rolled once (`RewardRoll` under the purpose `shop`, keyed by its room: one item a spot, never one twice, each at its line's `price`). The item nearest a standing wizard's own feet within `SHELF_REACH` is in that seat's reach (`shelf_near_changed`, with its price for them), and **A buys it** (the owner, 2026-11-19: `shelf_buy_requested(player, item)`, a command, so the first seat to ask in a tick takes it; nobody waits): the buyer is the standing body the seat drives (its wizard, or a foe it possesses: reach, coins and room are that body's, the owner's of 2026-11-19); with its price (times that player's `price_scale`) in its own coins and room for it in its bag, it goes into its inventory as a reward's choice does, its price comes off its coin stack and it is gone from the shelf for everyone (`shelf_changed`); else `shelf_refused`. Walking over an item buys nothing. The shelf sells potions in place of heals (bought into the bag, drunk later) and keeps what is left all run. **Its reroll sign** (the owner, 2026-11-19; `special_room_opened`'s `sign`): one a shop, a shelf thing numbered with its items (`shelf_changed` and `shelf_near_changed` with the view kind `SHELF_SIGN`, its next price), so A at it is a `shelf_buy_requested` too: with `sign_price(uses)` (3, 5, 8, then each the sum of the two before) times the player's `price_scale` in the driven body's coins, the price comes off, everything on the shelf goes and every spot (a bought one too) is stocked anew from the shop's table with items the shelf did not hold while the table has them (purposes `shop_sign`, then `shop_sign_fill`; keyed by the room, counted by its uses); else `shelf_refused`. `signs()` lists them.
- **A fountain** (CB11, decision 20): on `special_room_opened` of a fountain its pool fills with `POOL_HEALTH` times the highest `fountain_pool` of the players then; every hurt standing wizard whose own feet are in it draws `DRAW_RATE` health a second (through Combat, `health_gift_requested`) until whole or the pool is dry (`fountain_changed`).
- **Coins** (decision 18; the owner, 2026-11-19: "Coins are stacked inventory items"): a player's coins are one stack in its wizard's currency bag (beside its keys) (`CoinBase.ID`, content/coins; `coins_of(seat)`), said by its Inventory as any bag (`bag_changed`: the HUD's purse). A foe carries its coins in its own currency bag from the start (its data's `EnemyBase.coins`, or a roll up to `coins_most`: `Actor._carry_coins`; an add none). As it dies (`enemy_died`, possessed or not), by its `coin_drop_chance` (the run's chance, purpose `coin_drop`, keyed by its actor key and counted, `_drops`), what it still carries of the coins it came with (`Inventory.carried_kept`; the owner, 2026-11-19: "Everything is droppable") lands round where it fell (the owner, 2026-11-19); the first standing wizard (seat order) whose feet come within `COIN_REACH` takes one, and every player's coin stack gains one (shared income kept). A boss's go to every player's stack as it dies instead (decision 25), always, whatever its drop chance (the owner, 2026-11-19); an add's never drop. **Every coin it holds drops** (the owner, 2026-11-19): a coin it took later (a player's dropped one, taken while possessed) is never shared, and drops as it dies, every time and for an add or a boss too, as a plain coin through the Items area (`Inventory.drop_taken`: one copy at a time, `item_drop_requested`), walked over into one body's own bag; so no coin is made or lost. Its keys go with it (as built). **A rejoin keeps its coins** (the owner, 2026-11-19): a seat that leaves and comes again in the run comes back with its whole inventory, coins and all (Flow's `LeftPlayers`, gameplay/flow/README.md), and Rewards gives it no second `start_coins` (`_funded`). A seat's wizard, as it first comes in a run, gains the player's `start_coins`; `spend(seat, amount)` takes from one's own only (CB11's shop). A new world (`room_entered` FIRST_ROOM) and a run's start clear the floor.
- **Pickups said** (`item_picked_up`, for Feel's pickup sounds; the owner, 2026-11-19): a coin walked over, a shelf item bought, a reward taken (`EventsScript.PICKUP_REWARD`).
- **State:** the offers (each with its table and whether and where it lies), each seat's reward item in reach, the reroll signs (room, place, table, uses) and the shelves' spots, the rolls and rerolls counted, the funded seats, the drop rolls counted, the coins on the floor, the shelves, each seat's shelf item in reach, the pools and the run's changes are state (hashed, snapshotted); a player's coins and potions are its wizard's inventory's, state with the body. `rebuild_derived` says every coin, shelf item, pool, waiting list, shelf item in reach and seat's stats again after a restore.

## Contract

- **Public entry script:** `gameplay/rewards/rewards.gd`. Other code uses only this script and the events it sends and listens to; nothing else in the folder is public. The script arrives when the area is built.
- **Dependency direction:** `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`. This area may use `core/` and the registry. `core/` never depends on it, and content never reaches into it.
- **Events** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions; this list matches it and the table in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md)):
  - Sends: `reward_item_changed`, `reward_near_changed`, `item_picked_up`, `reward_rolled`, `reward_taken`, `reward_rerolled`, `reward_reroll_refused`, `rewards_waiting_changed`, `coin_changed`, `shelf_changed`, `shelf_near_changed`, `shelf_refused`, `fountain_changed`, `run_stats_told`, `health_gift_requested` (to combat: a fountain's draw), `reward_refused`.
  - Listens to: `room_rewards_due`, `special_room_opened`, `room_entered`, `enemy_died`, `run_stats_ruled`, `reward_pick_requested` (from the HUD, a command; `reward_taken` is the announced result), `reward_reroll_requested` and `shelf_buy_requested` (from the HUD, commands).
- Areas never call each other directly; they talk through events. [gameplay/README.md](../README.md) has the rules every sub-area shares.

## Allowed files

`.gd` and `.tres`, plus `.uid`, `.import` and `.license` files. Limit: 1 MB per file. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. The text shared by all sub-areas is in [gameplay/README.md](../README.md).

## Example to copy

`rewards.gd` (the area: its state, its events, its step in `Tick.STEPS` "Rewards"), `reward_offer.gd` (a row of state), `reward_roll.gd` (the policy, apart, so it changes alone). A new kind of reward is a new kind of item (a row of `ItemKinds.RULES` and its content, core/items): a table line names it by kind and id, and nothing here changes; a new table is a file in `content/rewards/`. Tests: `tests/gameplay/rewards/test_rewards.gd`.

## Review questions

A good PR here changes how rewards are rolled, kept or bought for each player, reads tables from data, and never makes one player wait for another's choice. Besides the general review:

- Does each player get an independent roll? Are unspent rewards kept and never lost?
- Does it keep to the dependency direction, without calling another area directly?
