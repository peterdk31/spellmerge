# HUD

## Purpose

Everything the players see and click: panels, the spell builder UI, menus, shared widgets and the one theme. It must stay readable with 4 players on one screen and fully usable with a gamepad.

## Contract

- **Sub-folders:** `panels/` (per-player panels), `builder/` (the spell builder), `menus/` (main menu, pause, summary), `widgets/` (shared, reusable controls), `theme/` (the single theme).
- **Events both ways.** The HUD listens to state events from `core/events` and shows only what they say. It also sends player-intent events: requests that carry the player index (0–3), never a finished outcome. It never calls gameplay directly; gameplay checks each request, applies or refuses it, and announces the result as a normal state event (docs/DECISIONS.md, "The HUD sends player-intent events"). Dependency direction: `core` ← `gameplay` ← `content`; the HUD sits beside `gameplay` and uses only `core/`.
- **Events the HUD sends** (`core/events/events.gd` owns the definitions): `play_requested`, `sandbox_requested`, `sandbox_action_requested`, `horde_requested` and `door_vote_requested` (received by `flow`), `staff_change_requested`, `card_move_requested` and `staff_cards_requested` (`player`), `reward_pick_requested` (`rewards`), `horde_pick_requested` (`horde`) and `pause_requested` (`run`) (table in [gameplay/README.md](../gameplay/README.md)). The HUD is the only sender of these events and sends no state events, so each event keeps one sender. Gameplay areas listen to them.
- **Public entry script:** none for the area as a whole; each scene's root script is its own entry (for example a menu scene's script under `menus/`). Other areas never reach into the HUD.
- Widgets build on `core/ui_focus`, so each panel takes input only from its owner (player index 0–3). Nothing assumes a single player, and a player in the builder never makes anyone else wait.
- Use the shared theme; don't style single controls by hand.
- Accessibility: readable text sizes, no information by colour alone, gamepad navigation everywhere.

- **The theme** is `theme/spellmerge_theme.tres`, set as the project theme (`gui/theme/custom`), so every control uses it. Player colours are type variations: `PlayerPanel1-4` (panel border), `PlayerLabel1-4` (text), plus `HealthBar` and `ManaBar`. Add a new look there, not as an override on one control. Colours are from `docs/STYLE.md`.
- **The panels** (`panels/`): `panels.tscn` is the autoload `Hud`, a `CanvasLayer` with one `player_panel/` per player in a corner (P1 top left, P2 top right, P3 bottom left, P4 bottom right; about 112 px wide and 40 px tall plus 13 px per staff row (at most about 95 px), so four fit a 640×360 view; the background is 60% translucent so a player or enemy under a corner panel still shows). It hears `player_joined`, `player_left`, `room_entered`, `player_stats_changed` (from `player`), `staff_changed` and `UIFocus.opened`/`closed`, and shows only that. **A panel is always the player's own staff** (owner, 2026-10-28): its numbers, staff rows and builder stay the player's while the seat drives another actor (possess) and while an enemy's AI drives the player, live, so the player sees their staff take hits; the possession is drawn in the world over the possessed actor (`gameplay/actor`'s `PossessedLook`), not on the panel. A panel shows for a joined player after the first room; it takes no input. It says "HP", "MP" with numbers, and over the health bar "DOWN" at 0 health and "MENU" while that player has a menu open (their builder, or the pause menu they opened; DOWN first), so nothing is shown by colour alone. There is no "P1" or "Staff 2" line (owner, 2026-10-27: the corner and the border colour say whose panel it is).
- **The buttons** (owner, 2026-10-26): the staffs are on the shoulders (RB, RT, LB, LT: `staff_1` to `staff_4`), and the face buttons belong to the menus: `accept` (A) activates, `back` (B) goes back and `alternate` (X) is a menu's second action, all through `core/ui_focus`. `panels.gd` reads two buttons itself, from `core/input` press counts (a quick tap is not lost; a slot with no device is ignored): `back` (B; Tab or E) opens the player's builder in play, when they have no menu open (at this poll and the one before, so the B that closes a menu never reopens the builder), are not down and the pause menu is not open (so also in the horde's build break, while the game is paused); `pause` (Start; Escape) closes the menu the player has open, or opens the pause menu. Y is free for now.
- **The radial builder** (`builder/radial_builder.gd`, owner 2026-10-26; nodes `Root/Radial1-4`, made by `panels.gd` under the corner panels): the default builder (owner, 2026-10-26), beside the list builder. `back` opens it, and closes it again; the pause menu's Sandbox page ("Builder: Radial/List", `builder_options_changed`) switches `back` to the list builder, only in `dev` and `preview` builds, where that page shows. It fills its player's quarter, laid out alike in every quarter (owner, 2026-10-31, docs/milestones/M2-builder-tree.md, T4): a column on the left of the quarter with, top to bottom, the HP and MP bars (two 2 px bars in a 1 px frame of the player's colour, following `player_stats_changed`), the staff tabs between the LB and RB glyphs, the shown staff's slots in five columns (four rows show; more scroll) and the **card info panel** (the card under the slot marker: its name, its kind in the kind's colour with a frame in it, its lore line wrapped, and under a line its `Spells.component_stats`, none for a trigger; "Empty slot" for an empty one; `card_info()`), and the ring on its right with the hint row under it. While it is open, its player's corner panel is hidden (`panels.gd`), and shows again when it closes. **The ring** holds the bag, one place per card id and level (with the count and level drawn when there are several or a level above 1): every card grouped by kind with a coloured arc and a gap between kinds ("Ring: Every card"), or one kind at a time with the kind's letters on a row above and the d-pad's left and right (`kind_prev`, `kind_next`) flipping it ("Ring: One kind"); tiles shrink with the count and below 10 px become plain marks. **Ring styles** (owner, 2026-10-30, experiments for picking among many cards with a pad; `builder/rings/`, one file each, set as the radial's `ring_picker`; deleting one is its file, its test and its entry in `PauseMenu.Ring` and `panels.gd`): "Ring: Zoom" (`ring_zoom.gd`: the stick held still at full tilt locks a spread fan where turning picks at a quarter of the speed, sliding past its edges) and "Ring: Kinds: hold" or "Kinds: depth" (`ring_kinds.gd`: one sector per kind first; resting on it, or pushing the stick further out, unwraps that kind's cards over the ring). They read the push through `PlayerInput.menu_tilt`. The pointed card holds about 4 degrees past its border (hysteresis), in every ring. **The right stick** (`PlayerInput.menu_aim`; P1's mouse) points at a card: it pops out with a double gold frame and the pad rumbles once (`PlayerInput.rumble`; a gap rumbles nothing), and the hub in the ring's middle shows its icon, name, kind and `Spells.component_stats`; with nothing pointed at it shows the staff's numbers. Letting go of the stick (or the mouse leaving the ring) lets the card go, and the hub shows the staff again. **The left stick** (`menu_move`, with key-repeat; P1's move keys or a click on a slot) moves the gold marker (with corner ticks) through the slots; the pointed card shows faded in the marked slot (a gold caret where a push inserts). The casts of the staff's cycle are framed in the slots: the cards one cast reads share a frame (one per row), every other cast in the other Bone shade, so the cards that fire together read as a group while building (owner, 2026-10-27; `slot_groups`). While a card is pointed at, the slots show the staff as RT would leave it: the card faded in at the marker, the rest pushed on, and that staff's frames and "!" marks, so a combination is checked before it is put in (a full staff that would refuse a push keeps its own slots and the caret). **RT** (`staff_2`) puts the card in and moves the marker on: with "Full slot: Push right" (the default) a taken slot and those after it move right, as one `staff_cards_requested`, and a full staff refuses with a note; with "Replace" it is a `staff_change_requested`. **LT** (`staff_4`) takes the marked card out (a push closes the gap); on an empty slot with nothing after it, it takes out the last card before it and the marker steps back ("Undo" in the hint); with push, an empty slot with cards after it closes ("Close gap"). **LT held** (0.6 s; the press has already taken a card out) clears the rest of the staff: a red frame fills round the slots, then every card fades out at once (it reads LT through `PlayerInput.is_held`, which answers while the menu holds the capture). Y no longer clears. **LB, RB** switch staffs (UIFocus `paged`). X and A do nothing (the staff codes are gone; their row over the hints is left free). The cast names (staffs E1) are gone too (owner, 2026-10-31). **The numbers** (the hub, with no card pointed at): "Mana 12%" (the dearest cast of the cycle as a share of the player's maximum mana; red with "!" above 100 %, since it cannot be cast), "Dmg" (the cycle, every shot landing once) and "Time" (the cycle's seconds); `number_lines()` adds "> new" for what RT would make of each. They are no longer drawn under the slots. A refusal's note shows over the top of the card info panel for a moment. **The tree** (owner, 2026-10-31, `builder/tree_view.gd`, part T5 of docs/milestones/M2-builder-tree.md): **Y** (`extra`) swaps the ring and its kind row for a tree of the shown staff's whole cycle in the same space (the quarter's ring side down to the hint row), and back; it opens on the ring. A "Tree" header, then a 19 px row per carrier and per trigger from each cast's `tree` in `Spells.staff_view`: a carrier row has the cast number on a cast's first row, the carrier's own cards as the slots' 16 px tiles, its name in the carrier colour and, on a cast's first row, the cast's mana share and damage on the right; a trigger row is indented 12 px a payload level with a branch line from its parent, and has the trigger's tile, a small arrow, the tiles of the first carrier it fires and the trigger card's name in the trigger colour; a payload's further carriers are rows of their own. The row holding the marked slot is banded and that card's tile has the gold marker frame; moving the marker scrolls its row into view. A taller tree scrolls with the right stick (P1: the mouse wheel), with a 2 px bar on its right edge. The timing replay: a gold frame steps through the rows, one a `TREE_BEAT` (0.4 s), and loops. RT has nothing to put in from the tree; P1's click on a tile marks its slot. The hint row shows the RT, LT, Y ("Tree" or "Ring") and B glyphs with their verbs, dimmed when the button would do nothing. It opens through `core/ui_focus` with itself as its one control (capture, the "MENU" word, `back` and paging), reads the rest from `core/input` for its own player only, and closes like the list builder (`pause`, going down, the run ending).
- **The pause menu** (`menus/pause/`, node `Root/PauseMenu` of `panels.tscn`, in the middle of the view): opened by any player's `pause` in a room; it stops the game for everyone (`get_tree().paused`; the HUD, `PlayerInput` and `UIFocus` run while paused) and only the player who opened it works it (its title says "P2 PAUSED" in that player's colour). Pages: the main one ("Resume", "Sandbox" only while `sandbox_changed(true)`, "Settings"); **Sandbox** (staffs B7: "Imp wave" and "Reset dummies" send `sandbox_action_requested(player, imp_wave | reset_dummies)` and stay on the page; the builder switches "Builder: Radial/List", "Ring: Every card/One kind/Zoom/Kinds: hold/Kinds: depth" and "Full slot: Push right/Replace" flip on `accept`, stay on the page and send `builder_options_changed` to `panels.gd`; "Back"); **Settings** ("Fullscreen: Off/On", the "Effect slots" slider, "Screen shake: On/Off", "Back"; the shake switch sends `screen_shake_changed` to feel and keeps the choice in the save (`SaveFile` setting `screen_shake`, read when the menu is bound; melee I6)). The Gameplay page with "Friendly fire" and "Enemy fire" is gone (actors G3c): every spell hits every player and every enemy, always. `back` goes up a page and resumes from the main page; `pause` again, Resume, the opener's device leaving and the run ending close it, and closing un-pauses, except in the horde's build break (it hears `horde_break_changed`), which keeps the game paused until every player is ready. The focused button is marked with "> ". The main menu shows a focused "Sandbox" button above Play in `dev` and `preview` builds (`main_menu.gd`'s `channel`), which sends `sandbox_requested(0)`. Below Play, in every build, a "Horde" button sends `horde_requested(0)` (docs/milestones/horde.md); it never takes the focus (tests/hud/test_main_menu_horde.gd).
- **The loading screen** (`panels/loading/`, node `Root/Loading` of `panels.tscn`, made by `panels.gd`, over everything; owner, 2026-10-29): the boot screen's colour, "Loading" and a bar, shown from `loading_started` to `loading_finished` and filled from `loading_progressed` (`core/loading`, for any load: today the shaders before the first room). No input.
- **The hurt vignette** (`panels/vignette/`, node `Root/Vignette` of `panels.tscn`, created by `panels.gd`): a red glow at the screen edges, strongest toward the corner of the player whose health fell, fading in half a second. It comes only from a drop in the health of `player_stats_changed`, takes no input and never covers the panels. A hit adds glow by the share of maximum health it took, and less the more players are playing (divided by the square root of their number), so a four-player fight does not keep the screen red; the middle of the screen stays clear. The setting `Hud.get_vignette().enabled = false` turns it off (kept in memory only; unlike `Feel.shake_enabled`, which the Settings page saves in `core/save`, it has no switch yet).
- **The staff rows** (owner, 2026-10-27; `panels/player_panel/staff_row.gd`, nodes `StaffRow1-4` under the mana bar): one row per staff that has cards (0 to 4 rows; an empty staff shows none), to see the cycle and find a mistake in a build. Each row: the staff's button glyph (`widgets/action_glyph.gd`: RB, RT, LB, LT, or the keys), then the cast sequence as 8 px card icons in read order, one framed group per cast of the cycle (from `staff_changed`, the plain `Spells.staff_view`: each cast's `slots` and `wasted`, icons from `Spells.component_icon` once per plan). The cast fired next has a gold frame and full-bright icons (the others are dimmer); a wasted card is greyed and struck through; cards past the row's width are counted as "+N" (gold when the next cast is among them). The staff's delay shows one of two ways (owner, 2026-10-27; the pause menu's Sandbox page switches it for every panel, "Reload: Button" or "Next cast"): by default a shade covers the button glyph after a cast and pulls back up as the delay runs (its edge cyan, or orange for the recharge after the last cast, when the next cast's gold frame is dashed too), so the glyph column says which staffs are ready; or a shade over the next cast pulls back left to right (over the whole cycle during a recharge, with no gold frame) and the glyph greys while the staff waits. Either way the glyph gets a white rim for a few frames when the staff is ready again. The next cast and the delay are the one thing read live: each frame `panels.gd` (`step_staffs`) asks `Spells.cast_index` and `Spells.cooldown_left` of the seat's own body (`Spells.home_of_seat`) for every shown row, plain numbers that allocate nothing, and a row only redraws when they changed (a rise in the delay is a cast; the cursor back at 0 then means a recharge). The rows hide while their player has a menu open (the builder shows the staffs, and the radial builder's column takes the space under the panel), and clear when the player leaves or the run ends (a player announces its staffs when it appears). There is no swap: every staff has its own button.
- **The wave line:** a "Wave N" label (`Root/Strip/WaveLabel`, 12 px, in the middle of the view left of the break line, no input) set from `wave_started` and shown only while a wave is on: hidden at `room_cleared` (so not during the build break) and when a run starts or ends.
- **The build break line** (M2 part 19; `Root/Strip/BreakRow`, right of the wave line in the same strip, no input): a code-drawn hammer (`widgets/hammer_icon.gd`) and "Build N" (seconds left, rounded up). It shows from `build_break_started(seconds)` and counts down from `_process` (`step_break(delta)`); it is display only, so `Flow` alone decides when the wave starts. Limit: the HUD only knows the break from the event, so a player or HUD that joins mid-break shows no break line until the next one. `wave_started` and a run starting or ending hide it.
- **The horde HUD** (`panels/horde/`, node `Root/Horde` of `panels.tscn`, bound by `panels.gd`; docs/milestones/horde.md, H5): shows nothing outside a horde run. **Status** (top middle, between the top corner panels): "03:12  Kills 140  Level 4" and the experience bar with "XP 12/30", from `horde_status`; shown on `horde_started`, hidden on `horde_ended`. **Picks** (`horde_pick.gd`, one box per seat beside its corner panel toward the middle, in the seat's border and title colour): `horde_picks_offered(seat, card_ids)` shows up to three cards (`pick_card.gd`: a stripe in the kind's colour, the 16 px icon, the name and the kind's word, from `Spells.component_name`, `component_icon`, `component_kind`, with the radial builder's kind words and colours); the chosen one has a gold frame and a gold mark. That seat picks with its own device, read from `core/input` press counts like the radial builder: the d-pad's left and right (`kind_prev`, `kind_next`; PgUp, PgDn) move the choice, `accept` (A; Enter) sends `horde_pick_requested(seat, choice)`, and P1 on the keyboard also points with the mouse and clicks (the left button). The box closes on `horde_pick_taken(seat, _)` (also on `player_left`, `horde_over`, `horde_started` and `horde_ended`). **The build break** (owner, 2026-10-31: in Horde only, the game pauses until every player is ready; the director pauses the tree on a level-up and sends `horde_break_changed(true)`). The HUD runs while paused (`panels.gd` is PROCESS_MODE_ALWAYS, and so are `core/input` and `core/ui_focus`), so each seat's box walks it through three steps: **1. Pick** as above. **2. Build, then ready:** with no offer open and the seat not ready, the box says "Build, then ready" with the glyphs of `back` (B; Tab, E) "Build" and `accept` (A; Enter) "Ready". B opens the seat's builder as in play (`panels.gd` reads it, also while paused, unless the pause menu is open); the builder's requests are events the player's controller answers at once, so a change made in the pause is on the wizard's staff. The box hides while the builder is open and shows again when it closes (B or Start). `accept` on the box sends `horde_ready_requested(seat)`. **3. Ready:** on `horde_ready_changed(seat, true)` the box says "Ready" until the break ends. Under the status, the hammer and "Waiting for P2, P3" name every seat the break still waits for (from `horde_ready_changed` and `player_left`), until `horde_break_changed(false)`. **Why this scheme:** A and the d-pad are the box's only when the seat has no menu open, and the builder owns every button while it is open, so they never clash; B is already "open the builder" in play, and the box only points at it. The game is paused, so nothing walks or casts: the d-pad and P1's click take nothing else. The picks open no `core/ui_focus` panel. While the seat has a menu open (its builder, or the pause menu it works) its box hides and reads nothing; while the pause menu is open (anyone's; `panels.gd` hands the box `pause_open`) or the game is paused outside a break, no box reads anything; nor at the first poll after a menu closed or the game resumed, so the `accept` that closed a menu never takes a card or says ready. Presses from before an offer or a prompt take nothing. **The pause menu in a break:** it opens and works as always; it hears `horde_break_changed`, and closing it during a break leaves the game paused; when the break ends under it (the director un-pauses first) it pauses again, and closing it then un-pauses. **The end** (the middle of the view): "The horde won" and "Time 05:12  Kills 488  Level 9" from `horde_over`, until the next `horde_started` or `horde_ended`. Test: `tests/hud/test_horde_hud.gd`.
- **Staff codes are gone** (owner, 2026-10-31, docs/milestones/M2-builder-tree.md, part T1): neither builder copies or pastes a staff; X and A do nothing in the builders. Sharing a build is a screenshot of the tree.
- **The staff builder** (`builder/`, staffs A6a and B8; nodes `Root/Builder1-4` of `panels.tscn`): the corner panel expanded toward the middle, beside its player's corner panel (to the right of P1 and P3, to the left of P2 and P4), so four open builders and four corner panels fit 640×360 without covering one another. The title ("P1 Staff 2"), the four staff tabs by number (`1` to `4`: RB, RT, LB, LT on a pad) and `Close`, then two lists in scrolling windows (`widgets/row_window.gd`, 8 rows at a time, 8 px text): the slots of the shown staff ("3 Fire >5" for a card that goes into the carrier in slot 5, "> you" on the caster, "> aim" at the aim point; "5 Bolt #1" for a carrier and its cast; "4 -" for an empty slot; " !" after a card that does nothing where it is; "^" before a card picked up; " <" after the cards linked to the focused one) and the bag ("Empty", then every card, the one in the selected slot marked " *"). Opened by its owner's `back` (B) in play at any time, waves included, and closed by `Close`, by `back` anywhere but on a bag row (there it goes back to the slot), by `pause`, when its owner goes down or when the run ends. The d-pad moves in two dimensions through a navigator given to `core/ui_focus` (tabs in a row, up from a list to the tabs, left and right between the slots and the bag), so every slot is reached with the d-pad alone; LB and RB (`page_prev`, `page_next`) show the previous or next staff. **Pick and place:** `accept` on a slot with a card picks it up, `accept` on another slot (of any staff) places it, swapping, with `card_move_requested(player, from staff, from slot, to staff, to slot)`; `accept` on the bag while holding, or `alternate` on a slot, sends the card back to the bag (to staff -1); `back` drops it where it was. With nothing held, activating an empty slot selects it and moves focus to the bag, and activating a bag card sends `staff_change_requested(player, staff, slot, id)` and selects the next slot, so a staff is still built left to right by picking cards one after another. **Player 1's mouse:** a click works as `accept`, a drag from a slot to a slot (or to the bag), or from the bag to a slot, moves the card (only builder 1's buttons take the mouse). It never changes what it shows by itself: the picture follows `staff_changed(player, staff, plan)` (the plain `Spells.staff_view`: `cards`, `casts`, `wasted`) and `bag_changed` from `player`. **The info panel** (`Info`, a top-level child, 112×112 px, in the free strip of the player's quarter) shows the cast plan (for each cast of the staff's cycle, from the plan, a line with its generated name (`name`, staffs E1) and under it its words then its damage if every shot lands once, the plan's `damage`) and a note: "Refused: <reason>" after a `build_refused`, and nothing for a wasted card: its "!" mark is enough (owner, 2026-10-30), else where the focused card goes ("Goes into Bolt (slot 2).") and its description (`Spells.component_description`); its last line is a hint for the focused control (`widgets/hint_line.gd`: the glyph of the button, then the verb). **Show buttons, not action names** (owner, #4): use `widgets/action_glyph.gd`, never the words "Cast" or "Dash". Buttons use the `BuilderButton` theme variation; the one in focus takes `BuilderButtonFocus` besides the "> " mark.

## Allowed files

`.gd`, `.tscn`, `.tres`, UI art (`.png`, `.svg`), plus `.uid`, `.import` and `.license` files. Limits: 1 MB per image, 1 MB for anything else. Typed GDScript only; no banned APIs. Tests for the HUD go in `tests/hud/`; the runner and naming are documented in `tests/README.md`, which a later task writes.

## Steps

1. Reuse widgets from `widgets/` before adding new ones.
2. Add UI art lines to `art/manifest.json`.
3. Run the checks below, then open a PR using the template, with a screenshot or short GIF.

## Review questions

Besides the general review:

- Is it readable with 4 players at 1× on a small screen?
- Can it be fully used with a gamepad, and does each panel respond only to its owner?
- Does it reuse the shared theme and widgets?
